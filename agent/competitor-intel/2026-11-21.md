# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-11-21

## Headline

Apex Tuition has pushed 29 new pages this week, and all of them are Year 1 English worksheets. It's a clear signal they're building out a primary-school worksheet library as a content moat. They're starting at the youngest year levels, which suggests more year levels (and likely maths) are coming. Worth watching whether Years 7-10 maths worksheets appear in the next few weeks - that would be a direct overlap with Tuterly's core.

## Per-competitor findings

### Apex Tuition Australia
- 29 new pages, all Year 1 English worksheets, plus a hub page: `/resources/year-1-english-worksheets-free-worksheets-answers`.
- Topics covered: phonics (counting sounds, blends, one letter/different sounds), early reading (reading aloud, reading for meaning, text-to-self connections), narrative structure (beginning/problem/ending, retell a story, story characters), grammar basics (simple sentences, full stops/capitals, naming/doing/describing words), and comprehension (fact vs opinion, high-frequency words).
- Pattern: they're seeding a single-year, single-subject cluster with a hub-and-spoke structure (one resources landing page + ~25 individual worksheet pages with answers). This is a repeatable template they're likely to scale to other years.
- Note for Tuterly: Apex already has a public worksheets section, which competes with `/worksheets`. If they move into Years 7-10 maths worksheets, that's a direct flank on our app's catchment.

- Only one competitor (Apex) is actively publishing. Everyone else is quiet heading into end of school year - consistent with Term 4 wind-down.
- The one active player is pursuing a worksheet-library play, not a blog/SEO play. This is a shift from the generic "study tips" posts that dominated earlier in the year.
- No pricing page changes, no positioning changes, no new landing pages targeting Melbourne, VCE, or scholarship test prep detected across the group.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Years 7-10 maths worksheets gap.** Apex is laying track at Year 1. We have `/worksheets` already - if we extend it with Years 7-10 maths worksheets tied to common wrong-answer topics from the app (surds, linear equations, Pythagoras, probability trees, algebraic fractions), we get a content asset that also doubles as top-of-funnel for the app. The angle Apex can't match: "worksheets generated from the mistakes real Year 7-10 students actually make."

2. **"Common mistakes" content is uncontested.** No competitor this week published anything in the "common mistakes in Year 8 algebra" / "why students get this question wrong" lane. This is a natural blog angle for the Tuterly app since it maps directly to what the product does. Nothing to react to - it's just open ground.

3. **Test prep for Years 7-10.** We have `/scholarship-test-prep` already. No competitor has moved on selective/scholarship maths content this week. Worth considering a Year 7-9 NAPLAN maths or selective maths prep sub-page while the space is quiet.

4. **No competitor app movement.** No new homework/practice tool pages, no app landing pages, no AI-powered study products launched by any monitored competitor this week. Tuterly's "snap a wrong question, get fresh versions" angle remains unchallenged in this set.

5. **Hub-and-spoke worksheet template.** Apex's structure (one hub page + many worksheet pages with answers) is a format worth copying for our Years 7-10 maths worksheets if we don't already use it - it concentrates ranking authority on the hub while giving each topic its own page.

## What to watch next week

- Whether Apex extends worksheets to Year 2+ English or crosses into maths. If maths appears at any year level, escalate.
- Whether EzyMaths and Math Minds come back online - both are direct maths-focused competitors and we're currently blind to them.
- Any Cluey movement - 1,702 URLs stable for the week is unusual for them; they tend to publish regularly.
