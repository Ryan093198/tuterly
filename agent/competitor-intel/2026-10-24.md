## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Publishing has stalled across the board.** Six of the live competitors shipped nothing new. In a normal pre-exam / Term 4 window this is unusual - it suggests the field is coasting rather than competing on content.
2. **No one is talking about ATAR planning as a tool.** None of the active competitors added anything resembling Tuterly's `/atar-planner` or `/sample-report` concepts. These remain genuinely differentiated.
3. **"Alternative to X" pages remain uncontested.** Neither Cluey nor Tutor Doctor are defending their brand terms with comparison content. Tuterly's `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative` pages have clear air.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Competitions and scholarship prep content.** North Shore's archive confirms sustained demand for AMC, SEAMO, ICAS, selective/GATE/scholarship prep. Tuterly has `/scholarship-test-prep` but nothing on academic competitions - a `/amc-prep` or broader competitions hub would be a cheap land-grab, especially aimed at Melbourne parents who currently default to Sydney-centric providers.
- **Holiday intensive programs.** North Shore leans heavily on term-break holiday programs. Tuterly doesn't appear to have a holiday-program landing page. Even a seasonal page positioning tutor-led holiday catch-up or exam prep sprints would fill a gap.
- **State/regional pages.** North Shore's state-tagged content ([VIC], [QLD], [WA], [SA]) suggests parents search regionally. Tuterly is Melbourne-focused but could extend `/best-tutoring-melbourne` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` with suburb- or region-level pages (inner east, bayside, north) to intercept hyperlocal queries.
- **Defensive comparison pages.** With Kip McGrath, Success Tutoring, and Apex all static, now is a low-risk window to add `/kip-mcgrath-alternative`, `/success-tutoring-alternative`, or `/learnmate-alternative` while they're not watching.
- **Content inertia across the market.** If Tuterly ships even a modest cadence on `/learn` (one or two genuinely useful VCE/parent pieces per week), you'll out-publish the entire direct competitive set this quarter.
