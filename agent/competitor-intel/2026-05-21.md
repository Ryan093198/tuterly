## Standing opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's nothing reactive to chase, here are gaps visible from the competitor set that Tuterly's current paths don't obviously cover. These are inferred from what competitors are known to publish broadly, not from this week's (empty) diffs - treat as a backlog, not new news:

- **Parent-facing decision content beyond /parents.** Competitors like Cluey and Learnmate maintain deep parent resource libraries. Tuterly has /parents and /learn but no obvious equivalent of "how to choose a tutor", "signs your child needs a tutor", or year-level guides. Worth auditing /learn's depth here.
- **Subject × year-level landing pages.** Tuterly has /vce-tutoring and subject-agnostic location pages, but not granular pages like "Year 9 Maths tutoring Melbourne" or "VCE Chemistry tutoring". Learnmate's 6.5k URL count is largely built on this pattern.
- **Suburb-level pages.** Tuterly has /best-tutoring-melbourne and /online-tutoring-melbourne but no obvious suburb breakouts. Kip McGrath and Tutor Doctor lean heavily on location pages.
- **Competitor-comparison pages.** Tuterly already has /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative. Natural extensions given this week's competitor set: Learnmate alternative (closest marketplace match - highest priority), Kip McGrath alternative, Begin Bright alternative.
- **NAPLAN and selective/scholarship content.** Tuterly has /scholarship-test-prep but North Shore and Begin Bright build much of their authority on NAPLAN and selective school prep. Worth a content sweep.
