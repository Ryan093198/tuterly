# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-05-21

## Headline observation

This week is a **baseline week** for six competitors (Learnmate, Apex Tuition, Alchemy, Success Tutoring, North Shore, Tutoring for Excellence, Math Minds) - meaning our crawler is seeing their sites for the first time and recording everything as "new". The four established competitors we already track (Cluey, Tutor Doctor, EzyMaths, Kip McGrath) had **zero changes** this week. So there are no genuine weekly moves to react to, but the baseline data exposes a meaningful structural gap: **Math Minds is running a dense suburb-by-suburb local SEO play across Melbourne's eastern suburbs** (Camberwell, Hawthorn, Box Hill, Ivanhoe East, Balwyn North, Glen Iris, Canterbury, Surrey Hills, Mount Waverley, Ringwood, Chadstone, Clayton, Oakleigh, Wheelers Hill, Wantirna South, Vermont South, Nunawading, Deepdene). They are explicitly anchoring pages to specific schools ("2-min drive from Camberwell Grammar", "opposite Box Hill High"). Tuterly has no equivalent suburb pages - this is the most exploitable gap in the data.

### New baselines (treat as a one-time snapshot, not weekly news)

**Math Minds** (66 URLs) - the most strategically interesting baseline. Tightly focused: maths-only, Gr 3-VCE, hybrid online + in-centre. Suburb pages are clearly the backbone of their acquisition. They also publish school-ranking content (VCE rankings 2025, NAPLAN primary school rankings 2024) and a VIC school holidays/term dates page - both are evergreen traffic magnets. Free assessment is the universal CTA.

**Learnmate** (6,505 URLs) - very large blog footprint. Topic spread covers VCE/HSC/IB exam prep, ATAR results breakdowns by state (HSC 2024, SACE 2024, QCE 2024), study technique listicles, subject-specific guides (English Language, IB Chemistry, IB Psychology, Calculus), and a "new online learning platform" announcement suggesting they have rebuilt their booking/lesson product. Worth watching whether that platform launch shifts their positioning from marketplace to integrated tooling.

**Apex Tuition Australia** (1,675 URLs) - Melbourne-focused. Clear page taxonomy: private tutoring Melbourne, online tutoring Melbourne, high school / middle school / primary school tutors Melbourne, IB tutors, group tutoring, plus a public pricing page. The group tutoring service line is a positioning angle Tuterly doesn't currently have a page for.

**Alchemy Tuition** (2,691 URLs) - heavy blog volume aimed squarely at **parents of disengaged or struggling kids**: "7 ways to motivate an unmotivated child", "5 ways to handle 'I hate school' moments", homework strategies, attendance, confidence-building. This is a parent-emotional-pain angle, distinct from the exam-prep angle most competitors take.

**North Shore Coaching College** (1,008 URLs) - news-heavy site driven by per-state, per-term operational updates (VIC/QLD/WA/SA timetables, holiday programs, campus news). Multi-state physical footprint is their main differentiator, including a Hong Kong education seminar - so they target Asian-background families.

**Tutoring for Excellence** (203 URLs) - small, older blog. Mostly evergreen parent content. Nothing standout.

**Success Tutoring** (2 URLs) - either the site is mostly JS-rendered or our crawler couldn't get past the homepage. Worth a manual look before next week.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Suburb landing pages are the dominant local SEO play in Melbourne.** Math Minds runs ~18; Apex runs city-level pages. Tuterly has `/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne`, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` but no suburb tier underneath them.
2. **Annual ATAR/school-ranking content is a shared traffic strategy.** Learnmate covers HSC, SACE, QCE 2024. Math Minds covers VCE 2025 + NAPLAN primary 2024. Tuterly has `/atar-planner` but no equivalent rankings/results explainer content.
3. **State term-date / school-holiday pages** (Math Minds has one for VIC) are easy evergreen pickups nobody else seems to own well.
4. **Parent-emotion content** (motivation, school refusal, homework battles) is Alchemy's lane and largely uncontested by the others.
5. **Group tutoring** as a distinct service line shows up at Apex - Tuterly doesn't address this format.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Build out Melbourne suburb pages** anchored to specific schools, mirroring Math Minds' approach but across our broader subject range (not just maths). High-value targets they already occupy: Camberwell, Hawthorn, Box Hill, Balwyn North, Glen Iris, Ivanhoe.
- **VCE rankings / school results hub** - extend `/atar-planner` with an annual VCE results breakdown. Learnmate and Math Minds both prove this draws traffic.
- **VIC school holidays + term dates page** - small effort, evergreen returns, currently a Math Minds asset.
- **Parent-pain content cluster** under `/parents` or `/learn`: motivation, school refusal, homework conflict. Alchemy owns this and nobody else challenges them.
- **Group tutoring positioning** - either a page explaining we don't do it (and why 1:1 is better), or a service line if Tuterly tutors offer it informally.
- **Watch Learnmate's new platform launch** - if they move from marketplace to integrated lesson tooling, that narrows the differentiation gap with Tuterly's product.
