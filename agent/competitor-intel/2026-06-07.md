# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-06-07

## Headline

A genuinely quiet week. Every competitor we successfully crawled showed zero new or removed URLs against last week's baseline. No new landing pages, no pricing pages, no new subject or location targets, no positioning shifts visible in site structure. This is unusual across a 10+ competitor set and worth noting as a calm window - a good moment to ship the gap-filling pages flagged below before anyone else moves.

## Cross-competitor patterns

The only pattern this week is the absence of one. Zero net change across nine successfully crawled sites suggests either a genuine publishing lull (end of term cycle, mid-year planning) or that competitors are working on staging/unindexed pages we'll see land in the coming weeks. Either way, no one moved on:

- New VCE/HSC season pages despite mid-year being the natural moment
- Pricing updates
- New location pages
- New "alternative to X" comparison pages

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since nothing changed competitively, the opportunity list is the same as last week, which means the window is still open:

1. **Comparison page expansion.** We have /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative. Learnmate is the most structurally similar competitor to us and has no equivalent defensive page from their side - a /learnmate-alternative page is a clean gap. Same logic for Kip McGrath, which dominates branded search but has no marketplace counter-positioning.

2. **Mid-year report / term 2 reflection content.** None of the competitors published anything tied to the mid-year report card moment this week. Our /sample-report and /parents sections could host a "what to do with your child's mid-year report" piece while the field is empty.

3. **Math-specific landing pages.** EzyMaths (439 URLs) and Math Minds (66 URLs) both stake out maths as a category. We have /vce-tutoring and /worksheets but no dedicated maths hub. A /maths-tutoring-melbourne or year-level maths pages would compete directly where these two are static.

4. **Location depth.** Our location pages are Melbourne-centric (/best-tutoring-melbourne, /tutoring-prices-melbourne, /online-tutoring-melbourne). Alchemy and Learnmate's larger URL counts come partly from suburb-level pages. If we want to compete on suburb-level intent in Melbourne, this remains an open area and no one extended their footprint this week.

5. **Scholarship / selective prep.** We have /scholarship-test-prep. North Shore is the main competitor here and we couldn't crawl them this week - worth a manual check next week, but in the meantime our page is one of the few directly targeting this intent among the sites we do see.
