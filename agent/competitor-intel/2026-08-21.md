## Cross-competitor patterns

The only pattern this week is stillness. Late August is mid-Term 3 in Victoria - VCE trial exams and prep season - and none of the direct competitors are shipping fresh content into that window. Cluey and Apex in particular usually have blog cadence and have gone quiet.

Two readings of that:
1. They're publishing but not through sitemap-tracked paths (social, email, paid landing pages we don't see).
2. Content ops are genuinely idle and the SEO surface is static.

Either way, Tuterly has an open field for the next fortnight on VCE trial exam and end-of-year ATAR content.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because there's nothing to react to, here's where our own footprint has gaps worth filling while competitors are quiet:

- **No dedicated Year 12 trial exam page.** We have /vce-tutoring and /atar-planner, but nothing timed to the trial exam window happening right now. A "VCE trial exam recovery" or "what your trial exam result actually means" page would land in a vacuum.
- **No subject-specific landing pages.** Competitors like EzyMaths own the "maths" vertical by name. We have no /vce-methods, /vce-english, /vce-specialist, /vce-chemistry etc. Given /vce-tutoring already exists, subject splits are a natural extension and none of the competitors moved on subject pages this week.
- **No primary/early-years surface.** Kip McGrath and Begin Bright anchor on primary. We have no /primary-tutoring or /naplan pages. Not necessarily a strategic priority but a visible gap.
- **No year-level pages.** Nothing at /year-11-tutoring, /year-10-tutoring etc. These are cheap to produce and competitors haven't refreshed theirs.
- **Comparison pages are underused.** We have /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative. Given Learnmate is the largest competitor by URL count and directly overlaps our marketplace model, a /learnmate-alternative page is the obvious next one. Apex would be the one after that.
- **Scholarship test prep is a strong differentiator.** None of the tracked competitors added scholarship content this week and we already have /scholarship-test-prep. Worth expanding into selective schools and specific scholarship exams (ACER, Edutest) as subpages while we own the space.
