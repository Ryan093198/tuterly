# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-08-01

## Headline observation

North Shore Coaching College is the only competitor to publish anything new this week, and they've done it around the Australian Mathematics Competition (AMC) - spinning up location-specific event pages (Norwood SA, Southport QLD, Upper Mount Gravatt QLD) plus a dedicated AMC event category. This is a clear play to capture parents searching for AMC prep and test venues in the lead-up to competition season. Tuterly has no AMC content, and this is a well-defined, high-intent parent audience worth a response.

## Per-competitor findings

**North Shore Coaching College** - 4 new pages, all AMC-related:
- Three individual event pages for AMC sittings in Norwood (SA), Southport (QLD) and Upper Mount Gravatt (QLD)
- A new `/event-category/amc/` hub, suggesting more AMC event pages will follow across their national footprint

The location-per-page structure implies they're using their physical centres as AMC test venues - a hook Tuterly can't match directly but can counter with prep content.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **The market is quiet.** Of the eight competitors we could actually read, seven published nothing. Mid-year, post-report-cards, pre-Term 4 is a genuinely slow content window - which means anything Tuterly ships now stands out more than usual.

2. **Competition-based prep as a wedge.** North Shore is the only one making a move, and it's around a specific academic competition rather than curriculum tutoring. This mirrors a broader pattern where the "prep for a named event" angle (selective, scholarship, NAPLAN, now AMC) converts better than generic subject pages.

3. **Site-size vs. activity mismatch.** Learnmate (6,419 URLs), Cluey (1,703) and Apex (1,698) are sitting on huge inventories but not adding to them. Their strategy is currently defensive - ranking with what they have, not extending. A well-targeted new page from Tuterly faces less fresh competition than the URL counts suggest.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Build AMC prep content before North Shore locks it in.** They're claiming venue/event intent. Tuterly can claim prep intent with something like `/amc-prep` or `/maths-competition-tutoring-melbourne`: past paper walkthroughs, what to expect at each year level, tutor-matched prep packages. This slots naturally next to the existing `/scholarship-test-prep` page.

2. **Extend the "named test prep" cluster.** Tuterly already has `/scholarship-test-prep`. Add AMC, and consider ICAS, Kangaroo Maths and the Melbourne selective entry test. This is a defensible content territory none of the marketplace competitors (Learnmate, Apex) are attacking.

3. **Worksheets angle on AMC.** `/worksheets` gives Tuterly a distribution advantage North Shore doesn't have - past AMC-style problem sets by year level would be genuinely useful and highly linkable.

4. **Pressure-test the alternative pages.** Cluey and Tutor Doctor are both static this week, so the `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative` pages remain accurate. No urgency, but worth a quick sanity check that pricing and feature comparisons still match their current live pages.

## What to watch next week

- Whether North Shore's AMC event pages expand to Victorian locations (direct Melbourne overlap for Tuterly)
- Whether Cluey or Learnmate break their silence with back-to-school / Term 4 content
- Recovering Begin Bright, Alchemy, Success Tutoring and Math Minds in the crawler - four blind spots is too many
