# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-08-05

## Headline

A genuinely quiet week. Across 11 successfully-crawled competitors, zero new pages and zero removed pages were detected. No competitor shipped visible new content, landing pages, or pricing changes this cycle. Begin Bright's site failed to fetch, so we have no signal there.

Rather than pad this report with noise, I'll use the quiet week to flag structural observations from the current URL footprints and where Tuterly's topic coverage stands relative to them.

## Cross-competitor patterns

The only pattern this week is absence of movement. That is worth naming: mid-August is typically when tutoring sites push Term 3 / pre-exam content (VCE trial exams, HSC trials, NAPLAN follow-up, scholarship test season for 2027 entry). None of the tracked competitors shipped anything visible in that window. Either they are publishing on social/email rather than site, or our next cycle will show a burst.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's no new competitor content to react to, the more useful lens is standing gaps based on their existing footprints versus our top-level paths:

1. **Trial exam / exam-week content.** No competitor moved on this, but we're inside the VCE trial exam window. Our `/vce-tutoring` and `/atar-planner` pages could carry a dated "trial exam recovery" or "how to read your trial results" angle that none of Cluey, Learnmate or Apex currently owns as a landing page.

2. **Scholarship test season.** North Shore is the strongest player here and did not update this week. Our `/scholarship-test-prep` page has a clear opening to publish a Melbourne-specific angle (Haileybury, Scotch, MLC, Trinity test windows) that North Shore's Sydney-leaning content doesn't serve.

3. **Marketplace comparison pages.** We already have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate's 6,400-URL footprint and structural similarity to us, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is the obvious next comparison asset - and there's no sign Learnmate is defending against it.

4. **Pricing transparency.** Our `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` remains a differentiator - none of the tracked competitors added pricing content this week, and most still avoid it entirely. Worth reinforcing rather than treating as done.

5. **Worksheets.** `/worksheets` is a Tuterly-specific asset; only EzyMaths and Math Minds have adjacent territory, and neither shipped anything. Low-risk area to keep expanding.

## Recommended actions

- Fix the Begin Bright fetch and the Success Tutoring crawl before next cycle so we're not flying blind on two competitors.
- Treat this week's silence as a signal to publish, not to relax - we have a clear window before the expected Term 3 content wave from Cluey and Learnmate.
- Prioritise a Learnmate comparison page while they're not defending the term.
