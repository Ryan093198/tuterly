# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-11-26

## Headline

Apex Tuition is scaling its free worksheet library hard - 53 new Year 2 English worksheets dropped this week alone, on top of what's already a 2,200+ page site. They're clearly building a long-tail SEO moat around primary worksheets. Tuterly's `/worksheets` path exists but is not being fed at anywhere near this cadence. If the plan is to compete on organic for parent-driven practice queries, Apex is pulling away.

## Per competitor

### Apex Tuition (53 new, 0 removed)
All 53 new pages are Year 2 English - two hub pages (Year 2 and Year 3 English worksheet indexes with answers) and ~25 individual worksheets covering phonics (blend/segment/swap sounds, vowel teams, silent letters), grammar (compound sentences, noun/verb groups, pronouns), reading comprehension (Koalas, The Night Train), text structure (contents/index/menus, text types, purpose and audience), and story analysis (Goldilocks, comparing stories).

Pattern worth noting: each worksheet ships with answers and is titled for search ("Year 2 English X Worksheet | Free Worksheets & Answers"). This is a template they can roll across every year level and subject - expect Year 3 English, then likely maths, in coming weeks.

### North Shore Coaching College (63 new, 0 removed)
Not content - event calendar pages. The new URLs are state-level term dates, scholarship/selective test dates (SA Ignite Year 6/7, ACER scholarship, SWAT Year 3-5), SEAMO competition sittings (Randwick, Belconnen, Hornsby), and Hong Kong campus term schedules. Titles are all the generic "Calendar View" - this looks like a bulk import of their events system rather than a strategic content push. Still useful signal: they're actively marketing to selective/scholarship test families and SEAMO competition kids across NSW/ACT/SA.

### Success Tutoring (2 new, 2 removed)
Centre URL restructure, not expansion. They moved `/tutoring/wa/midland` → `/centres/wa/midland-2-tutoring` and the same for Sunshine VIC. The "-2" suffix suggests these are second attempts/duplicates. Watch whether `/tutoring/[state]/[suburb]` → `/centres/[state]/[suburb]` is a site-wide migration - if so, it'll keep showing up in diffs for weeks.

### Alchemy Tuition (0 new, 1 removed)
Removed `/apply/`. Could mean they've paused tutor recruitment or moved the form behind a different URL. Minor.

### Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath, Learnmate, Tutoring for Excellence, Math Minds
No changes.

### Errors
EzyMath Tutoring (403 on sitemap) and Begin Bright (fetch failed) - both have been persistently blocked/down. May need to switch to HTML crawl for these two.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Worksheet libraries are the content battleground.** Apex is the only one actively publishing, but they're publishing at a rate (25+ worksheets/week) that will be very hard to match later. Nobody is doing this for Years 7-10 maths yet - that's the gap.
2. **Selective/scholarship test marketing is seasonal.** North Shore's event dump is timed for January holiday programs and Term 1 scholarship sittings. Tuterly's `/scholarship-test-prep` page should be refreshed now, not in February.
3. **No competitor shipped an app update or practice tool this week.** The "snap a question, get fresh versions" angle remains unclaimed in this set.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

**Years 7-10 maths content gap (direct product tie-in):**
- Apex is burning cycles on Year 2 English. None of the tracked competitors published Year 7-10 maths topic guides, common-mistake breakdowns, or test prep this week. Tuterly could own this with a weekly "common mistakes in [topic]" series - each post naturally demos the app's "practise fresh versions" value prop.
- Suggested first posts, all aligned to the app: linear equations common errors, fractions-to-algebra transition traps, Pythagoras word problem mistakes, Year 9 indices, Year 10 quadratics factorising.

**Worksheet strategy decision needed:**
- Either match Apex's cadence on Year 7-10 maths worksheets (defensible because it's the app's zone), or explicitly don't compete and position the app as the modern alternative to static PDFs. Current `/worksheets` sits in the middle.

**Selective/scholarship timing:**
- North Shore is actively listing ACER, Ignite, SWAT and SEAMO dates. A Tuterly post rounding up 2027 selective/scholarship test dates across states, with a maths-prep angle, would pick up traffic they're funnelling to event pages that don't convert well.

**Competitor comparison coverage:**
- Tuterly has `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Both competitors shipped nothing this week, so content there is current. Consider adding a Success Tutoring alternative page given their aggressive centre footprint, and an Apex alternative page if their worksheet library starts ranking against yours.

**App positioning:**
- Still no competitor in this set with a comparable student-facing practice app. Worth leaning into "an app, not a weekly session" messaging on the homepage while that remains true.
