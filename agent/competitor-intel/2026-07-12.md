# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-07-12

## Headline

Cluey's sitemap suddenly exposed ~1,290 "new" URLs this week, but most are old staging pages, deleted-flagged drafts, and legacy campaign pages (NAPLAN 2023, 2022 maths quiz, etc.). This looks like a site restructure or sitemap regeneration rather than a burst of fresh content. The genuinely interesting live pages are a small handful: a grandparents offer, a competition, a Year 7 parent guide, and a schools directory. Everyone else was quiet.

## Cluey Learning

Ignoring the 404s and obvious staging URLs, the pages worth noting:

- **/grandkids** — "20% Off Tutoring For Grandchildren." A dedicated landing page targeting grandparents as buyers, with a discount hook. Novel audience segmentation we haven't seen before.
- **/win-tutoring-every-week** — a competition offering a year of free tutoring. Lead-gen play.
- **/schools** — an Australian schools directory ("find your school or search by location"). Directly overlaps with Tuterly's /directory.
- **/year-7** — a Year 7 high-school transition guide aimed at parents. Life-stage content, not subject content.
- **/free-maths-worksheets** and **/maths-quiz-for-parents** — lead magnets (worksheet downloads, parent quiz). Overlaps with Tuterly's /worksheets.
- **/school-holiday-activities-brisbane / -gold-coast** — city-specific holiday activity pages (titles are mis-tagged as Sydney/Canberra, suggesting rushed rollout).
- **/naplan-2024** live; **/naplan-2023** still indexed.
- **/request-call** — a simple callback capture page, separate from their main contact flow.

The **grandparents angle** and the **Year 7 transition guide** are the two most strategically interesting live moves. Everything else is either evergreen resurfacing or campaign scaffolding.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Very quiet week overall.** Nine of eleven competitors made zero content changes. Only Cluey moved meaningfully, and half of that is sitemap noise.
- **Lead magnets and audience segmentation** are Cluey's playbook: worksheets, parent quizzes, competitions, grandparent-targeted offers, life-stage guides. Nobody else is doing this.
- **Schools directories** are becoming a shared feature — Cluey now has one, Tuterly has /directory. Worth checking how theirs compares (breadth, per-school data, whether it drives leads).

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Grandparents as buyers.** Cluey just built a page for this. We have nothing. Low-effort landing page or a section within /parents could capture this segment, especially given older grandparents fund tutoring but rarely see themselves in tutoring marketing.
2. **Year-level transition guides.** Cluey's /year-7 guide is a parent-anxiety hook (starting high school). Tuterly's /learn and /parents could host equivalents for Year 7, Year 11 (VCE onset), and Year 12. Ties naturally into /vce-tutoring and /atar-planner.
3. **VCE Accounting and VCE Economics.** Learnmate just removed both tutor pages. If we have or can recruit tutors in these subjects, there's a short-term gap in the Melbourne VCE tutor search landscape. Worth a targeted push under /vce-tutoring.
4. **Parent-facing quizzes and interactive lead magnets.** Cluey's maths quiz for parents is a clever engagement hook. Tuterly's /worksheets is static by comparison — an interactive ATAR/subject readiness quiz tied to /atar-planner could outperform it.
5. **City-specific school holiday content.** Cluey is rolling out /school-holiday-activities-[city] pages (sloppily). A cleaner Melbourne-specific version tied to /best-tutoring-melbourne and holiday intensives would be easy to leapfrog on.
