## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Total content freeze.** Nine of the ten reachable competitors made zero changes this week. This is unusual enough to flag - it may reflect the mid-year school holiday lull where marketing teams pause publishing. Expect a burst of new content in mid-July as Term 3 approaches, particularly around VCE/HSC trial exam prep.

2. **Sitemap blind spots are growing.** Alchemy, Math Minds, Begin Bright, and effectively Success Tutoring are all giving us no usable data. We should consider a fallback - either a manual monthly crawl of their key pages, or a homepage-screenshot diff - so we're not flying blind on four competitors indefinitely.

3. **Nobody is publishing what we'd expect right now.** Mid-year is the natural moment to publish Term 3 study guides, July holiday programs, GAT prep (just past), and Year 12 mid-year report card analysis for parents. None of the tracked competitors did. That is an opportunity.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **GAT debrief and Unit 3/4 mid-year content.** The General Achievement Test ran in mid-June. No competitor we track has published a debrief or "what your GAT result means" piece for VCE parents. This slots naturally under /vce-tutoring or /learn.

- **July holiday intensives.** None of the Melbourne-focused competitors (Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Learnmate, Apex, EzyMaths) have new holiday program pages live. Worth checking whether we have a /learn or campaign page for July intensives and, if not, building one before the first week of July.

- **Mid-year report interpretation for parents.** Schools issue semester one reports late June/early July. A /parents resource on how to read a Victorian school report and when to act on it is the kind of evergreen page no competitor is touching - and it funnels into tutor matching.

- **Address the Learnmate volume gap.** They have 6,430 indexed pages, mostly subject-by-suburb. Our /directory and /online-tutoring-melbourne are the closest equivalents. Even modest expansion of programmatic subject + suburb pages would close a structural disadvantage that doesn't show up in week-to-week diffs but compounds.

- **"Cluey alternative" and "Tutor Doctor alternative" pages still uncontested.** Cluey and Tutor Doctor published nothing this week and have made no positioning changes for weeks. Our comparison pages remain unchallenged - good time to refresh them with current pricing if we have it.

## Recommended action

Use this quiet week to ship the GAT debrief and July holiday intensive pages. Both are time-sensitive, neither is contested, and both map to existing top-level paths (/vce-tutoring, /learn).
