# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-07-08

## Headline

Learnmate is aggressively expanding beyond Victoria/VCE into a genuinely national marketplace this week — publishing at least 11 QCE (Queensland) and 7 SACE (South Australia) subject landing pages in a single push, plus IB, UCAT, and city-specific year-level pages. If they hold this pace, they'll own the interstate SEO real estate for senior curricula that Tuterly's VCE-centric pages don't touch at all. Every other tracked competitor was flat this week.

## Learnmate

30 new pages, 0 removed. The new pages fall into four clear clusters:

**QCE (Queensland) subject pages** — new tutor landers for Accounting, Biology, Chemistry, English, General Maths, Legal Studies, Maths Methods, Physics, and Psychology. Positioning leans on QCAA specialists, "Commendation recipients," UQ PhDs, and Griffith Medicine students. This is a full QCE senior-subject rollout, not a one-off.

**SACE (South Australia) subject pages** — Accounting, Chemistry, General Maths, Maths Methods, Physics, Psychology, and Specialist Maths. Same template, name-checking "SACE merit graduates" and Adelaide Med students. Stage 1, Stage 2, and External Exam all called out.

**National / high-value credentials** — new pages for IB Tutors Australia (leaning on "IB 45 graduates" and qualified DP teachers, covering IAs and Extended Essay) and UCAT Tutors Australia ("98th–99th percentile," all four subtests, online nationwide).

**Year-level and city cuts** — Year 11 (national), Year 12 Sydney, Year 7 Maths, Year 8 English, Year 9 Perth. They're layering year × subject × city as separate URLs.

**Note:** Two TASC (Tasmania) pages — `tasc-general-maths-tutor` and `tasc-modern-history-tutor` — appeared in their URL set but return 404. Either broken links from a nav rollout or planned pages not yet published. Worth re-checking next week to see if TASC is the next state they target.

- **Success Tutoring** — still only 2 URLs indexed; effectively no public site to track.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Learnmate is the only mover, and they're moving fast in one direction: geographic + curriculum expansion.** The rest of the field is static. This is a Learnmate story, not an industry story.
2. **The template Learnmate is scaling** is state-curriculum × subject, with credential-heavy meta descriptions (percentiles, specific universities, teaching qualifications). It's a cheap-to-produce SEO play but the credential specificity makes each page feel bespoke.
3. **Interstate senior curricula (QCE, SACE, IB) and UCAT** are the current whitespace competitors are racing into — none of the traditional franchises (Kip, Tutor Doctor, Cluey) are contesting this.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **QCE / SACE / IB gap.** Tuterly's site is VCE and Melbourne-anchored (`/vce-tutoring`, `/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne`). If interstate is on the roadmap at all, Learnmate is now planting the flag on the exact subject landers you'd want to rank for. Even a lightweight IB page would be worth considering — the DP has genuine demand and no franchise is targeting it.
- **UCAT.** Learnmate now has a dedicated UCAT lander with credential-led positioning. Tuterly's `/scholarship-test-prep` is adjacent but doesn't cover UCAT. Given the price point families pay for UCAT prep, a `/ucat-tutoring` or similar page is a defensible addition.
- **Year × city pages.** Learnmate is producing Year 12 Sydney, Year 9 Perth, etc. Tuterly's Melbourne pages are strong, but there's no year-level cut (e.g. `/year-12-tutoring-melbourne`, `/year-7-maths-melbourne`). This is a low-effort way to hold Melbourne turf more tightly before Learnmate templates their way into it.
- **Angle Tuterly can beat them on:** Learnmate's new pages lean hard on individual tutor credentials ("99th percentile," "IB 45"). Tuterly's marketplace + ATAR planner + sample report combo is a more concrete parent-facing proof than a percentile claim. A landing page that pairs credentials with the ATAR planner and a sample progress report would out-substance Learnmate's template on the same keywords.
- **Watch item for next week:** whether the TASC 404s go live, whether the QCE/SACE push extends to English variants and humanities, and whether Cluey or Tutor Doctor react.
