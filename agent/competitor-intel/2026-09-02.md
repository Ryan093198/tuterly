Both fit Learnmate's established two-track content strategy: parent-facing decision content, plus student-facing VCE cram content. No pricing, positioning, or product changes visible.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **NAPLAN proficiency band content.** We have `/parents` and `/learn` but nothing addressing the current NAPLAN band system parents are actually seeing on reports. Learnmate got there first this week but the topic is wide open - a stronger version could sit under `/learn` or `/parents` and tie into `/sample-report`. Angle we could beat Learnmate on: what a good tutor-led response plan actually looks like week by week, not just "consider a tutor."
2. **VCE subject topic pages.** Learnmate continues to accumulate deep per-topic VCE guides (Business Management CSR this week). Our `/vce-tutoring` page is a single hub. If VCE is a real growth channel for us, we're being outbuilt on topic depth. Worth deciding: do we compete on this, or stay hub-only and rely on `/atar-planner` and `/worksheets` for student pull?
3. **Comparison pages remain our unique surface.** No competitor has responded to `/cluey-alternative` or `/tutor-doctor-alternative` with anything similar. That's still a defensible edge - worth considering a third comparison page (Kip McGrath is the obvious next target given their brand recognition and static content strategy).
4. **Competitor silence = share-of-voice opportunity.** With Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath and Apex all publishing nothing, any parent-facing piece we ship in the next fortnight has an unusually clear field.
