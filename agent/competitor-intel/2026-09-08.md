# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-09-08

## Headline

A genuinely quiet week. Across the nine competitors we successfully crawled, there was exactly one content change: Cluey Learning removed a single blog post. No new pages, no pricing shifts, no new landing pages from anyone. The most useful thing to do with this week is treat it as a chance to look at structural gaps rather than reactive moves.

## Per-competitor findings

**Cluey Learning** - Removed `/blog/managing-sessions/`. This looks like a support/help-style post about how to manage tutoring sessions inside their platform. Its removal is minor but worth noting: either they've folded that content into a logged-in help centre, or they've decided public-facing operational content wasn't pulling weight. No other movement across 1,702 URLs.

**Tutor Doctor Australia** - No changes across 401 URLs.

**EzyMaths Tutoring** - No changes across 495 URLs.

**Kip McGrath Education** - No changes across 324 URLs.

**Learnmate** - No changes across 6,428 URLs. Worth flagging that their footprint is roughly 4x anyone else's - almost certainly driven by tutor profile pages and location/subject combinations. Nothing new this week, but the sheer scale is the standing story with Learnmate.

**Apex Tuition Australia** - No changes across 1,702 URLs.

**Tutoring for Excellence** - No changes across 203 URLs.

**Success Tutoring** - Only 2 URLs indexed via sitemap. This is almost certainly a sitemap configuration issue on their end rather than a real inventory of two pages. Flagging so we don't over-interpret future "new page" counts from them.

**Begin Bright** - Fetch failed this week. Skip.

**Alchemy Tuition** - Sitemap returned zero URLs. Likely a sitemap config issue on their side; we're effectively blind to them until it's fixed.

**North Shore Coaching College** - Blocking us with a 403 on their sitemap. Same story - we can't see changes here and should consider an alternative crawl approach if we want visibility.

**Math Minds** - Sitemap returned zero URLs. Same blind spot as Alchemy.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Publishing has stalled across the board.** Zero new pages from any competitor in a week is unusual and probably reflects the September school-holiday lull more than a strategic shift. Worth watching whether October brings a surge tied to Term 4 / VCE exam season.

2. **Four of twelve competitors are effectively invisible to us** (Begin Bright, Alchemy, North Shore, Math Minds, plus Success Tutoring's broken sitemap). That's a monitoring gap worth fixing before we miss something real.

3. **Content pruning, not content adding.** The only movement was a removal (Cluey). If this becomes a trend - competitors trimming rather than expanding - it suggests the market is consolidating around fewer, stronger pages rather than chasing long-tail SEO.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since nothing new was published, the useful exercise is looking at what competitors have that we don't:

- **Scholarship and selective-school content beyond test prep.** We have `/scholarship-test-prep`. Competitors like North Shore and Cluey lean heavily on selective-entry and scholarship-exam content across many pages. Worth auditing whether our single page competes with their depth.

- **Primary-years positioning.** Kip McGrath and Begin Bright are heavily primary-focused. Tuterly's public paths skew secondary and VCE. If we want the parent-of-a-primary-kid segment, there's no clear entry page for them beyond `/parents`.

- **Subject-specific landing pages.** We have `/vce-tutoring` but no visible per-subject pages (English, Methods, Chemistry, etc.). EzyMaths owns "maths tutoring" as a category with 495 pages; Learnmate has thousands of subject/location combinations. A modest set of subject pages under `/vce-tutoring/` would be a low-cost gap-fill.

- **The removed Cluey "managing sessions" post** hints that operational/how-the-platform-works content may not convert. Something to keep in mind before we invest in similar explainers.

- **Competitor-comparison pages.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate's scale and it being our closest marketplace analogue, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is a clear next candidate.

## Monitoring fixes to action

- Investigate alternative sources for North Shore (403), Begin Bright (fetch failure), Alchemy and Math Minds (empty sitemaps). Right now roughly a third of our watchlist is dark.
