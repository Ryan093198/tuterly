# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-06-06

## Headline observation

No competitor published or removed a single tracked URL this week. Across the eight competitors we successfully crawled, the combined delta is zero new pages and zero removals. This is the quietest week we've seen and worth noting on its own - it suggests most of the field is in a holding pattern heading into the mid-year school break, which is a window where Tuterly could push new content or campaigns with very little competing noise.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Total publishing freeze.** Eight crawlable competitors, zero new pages between them. Either everyone is genuinely quiet, or several sitemaps aren't updating reliably. Worth a sanity check on at least Learnmate and Apex next week - both have large catalogues and it would be unusual for neither to add anything for two consecutive weeks.

2. **Persistent crawl issues at three competitors** - Begin Bright (fetch failed), Alchemy and Math Minds (empty sitemaps), and Success Tutoring (only 2 URLs visible). We've effectively lost visibility into four of twelve competitors. If we want a true picture of the smaller-end and boutique players, we may need an alternative monitoring method (homepage diff, or manually checking key pages) rather than relying on sitemap pulls alone.

3. **Footprint hierarchy unchanged**: Learnmate (6.3k) >> Cluey (1.7k) ≈ Apex (1.7k) > North Shore (1k) > EzyMaths (440) > Tutor Doctor (390) > Kip McGrath (325) > Tutoring for Excellence (200). Learnmate's scale advantage is entirely programmatic (tutor + subject + suburb pages). Apex's 1.7k without being a marketplace is the more interesting number - they're investing in content depth.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because there's no new competitor activity to react to, the lens this week is on standing gaps rather than fresh moves:

- **Quiet-week content push.** With no one shipping, anything Tuterly publishes this week has an unusually clear field. A good week to launch the next worksheet batch, an ATAR-planner update, or a new alternative page (e.g. a Learnmate or Apex comparison, which we don't yet have despite having Cluey and Tutor Doctor alternative pages).
- **Marketplace comparison gap.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative` but no equivalent positioning page against Learnmate, which is the most directly comparable model to Tuterly (marketplace, tutor profiles, subject-by-suburb pages). Given Learnmate's sitemap scale, ranking against them on branded comparison terms is likely tractable.
- **Mid-year break angle.** None of the competitors appear to have shipped holiday-period content this week. A parent-facing piece on using the mid-year break productively, or a tutor-facing piece on holiday demand, would land in an empty room.

## Suggested follow-ups

- Re-check Begin Bright, Alchemy, Math Minds and Success Tutoring next run; if still broken, replace sitemap monitoring with a lightweight homepage/key-page check.
- If Learnmate and Apex both show zero deltas again next week, raise confidence that we're seeing real quiet rather than a tooling issue.
