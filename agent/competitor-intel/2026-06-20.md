## Cross-competitor patterns

With four sites unreadable and the rest static, there's no real movement pattern to call out. The one durable pattern visible in the totals: Learnmate and Apex are operating at a directory scale (1,700-6,400 URLs) that the more curated players (Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath, EzyMaths, Tutoring for Excellence) are not trying to match. We sit closer to the curated camp in URL count but compete with both.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since nothing changed competitively this week, the useful exercise is gap-spotting against what we already know is on their sites versus our top-level paths:

- **Subject-specific landing pages.** Our top-level paths skew toward Melbourne, VCE, ATAR, pricing and comparison. We don't have visible top-level pages for individual subjects (maths, English, chemistry, physics). EzyMaths and Math Minds own the maths-specific angle by name alone. A `/maths-tutoring-melbourne` or subject hub under `/tutoring` would be a defensible add.
- **Primary vs secondary split.** Kip McGrath and Begin Bright are primary-skewed; Cluey and Learnmate span both. We have nothing top-level signalling primary-school tutoring. If we want parent traffic for younger years, a `/primary-tutoring` page is missing.
- **Scholarship and selective.** We have `/scholarship-test-prep`. North Shore and Begin Bright are strong here. Worth checking next week (once their sitemaps resolve) whether they've expanded selective-school content - this is a corner we could push harder into with a state-by-state or school-by-school breakdown.
- **The "alternative" play.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate's sheer scale and overlapping marketplace model, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is the obvious next one. Their content didn't change this week, so we'd be writing against a stable target.
- **Worksheets as a moat.** None of the competitors with readable sitemaps show a worksheets section in their URL profile at the scale we're building. `/worksheets` remains a differentiator worth investing in while they're not contesting it.
