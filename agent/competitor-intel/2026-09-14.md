# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-09-14

## Headline

A very quiet week. The only meaningful movement is **Apex Tuition extending its free maths worksheets library into Years 8 and 9**. That library is the single asset most directly overlapping Tuterly's `/worksheets` page, and Apex is now building it out systematically by year level. Worth watching closely - if they cover Years 7-10 in full, they'll become the default free-worksheets destination for parents Googling by year.

## Cross-competitor patterns

With only one real publisher active, there's not much of a pattern to report. But two things stand out over the last few weeks of quiet:

1. **Worksheets are becoming the contested surface.** Apex is the second competitor this quarter to invest in year-level worksheet libraries. Free downloadable resources indexed by year level are becoming table stakes for the top-of-funnel parent search.
2. **Nobody is publishing meaningfully on VCE, ATAR planning, or scholarship prep** - three of Tuterly's differentiated topic areas (`/atar-planner`, `/vce-tutoring`, `/scholarship-test-prep`). The gap remains wide open.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Defend and extend `/worksheets`.** Apex is publishing year-level maths worksheets with answers. Check whether Tuterly's worksheets are: (a) indexed by year level as landing pages, (b) clearly advertising that answers are included, and (c) covering Years 7-10 maths at minimum. If Apex fills out the full year range unopposed, they'll own that search intent.
- **Consider English worksheets as a flanking move.** All the worksheet activity from competitors is maths-focused. A year-level English worksheets library would be unopposed territory.
- **The ACT curriculum gap.** Learnmate pulling their BSSC search page hints at low demand or low ROI in that market. Probably not worth Tuterly chasing ACT-specific pages given the Melbourne focus, but confirms Victoria-first positioning is right.
- **Refresh comparison pages.** Cluey and Tutor Doctor haven't changed a single page this cycle. Tuterly's `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative` pages can be updated with confidence that competitor claims are stable - a good week to audit those for accuracy and freshness.
