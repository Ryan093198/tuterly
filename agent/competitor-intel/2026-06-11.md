# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-06-11

## Headline

A quiet week across the board. Every competitor we successfully crawled shows zero new or removed URLs compared to the prior baseline. No pricing pages moved, no new subject or location pages launched, no positioning shifts visible. This is unusual enough across 11 sites that it's worth a sanity check on the crawl pipeline before treating it as a genuine signal of an industry-wide pause.

## Per-competitor findings

**Cluey Learning** - 1,703 URLs, no changes. Their footprint remains the largest single-brand operation we track. Nothing new to react to on the /cluey-alternative angle this week.

**Tutor Doctor Australia** - 389 URLs, no changes. No movement to feed into /tutor-doctor-alternative.

**EzyMaths Tutoring** - 439 URLs, no changes.

**Kip McGrath Education** - 325 URLs, no changes.

**Begin Bright** - Fetch failed this week. No data. Worth retrying next cycle; if it fails again we may have a crawler issue specific to their site or they've changed hosting/bot rules.

**Learnmate** - 6,332 URLs, no changes. Still by far the largest URL count of any competitor - mostly driven by their tutor profile directory. No new profiles indexed this week is itself slightly suspicious for a marketplace of their size.

**Apex Tuition Australia** - 1,676 URLs, no changes.

**Alchemy Tuition** - 2,691 URLs, no changes.

**Success Tutoring** - Only 2 URLs detected. Almost certainly a crawl-scope or robots.txt issue rather than a real footprint - flag for the engineering side to investigate. We've effectively had no usable visibility into Success Tutoring for some time.

**North Shore Coaching College** - 1,018 URLs, no changes.

**Tutoring for Excellence** - 203 URLs, no changes.

**Math Minds** - 66 URLs, no changes.

## Cross-competitor patterns

The dominant pattern is the absence of one. Across nine sites with healthy URL counts, none added or removed a single page in the week. Possible explanations, in order of likelihood:

1. The diffing job is comparing against an identical snapshot (baseline not advancing, or two runs of the same crawl). Worth verifying with engineering before next week's report.
2. Genuine seasonal lull - mid-June sits in the gap between Term 2 marketing pushes and the EOFY/Term 3 enrolment cycle. Some quiet is plausible, but total stasis across 9 independent operators is not.
3. A change in how new URLs are being detected (e.g., sitemap parsing vs. crawl).

I'd treat this week as low-confidence until we confirm the pipeline is healthy.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because there's no new competitor activity to react to, this is a good week to look at standing gaps rather than fresh ones. From the URL totals alone:

- **Learnmate's 6,332 pages vs. Tuterly's directory** - Learnmate's scale advantage is almost entirely tutor profile pages. Our /directory and /tutors paths are the equivalent surface. Worth auditing whether our profile pages are indexable and as discoverable per-subject and per-suburb as theirs.
- **Alchemy's 2,691 and Apex's 1,676** - both well above us in raw indexed surface. These are typically built from suburb × subject × year-level combinations. Tuterly has strong Melbourne-specific landing pages (/best-tutoring-melbourne, /online-tutoring-melbourne, /tutoring-prices-melbourne) but limited per-suburb depth visible in the top-level paths. A programmatic suburb layer under /tutoring or /directory remains an underexploited angle.
- **Comparison/alternative pages** - we have /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative but no equivalent for Learnmate, which is arguably our closest marketplace competitor by model. A /learnmate-alternative page is the obvious gap.
- **Scholarship and selective prep** - we have /scholarship-test-prep. North Shore Coaching College is the established player in that segment; with 1,018 pages they likely have deeper per-school content (Melbourne High, MacRob, selective entry). Worth a manual review of their scholarship pages even though nothing changed this week.

## Action items

1. Engineering: confirm the baseline snapshot is advancing week-over-week and that zero diffs across nine sites isn't a pipeline artefact.
2. Engineering: investigate why Success Tutoring returns only 2 URLs and why Begin Bright is failing to fetch.
3. Content: scope a /learnmate-alternative page while Learnmate has published nothing new to respond to.
4. Content: audit per-suburb depth under /directory against Alchemy and Apex.
