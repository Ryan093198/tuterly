# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-06-13

## Headline

A genuinely quiet week. The only substantive content movement across the tracked set is Learnmate quietly removing its online VCE Accounting and VCE Economics tutor landing pages. Given Learnmate's scale (6,300+ URLs) and their usual pattern of expanding subject coverage rather than retreating, this is worth a closer look - either those subjects are underperforming for them, or they're consolidating subject pages under a different structure. Either way, it opens a small window for Tuterly to own VCE Accounting and Economics search intent if we want it.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Pre-holiday slowdown.** Mid-June is end-of-Term-2 in Victoria. Across nine competitors that returned data cleanly, only Learnmate made a real content change. Expect activity to pick up in early July as competitors push Term 3 and EOY exam prep content.

2. **No pricing or positioning moves detected.** Nothing on home, pricing, or hero pages shifted across the set.

3. **Three tracking gaps to fix:** Begin Bright (fetch fail), Alchemy Tuition (empty sitemap), Math Minds (empty sitemap), and arguably Success Tutoring (only 2 URLs visible). Four out of twelve competitors aren't being monitored properly. Worth a sprint to harden the crawler.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **VCE Accounting and VCE Economics.** Learnmate's removal of these two pages is the most actionable signal this week. Our `/vce-tutoring` hub doesn't appear to have dedicated subject-level landing pages for individual VCE subjects (based on the path list provided). Spinning up `/vce-accounting-tutoring` and `/vce-economics-tutoring` pages now, while Learnmate is absent from those SERPs, is cheap and timely. Accounting and Economics are smaller cohorts than Methods/English but have engaged, ATAR-focused parents willing to pay.

- **Subject-by-subject VCE pages more broadly.** The Learnmate removal is a reminder that we likely don't have per-subject VCE pages at all. Competitors like Cluey and Learnmate do. This is a structural gap worth a roadmap item, not just a one-off response.

- **Author/blog infrastructure.** North Shore is building out named-author content (Adrian, Yammy). If we're not already attributing our `/learn` content to named tutors or education leads, doing so adds trust and is a small, fast win.

## What to watch next week

- Whether Learnmate replaces the removed Accounting/Economics pages with something new, or whether they redirect.
- Begin Bright once the fetch is working again - they were our biggest blind spot this week.
- Early Term 3 content pushes from Cluey and Tutor Doctor; both have been static for a while and are due to publish.
