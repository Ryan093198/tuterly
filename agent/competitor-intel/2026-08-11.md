## Cross-competitor patterns

The only pattern this week is stasis. Mid-August is typically a quiet content period between Term 3 start and the pre-exam push, so this is consistent with seasonal behaviour. Expect movement to pick up from late August into September as competitors publish VCE/HSC exam-prep content and trial-exam material.

Structural observation (not new, but worth restating while the diff is empty): the marketplaces (Learnmate at 6.4k URLs, Alchemy at 2.7k, Apex at 1.7k, Cluey at 1.7k) dwarf the franchise/centre operators (Kip McGrath 323, Tutor Doctor 398, Tutoring for Excellence 203). Tuterly is competing on the marketplace side of that split, and the URL-count gap with Learnmate is the single biggest structural difference - almost entirely tutor profile and location pages.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's nothing new to react to, this is a good week to look at standing gaps rather than weekly moves:

- **No scholarship-test content from the majority of competitors** on our radar, yet Tuterly has `/scholarship-test-prep`. Worth doubling down on this while it's underserved - selective-entry and scholarship prep is a high-intent, high-willingness-to-pay segment.
- **ATAR planning as a tool** (our `/atar-planner`) remains a distinctive asset. None of the competitors we track have shipped equivalents. Consider whether it deserves more prominent placement or a companion "VCE study planner" variant.
- **Comparison pages** - we have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate's size and direct marketplace overlap with us, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is the obvious next one. Apex would be second.
- **Worksheets as a moat** - `/worksheets` is a content asset none of the tracked competitors match. Worth checking whether it's getting the internal linking and topical depth to actually rank.
