## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Content velocity has collapsed this week.** Ten crawlable competitors, one publishing anything new. Late August is exam-prep season, so this is either genuine quiet or teams focused on paid/social rather than site content.
- **Learnmate is the only competitor with a visible per-VCE-subject landing page strategy.** They now clearly cover the commerce subjects (Economics, Accounting) in addition to their existing subject roster. No one else is competing at that granularity.
- **The "online + [city/subject]" combination remains Learnmate's positioning wedge.** Every new page they've added recently leads with "Online" in the title and meta, versus the more centre-based framing from Kip McGrath and Tutor Doctor.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Build out VCE subject pages.** Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring` is a single hub. Learnmate is systematically capturing "VCE [subject] tutor" intent one page at a time. A per-subject page set — Methods, Specialist, English, Chemistry, Biology, Physics, Economics, Accounting, Legal, Business Management — would directly contest their strategy and slot naturally under the existing `/vce-tutoring` path.
2. **Economics and Accounting specifically.** These are the subjects Learnmate just moved on, suggesting they see demand. Worth prioritising in any VCE subject page rollout so we're not chasing them 6+ months late.
3. **Angle we can beat them on.** Learnmate's new pages are thin template pages ("experts in effective online tutoring for students of all ages"). Tuterly could publish genuinely useful VCE subject pages — study design breakdown, exam structure, common student pain points, sample worksheets from `/worksheets`, links into `/atar-planner`. That's a differentiation Learnmate structurally can't match as a pure marketplace.
4. **Watch Success Tutoring and Begin Bright manually.** Both are effectively invisible in our data right now (2 URLs and a fetch failure respectively). If either is quietly rebuilding, we'll miss it. Worth one manual browse next week.
