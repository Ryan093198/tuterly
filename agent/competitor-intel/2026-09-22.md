# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-09-22

## Headline

Alchemy Tuition has launched a coordinated set of eight new subject and level landing pages this week - including a direct **VCE tutoring** page and a **private tutoring** page. This is the only meaningful competitor movement, and it puts Alchemy in more direct SEO competition with Tuterly's own `/vce-tutoring` and `/tutoring` pages.

## Alchemy Tuition

Eight new pages published, all following a consistent template ("Personalised X tutoring for Australian students. Get matched with an accredited tutor..."):

- **Curriculum pages**: `/vce-tutoring/`, `/qce-tutoring/`
- **Subject pages**: `/biology-tutoring/`, `/physics-tutoring/`, `/science-tutoring/`, `/statistics-tutoring/`
- **Level / format pages**: `/primary-school-tutoring/`, `/private-tutoring/`

A few things worth noting:

1. This looks like a deliberate build-out of a subject × level matrix rather than one-off content. Expect chemistry, English, maths and NSW HSC equivalents to follow in coming weeks.
2. Alchemy is pushing a national "get matched with an accredited tutor" positioning across all pages - very close to Tuterly's marketplace pitch, and explicitly covering both online and in-home.
3. Their new `/vce-tutoring/` page directly overlaps our `/vce-tutoring` page. Worth a side-by-side comparison on copy, tutor proof, pricing transparency and testimonials.
4. `/statistics-tutoring/` is a niche pickup we don't cover - relevant for university and late-secondary students.

## Cross-competitor patterns

The wider market is quiet - Alchemy is the only mover. That said, Alchemy's build-out reinforces a pattern we've been seeing: competitors are treating **subject-specific landing pages** (biology, physics, statistics) as the standard SEO play, not just level or state pages. Tuterly has state, curriculum (VCE) and format (online) coverage, but limited subject-specific pages beyond what sits under `/tutoring`.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Fill the subject page gap.** Alchemy now has biology, physics, science and statistics landing pages. We have `/vce-tutoring` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` but no equivalent subject-first pages. A Melbourne-anchored version (e.g. "VCE Biology tutoring Melbourne") could out-rank a generic national page.
2. **Sharpen the VCE page.** With Alchemy now targeting VCE nationally on a fresh page, our `/vce-tutoring` needs to lean harder on things they can't easily match: local Melbourne tutors, ATAR outcomes, sample reports, transparent pricing (we already have `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` and `/sample-report` - link them in).
3. **Consider a `/primary-tutoring` or similar level page.** Alchemy has just launched one; we route primary through `/parents` and `/tutoring` but don't have a dedicated primary landing page.
4. **Statistics tutoring** is uncontested territory among the others we track and useful for uni-transition students - low-effort page to test.
5. **Competitor alternative pages** - we already run `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Alchemy's aggressive expansion and national marketplace positioning, an `/alchemy-alternative` page is worth queuing up.
