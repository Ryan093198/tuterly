## Cross-competitor patterns

With no deltas to analyse, the only pattern is the absence of activity. A few observations worth carrying into next week:

- Three competitors (Begin Bright, Math Minds, Success Tutoring, Tutoring for Excellence) have now had crawl issues. If any of these persist for another week or two it's worth investigating whether they've changed platforms or let sites lapse - either would be a signal.
- The footprint gap between Learnmate (6.4k URLs) and the next-largest competitor Alchemy (2.7k) continues to widen the marketplace-vs-agency divide. Learnmate's directory scale remains the structural moat Tuterly's `/directory` and `/tutors` pages need to contest.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's nothing new to react to, this is a good week to press on standing gaps rather than chase competitor moves:

1. **Scholarship test prep** - Tuterly has `/scholarship-test-prep` but this is a topic where North Shore and Cluey both have deeper content trees. Worth an audit of whether our single page competes on depth.
2. **The comparison-page play is working and underused** - Tuterly already ranks `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate is the most structurally similar competitor and has the largest footprint, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is the obvious next addition. No competitor is publishing comparison content against us, so this remains an asymmetric channel.
3. **Melbourne-specific pricing transparency** - Tuterly's `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` is a page none of the tracked competitors have a direct equivalent to. Worth doubling down on with updated 2026 pricing data while the field is quiet.
4. **Worksheets and ATAR planner** - both are tool-style pages (`/worksheets`, `/atar-planner`) that no competitor in this list has an analogue for. If product work is happening here, promoting it now avoids the noise of a busier competitor week.
