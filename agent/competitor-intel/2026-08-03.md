No pricing pages moved, no new subject or location landing pages appeared, no positioning shifts. This is the kind of lull that's worth using offensively rather than reading anything into.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Everyone paused.** Early August, mid-Term 3 - no one is shipping new pages. If Tuterly publishes anything meaningful this week (new suburb pages, VCE content, alternative pages), it lands into empty air.
2. **Two very different site-size strategies remain in place.** Learnmate (6,419), Alchemy (2,691), Cluey (1,703) and Apex (1,698) are running large content/marketplace footprints. Kip McGrath, Tutor Doctor, Tutoring for Excellence and Math Minds are all under 500 URLs and treating their sites as brochures. Tuterly sits closer to the brochure end today; the Learnmate/Alchemy model is what to benchmark against if we want to compete on organic discovery.
3. **Data reliability issues to resolve.** Begin Bright errored and Success Tutoring returned only 2 URLs. Neither is a competitive signal - both are crawl problems we should fix so we don't miss future moves.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's nothing new to react to, this is a good week to press on gaps that have been visible for a while:

- **The "alternative" play.** We have /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative. The obvious next targets given this week's data are a Learnmate alternative page (they're the biggest marketplace competitor by URL count and the most direct model overlap) and potentially an Apex alternative or Kip McGrath alternative page. None of these competitors are publishing comparison content of their own, so we own the framing.
- **Scale content while they're idle.** Learnmate and Alchemy have built large footprints of tutor/subject/suburb pages. Tuterly's /directory and /tutoring paths are the natural equivalents - a quiet competitor week is the moment to expand these rather than chase blog topics.
- **VCE and ATAR depth.** We have /vce-tutoring and /atar-planner, both distinctive to us in this set. None of the competitors moved on VCE content this week. Worth doubling down on subject-specific VCE pages (Methods, Specialist, English, Chem) before Cluey or Apex fill that space.
- **Scholarship test prep.** /scholarship-test-prep is another page where no competitor pushed anything new. Selective/scholarship season content published now will be indexed and maturing by the Term 4 decision window.
- **Worksheets as a moat.** /worksheets remains something none of the tracked competitors appear to invest in as a primary path. Continuing to build this out differentiates us from both the marketplaces (Learnmate, Alchemy) and the franchises (Kip, Tutor Doctor).
