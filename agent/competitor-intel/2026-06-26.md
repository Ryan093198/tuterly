- **Stillness across the board.** Ten of eleven crawlable competitors made zero indexed changes. Mid-year (late June) is typically a quiet publishing window between Term 2 and Term 3 - expect a surge of "prepare for Term 3," Year 12 trial exam, and HSC/VCE mock-exam content in the next two to four weeks. Tuterly has a window to publish first.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Own the Term 3 prep window now.** Competitors haven't started publishing Term 3 / trial exam content yet. A push on `/vce-tutoring` and `/learn` with Term 3 readiness, VCE trial exam strategy, and Year 11-into-12 transition content would land before Cluey, Learnmate, or Apex move.

Consider a parent-facing outcomes page that pairs sample reports with short, specific results (subject, year level, score movement) - harder to fake than narrative testimonials and a clearer differentiator.
3. **Scholarship test prep is still uncontested in this dataset.** None of the monitored competitors touched scholarship content this week. `/scholarship-test-prep` is worth deepening - the AAS/ACER prep niche has thin coverage among generalist competitors.
4. **ATAR Planner remains a genuine moat.** No competitor has shipped anything resembling `/atar-planner`. Worth investing in tool-led acquisition while the gap holds.
5. **Centres-focused content is uncontested.** Kip McGrath, North Shore, and Begin Bright are the obvious centre-model competitors and none published anything centre-specific this week. `/centres` could pick up partnership or comparison angles without competitive response.
