## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Holiday program season is starting.** North Shore is the first mover with a January 2027 page. Expect Cluey, Kip McGrath and the centre-based players to follow in the next 2-3 weeks with their own summer intensive pages. The Tuterly angle: the app is exactly the tool a parent wants for a kid who "needs to catch up before Year 9/10" - practising fresh versions of questions they got wrong is literally the holiday-catch-up use case. A `/summer-catch-up` or `/year-9-ready` landing page framed against centre-based holiday programs (cheaper, self-paced, no driving) would land now.

2. **Nobody is publishing Years 7-10 maths content this week.** Zero new topic guides, mistake explainers or test-prep posts across ten competitors. That's a soft content market - a good time to ship foundational blog pieces (common Year 8 algebra mistakes, Year 9 linear equations worked examples, Year 10 trig test prep) without fighting fresh competitor pages for attention.

3. **No competitor app or practice-tool movement detected.** None of the tracked sites added homework/practice tooling pages this week. Tuterly's "snap a question, get fresh versions" positioning remains uncontested in this cohort.

- **Build a summer holiday landing page now** before Cluey and Kip McGrath publish theirs. Position against drive-to-a-centre holiday programs. Tuterly doesn't appear to have a `/holiday-program` or `/summer` path yet.
