## Cross-competitor patterns

Four of twelve competitors (EzyMaths, Begin Bright, North Shore, plus Alchemy and Math Minds with empty sitemaps) are effectively invisible to our current monitoring. That's a third of the tracked set we have no signal on. The pattern of two independent "zero URL" sitemap results (Alchemy and Math Minds) suggests either a shared platform quirk or deliberate sitemap suppression - worth a one-off manual visit to each.

Among the seven competitors we can see clearly, the total absence of change across a week is unusual enough to flag. Possible explanations: genuine quiet period heading into Term 4 / exam season when ops focus shifts away from site work; or our diff is only catching URL-level changes and missing on-page edits (copy, pricing, hero positioning) that wouldn't surface in a sitemap comparison.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Nothing competitor-driven this week, but the quiet gives room to push on gaps that remain structurally unaddressed by the field:

- **ATAR planning as a tool, not just content.** Our `/atar-planner` is still a differentiated asset - none of the tracked competitors appear to have a planning tool surface. Worth doubling down while the space stays empty.
- **Worksheets as a lead magnet.** `/worksheets` is another asset-type page the big players (Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath) don't seem to prioritise. If Success Tutoring's 8k URLs are mostly location/subject pages, we're competing on utility rather than page count.
- **Centres positioning.** Our `/centres` path addresses the physical-tutoring buyer; most competitors treat this as either franchise locations (Kip, Tutor Doctor) or ignore it (Cluey, Learnmate). A clearer "find a centre near you" angle could pull intent away from the franchise brands.
- **Comparison pages.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Success Tutoring and Learnmate are the largest footprints in the set, a `/success-tutoring-alternative` or `/learnmate-alternative` page may be worth testing - especially as neither has published any defensive content this week.

## Monitoring hygiene

Recommend fixing the crawl for EzyMaths (403), Alchemy and Math Minds (empty sitemaps) before next week's report, otherwise we're flying blind on roughly a third of the competitive set. Also worth adding a lightweight homepage-content diff alongside the sitemap check, so a quiet URL week doesn't mean a blind week.
