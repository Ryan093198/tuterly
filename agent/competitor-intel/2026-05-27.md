## Cross-competitor patterns

The only directional signal is North Shore's continued investment in calendar-driven content - newsletters tied to terms, holiday programs tied to school breaks. Kip McGrath and the larger brands do not appear to be doing this, at least not at a page level. It's a cheap, repeatable content rhythm that we don't currently match.

Beyond that, the silence is itself the pattern: none of the marketplace players (Learnmate, Apex, Alchemy) are publishing new comparison pages, pricing pages, or alternative pages. Our existing /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative pages are still uncontested territory - nobody is writing "Tuterly alternative" content back at us.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Seasonal / holiday content.** We have no holiday program or term-aligned landing page. North Shore is showing this is worth doing repeatedly. A "VCE July holiday intensive" or "Term 3 preparation" page would slot naturally alongside /vce-tutoring and /atar-planner.

2. **Newsletter-as-page.** North Shore publishes newsletters as indexable pages, not just emails. If we already send anything to parents or tutors, mirroring it as a /learn or /parents sub-page is essentially free content with a recurring cadence.

3. **Comparison page moat.** Nobody moved on alternative/comparison content this week. We have two (Cluey, Tutor Doctor). Worth considering a Learnmate comparison page given they're the closest marketplace competitor by model, and an Apex or Alchemy page if we want to broaden.

4. **Success Tutoring's near-invisible footprint** (2 URLs) is worth a manual investigation - if they're genuinely thin, there may be a positioning gap in their segment we can take.
