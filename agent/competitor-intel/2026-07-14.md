## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Location pages under pressure.** Learnmate's cleanup is the second data point in recent months suggesting the "postcode landing page" playbook has diminishing returns. Worth watching before we invest heavily in expanding `/directory` or building suburb-level pages of our own.
- **Content velocity is low across the category.** Ten of twelve competitors published nothing at all this week. Cluey and Tutor Doctor - the two names we already run alternative pages against - remain static. There is room to out-publish this field without needing much volume.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Competition/enrichment content.** North Shore uses AMC event pages as a hook. We have no equivalent on `/learn` or `/parents` covering maths/science competitions, Olympiads, or extension programs Melbourne families care about. A guide to AMC, ICAS, and AMT for VCE-track students would fill a genuine gap and complements `/scholarship-test-prep`.
2. **Location strategy - go deeper, not wider.** While Learnmate retreats from thin suburb pages, we could differentiate `/best-tutoring-melbourne` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` with genuinely useful suburb-level content (travel times, local school context, in-home vs online tradeoffs) rather than templated postcode pages. Quality over coverage.
3. **Alternative pages remain a moat while competitors sit still.** With Cluey and Tutor Doctor both publishing nothing, our `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative` pages face no counter-messaging. Good moment to refresh them with current pricing comparisons and updated testimonials.
