# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-08-24

## Headline

North Shore Coaching College appears to have dropped roughly 699 URLs from its site this week, shrinking to just 306 total pages. The removals include the homepage itself, the About page, Chinese-language variants (e.g. `/about-zh/`, `/登陆/`), and a large batch of promotional landing pages - enrolment vouchers, "20% / 25% / 35% off tuition" new-student offers, a Term 2 2026 promo, and a Carlingford grand-opening 50%-off page. This looks less like a strategic pivot and more like either a site migration, a crawl/access issue on their end, or a deliberate purge of expired promo pages. Worth watching next week to see if the count rebounds.

Everything else is quiet. No new pages tracked across any other competitor this week.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Site-wide dormancy.** Eleven of twelve competitors published nothing new this week. This is unusual enough to note - either a mid-year lull or a coincidence, but it means any content Tuterly ships right now lands into quiet air.
- **Promo-driven acquisition is a North Shore signature.** The removed URLs reveal a playbook of stacked percentage-off offers gated by T&Cs pages. Tuterly's pricing pages (`/tutoring-prices-melbourne`) don't currently lean on time-boxed offers - there's a test worth running.
- **Language segmentation gap.** North Shore is the only competitor in this set with visible Mandarin content. If Tuterly wants to court the Melbourne Chinese-speaking parent market (a meaningful segment for VCE and scholarship prep), there's no direct marketplace competitor doing this well.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Own the "expired competitor promo" gap.** If North Shore has genuinely pulled its discount pages, prospective parents searching for those offers will land nowhere. A comparison page or a "considering North Shore?" angle could capture that intent - similar to the existing Cluey and Tutor Doctor alternative pages.
2. **Scholarship test prep depth check.** North Shore historically competes hard on selective/scholarship prep. With their site in flux, this is a good week to audit `/scholarship-test-prep` against what Cluey and Apex still publish and widen the lead.
3. **Mandarin-language landing page for VCE / scholarship parents.** No direct marketplace competitor is serving this audience. Even a single well-built page tied to `/vce-tutoring` or `/scholarship-test-prep` would be differentiating.
4. **Directory depth vs Learnmate.** Not a this-week action, but Learnmate's 6,400+ URL footprint versus our `/directory` deserves a structured comparison - subject coverage, suburb coverage, profile richness.
