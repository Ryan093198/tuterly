## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Total publishing freeze.** Ten of twelve competitors had crawlable sitemaps and none published or removed anything. This is unusual enough to flag - it may reflect a pre-exam-season lull (Nov is late in the school year) or a reporting artefact. Worth confirming next week that the diffing is actually catching changes and not under-reporting.

2. **No competitor app movement detected.** Nothing in the crawl suggests any of these players launched or promoted a student-facing practice app this week. Tuterly's iPhone app remains unchallenged in positioning among this set.

3. **No new Year 7-10 maths content from anyone.** No new topic guides, common-mistakes posts, or test-prep pages appeared. This is the opportunity, not the threat - see below.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because nothing moved on the competitor side, the strategic read is about *standing gaps* rather than new ones. With the quiet week, lean into content production:

- **Year 7-10 maths topic guides.** None of the maths-heavy competitors (Math Minds, EzyMaths, Cluey) published anything new this week. We have an open field to publish topic-by-topic guides (linear equations, Pythagoras, surds, quadratics, trig ratios, probability, indices) aimed at the exact cohort the app serves. These double as SEO surface and as in-app content hooks.

- **"Common mistakes" content.** This is a natural fit for the snap-a-wrong-question product. No competitor is publishing in this angle. A series like "the 5 mistakes Year 9 students make on linear equations" maps directly to what the app sees students doing.

- **End-of-year and Year 10 → VCE transition prep.** Timing-appropriate for November. Cluey and Success Tutoring have the audience for this but published nothing this week.

- **App-first positioning.** Since no competitor is pushing a practice tool, our homepage and `/learn` surface could lean harder on "snap the question you got wrong" as the one-line differentiator. Nothing in the market is contesting that phrase right now.

- **Comparison pages.** `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative` exist; neither competitor changed anything to force a rewrite, but both are static enough that a refresh with app-specific angles (self-serve practice vs scheduled sessions) would age well.
