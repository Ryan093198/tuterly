# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-11-13

## Headline observation

Learnmate has quietly pulled its entire university tutoring vertical (12 subject landing pages gone, including the top-level `/tutors/university-tutors/` hub) along with two VCE online tutor pages (Accounting, Economics). This is the only substantive movement across the competitor set this week. It suggests Learnmate is narrowing its focus - likely away from university and possibly away from lower-demand VCE electives - and consolidating around its core school-years tutor marketplace. Worth watching whether more VCE subject pages disappear in coming weeks.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Very quiet week.** Nine competitors we can see had zero changes. The only real movement was Learnmate's cleanup. There is no new content, pricing, or positioning shift to react to.

2. **Learnmate stepping back from university tutoring.** None of the other marketplaces in our set lean heavily on uni tutoring either - this seems to confirm the Australian online tutoring market is converging on school-years (primary through Year 12) as the viable segment. Reinforces Tuterly's Years 7-10 focus.

3. **Three competitors unreadable** (EzyMaths, Begin Bright, North Shore). Of those, EzyMaths is the most strategically relevant given the maths overlap with the app. Flag for manual review.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **VCE subject pages.** With Learnmate quietly dropping VCE Accounting and Economics online tutor pages, there's a small opening to build out dedicated subject pages under `/vce-tutoring` for the VCE electives competitors are abandoning. Low-cost SEO play.

- **Years 7-10 maths content gap remains unaddressed by competitors this week.** No competitor published new topic guides, common-mistake breakdowns, or test-prep content for Years 7-10 maths. This continues to be open ground for Tuterly's content strategy to support the app - things like "Year 8 linear equations: the 5 mistakes students keep making" or "Year 9 Pythagoras practice questions" that map directly to the snap-and-practise loop.

- **Manual competitor app audit overdue.** Nothing surfaced in sitemaps this week about competitor apps or homework tools, but Kip McGrath, Cluey, and EzyMaths all have practice/homework products worth benchmarking against the Tuterly app's "snap a wrong question, get fresh versions" core loop. Suggest a one-off manual review next week rather than waiting for sitemap signals.

- **EzyMaths blind spot.** They are the closest pure-maths competitor and we cannot see their site via sitemap. Worth a manual content and pricing check so we are not surprised.
