# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-06-02

**Headline:** Learnmate has just published a two-part NAPLAN content play aimed at parents ahead of the March 2026 sittings - a guide to the test itself and a results-interpretation piece. NAPLAN is a parent-anxiety topic Tuterly currently has no dedicated landing page for, and Learnmate is moving early to own the search demand.

A very quiet week overall. Most competitors made no visible changes. Begin Bright's site failed to fetch - worth re-checking next cycle.

## Learnmate

Two new parent-facing NAPLAN pages:

- **NAPLAN: A Guide for Students and Parents (2026)** - frames the 11-23 March 2026 testing window, explains test structure, timings and proficiency levels, and funnels into NAPLAN tutoring in Sydney, Melbourne and online.
- **What Your Child's NAPLAN Results Mean** - explainer on the proficiency report, how to read the dot/shaded box, and "what to do next" advice for parents.

The combination is deliberate: one page captures pre-test intent, the other captures post-results-day panic. Both lean explanatory/parent-reassurance rather than salesy, with tutoring as the soft conversion. Learnmate is leaning further into the parent-as-decision-maker funnel and refreshing seasonal content well ahead of the 2026 cycle.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **NAPLAN is being seeded now for March 2026.** Learnmate is the only mover this week but it's a clear early bid. Expect Cluey and the franchise players (Kip McGrath, Begin Bright, North Shore) to follow with their own NAPLAN content in the next 4-8 weeks.
2. **Seasonal/term-specific landing pages remain the dominant new-page format** at the franchise end of the market (North Shore). These are evergreen-rebuilt rather than written fresh, but they do capture local + temporal search intent that marketplace sites typically don't.
3. **Genuinely slow week for content.** Nine of twelve tracked competitors published nothing. June is mid-term in most states - the next burst will likely land late June / early July around holiday programs.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **NAPLAN gap is real and urgent.** Tuterly has no /naplan path. A two-page structure mirroring Learnmate's (a parent guide + a results-interpretation page) would be defensible and timely. Tuterly's angle could be stronger than Learnmate's by: (a) tying directly into the existing /worksheets product for practice, (b) using /sample-report style transparency to show what tutor-led NAPLAN support actually looks like, and (c) pairing with the /atar-planner brand to position Tuterly as the data-literate option for parents who want to understand results, not just react to them.
- **"What your results mean" is a content angle Tuterly could own more broadly.** Learnmate is doing it for NAPLAN; Tuterly could extend the same pattern to VCE study scores, scholarship test results, and school report cards - all of which align with existing pages (/vce-tutoring, /scholarship-test-prep, /sample-report).
- **Holiday programs as exam prep, not catch-up.** North Shore's framing (holiday program filed under exam-preparation) is a positioning Tuterly's /vce-tutoring and /atar-planner could borrow for a July intensives push, especially with VCE mid-year exams behind students and unit 3/4 momentum needed.
- **Competitor inertia is an opening.** With nine competitors publishing nothing this week, any consistent publishing cadence from Tuterly compounds quickly in the parent-research segment.
