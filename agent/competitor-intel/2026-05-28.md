## Learnmate
Two new pages this week, both following the same template:

- `/tutors/online-vce-accounting-tutors/` - "Hire the Best Online VCE Accounting Tutors"
- `/tutors/online-vce-economics-tutors/` - "Hire the Best Online VCE Economics Tutors"

Both lead with "online" and "VCE [subject]" in the title and frame tutors as subject experts for "students of all ages". This continues Learnmate's long-running pattern of one-page-per-VCE-subject, which is how their URL count has grown past 6,300.

Opportunity for Tuterly: we have `/vce-tutoring` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` but no subject-specific VCE pages. Accounting and Economics are both relatively underserved VCE niches (smaller cohorts than Methods/English, less competition) and would be cheap wins to publish as dedicated pages - particularly with a Melbourne angle Learnmate's generic pages don't have.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Almost everyone is dormant.** Of 11 competitors fetched successfully, 10 published nothing. This is a genuinely quiet week, not a data issue - the pattern is consistent with end-of-term timing.

2. **Learnmate is the only competitor with a live content engine.** They are slowly but consistently widening their subject-page coverage. Over a year this compounds; over a week it's two pages. Tuterly should decide whether to match this surface-area strategy on a Melbourne/VCE-specific basis, or deliberately stay narrower and tool-led (ATAR planner, worksheets, directory).

3. **Subject-specific VCE landing pages are a structural gap for Tuterly.** Learnmate, and to a lesser extent Cluey, both index per-subject. Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring` is a single hub. Even publishing 6-10 high-priority VCE subject pages (Methods, Specialist, English, Further, Chemistry, Physics, Biology, Accounting, Economics, Legal) would close that gap and give the existing `/vce-tutoring` page a proper internal link cluster.

4. **No pricing or positioning shifts detected anywhere.** No competitor changed how they describe themselves, their pricing pages, or their core offer this week.
