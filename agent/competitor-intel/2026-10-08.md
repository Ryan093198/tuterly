# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-08

## Headline

Alchemy Tuition has done a major URL restructure this week: ~706 old suburb pages removed and ~707 new ones added. They've shifted from a flat `/{suburb}-english-tutoring/` pattern to a nested `/maths-tutoring/sydney/{suburb}/` structure, and the content focus has flipped from English (national coverage) to Maths (Sydney-only in the sample). This is a deliberate re-pointing of their location-page strategy toward Sydney maths - the single biggest competitive move this week.

## Alchemy Tuition

- **Scale of change**: 707 new URLs, 706 removed, out of 2,700 total. Roughly a quarter of their site turned over.
- **New pattern**: `/maths-tutoring/sydney/{suburb}/` - the sample alone covers Hornsby, Hurstville, Kellyville, Killara, Kensington, Kingsford, Kings Cross, Jannali, Ingleburn, Illawong and ~15 more Sydney suburbs. Every page uses the same template ("#1 Maths Tutoring {Suburb}! More than 15 years of experience... K-12... Book online today").
- **What was killed**: the entire `/{city}-english-tutoring/` national footprint - Adelaide, Albury, Alice Springs, Ballarat, Bendigo, Armidale, Bathurst and dozens more regional/interstate English pages. They've abandoned national English SEO.
- **Oddity**: one of the new URLs is `/maths-tutoring/sydney/jobs/` - looks like a templating bug where a "Jobs" nav item got slotted into the suburb loop. Worth watching whether they fix it.
- **Read**: Alchemy is doubling down on Sydney + Maths and walking away from national English coverage. They're not a direct Melbourne threat on the new pages, but the playbook (deep per-suburb pages under a `/subject/city/suburb/` tree) is instructive.

## Cluey Learning

- 1,290 URLs removed, 0 added. Looks like a large sitemap cleanup rather than a strategic shift - the sample is dominated by staging duplicates (`-staging`, `-ToBeDeleted`, `-productreview`, `-trustpilot` variants) and dated artefacts (`2023-options`, `2024-options`, `atar-quiz-for-parents` variants).
- Nothing suggests a product or positioning change. Mostly housekeeping.
- Minor note: the removed `english-literary-techniques` page is the kind of VCE/HSC English resource worth checking if it was actually a useful ranking page - may be a gap they've opened up.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Per-suburb landing pages remain the dominant SEO play** at the agency end of the market. Alchemy's restructure reinforces the `/subject/city/suburb/` pattern as the current best-practice shape.
- **Thin, templated local pages are everywhere** - Alchemy's new pages all read as near-identical copy with the suburb name swapped in. Google increasingly discounts this, but competitors are still betting on it.
- **Nobody else moved this week.** For a cohort of 13 sites, having 10 flat is unusually quiet - either a lull or our crawl is catching stable sitemaps between release cycles.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Melbourne per-suburb maths pages**: Alchemy has just proven they think suburb-level maths pages are worth the engineering effort - but they've conceded Melbourne entirely in this round. We have `/online-tutoring-melbourne`, `/vce-tutoring` and `/best-tutoring-melbourne` but no per-suburb depth. A programmatic `/tutoring/melbourne/{suburb}/` tree (ideally driven by actual tutor availability from our directory, not templated copy) would differentiate on substance as well as fill the gap.
2. **English coverage gap**: Alchemy has just deleted ~700 English pages nationally, and Cluey dropped an English literary techniques resource. If we want to lean into VCE English specifically, the competitive density has just dropped. Nothing on our site currently targets English as a subject.
3. **Avoid the templating trap**: Alchemy's `/maths-tutoring/sydney/jobs/` page is a cautionary tale - any programmatic suburb strategy we build needs human review on the suburb list.
4. **"Compare" pages still underexploited**: we have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Cluey's visible cleanup activity (and the dated 2023/2024 artefacts suggesting they're not maintaining content tightly), there's a credible angle for a freshness/comparison update highlighting what they've removed or deprioritised.
5. **ATAR tools**: Cluey has removed multiple `atar-quiz-for-parents` variants. Our `/atar-planner` is a differentiator worth pushing harder in parent-facing content while they're between iterations of their equivalent.
