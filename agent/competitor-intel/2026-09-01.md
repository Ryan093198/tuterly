## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Suburb pages are the only thing anyone is building right now.** Kip's Kenmore page is the sole publication event across ~12,000 monitored URLs. The bricks-and-mortar chains (Kip, North Shore, Begin Bright) continue to treat suburb-level landing pages as their primary growth lever.
- **Marketplaces are static this week.** Learnmate, Cluey and Apex - our closest structural competitors - didn't publish. No new subject hubs, no new exam-prep content, no pricing moves.
- **Silence on VCE / ATAR season.** Despite September being the run-up to VCE exams, no competitor pushed new VCE or ATAR content this week. Given we own `/vce-tutoring` and `/atar-planner`, this is a window to publish while nobody else is.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Melbourne suburb pages.** Kip is quietly compounding a suburb-page moat nationally. We have strong Melbourne-specific pages (`/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne`, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne`) but nothing at the suburb level. A programmatic set of Melbourne suburb pages, tied to our directory, would attack their local-search advantage on our home turf.
2. **VCE exam-window content.** Nobody is publishing into VCE season. Practical, timely content on `/vce-tutoring` and `/atar-planner` (last-month study plans, exam-day logistics, subject-specific revision) could land uncontested.
3. **Scholarship test timing.** No competitor moved on scholarship or selective test content this week either. Our `/scholarship-test-prep` page could be expanded with year-specific test date content ahead of the 2027 intake cycle.
4. **"Learnmate alternative" page.** We have Cluey and Tutor Doctor alternative pages but not Learnmate, who is our closest marketplace analogue and has 6,425 indexed pages working for them. Worth considering.
5. **Centres angle.** Kip's continued centre-page expansion is a reminder that parents searching for in-person tutoring are a real segment. Our `/centres` page could lean harder into the "marketplace of centres" positioning against the franchise model.
