# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-11-04

## Headline

Apex Tuition has rolled out a full Year 6 maths worksheet library this week - 24 topic-specific worksheet pages plus an index page. This extends their already-visible worksheet play one year below our Year 7-10 target band, which matters because Year 6 parents are exactly who we want discovering Tuterly before high school starts. Apex is building the organic funnel we should be defending.

## Per competitor

### Apex Tuition Australia
25 new pages, all worksheets. One hub page (`/resources/year-6-maths-worksheets-free-worksheets-answers`) and 24 individual topic worksheets under `/worksheets/year-6-maths-*`. The topic list is granular and curriculum-aligned:

- Number/algebra: adding & subtracting decimals, adding & subtracting fractions, comparing & ordering fractions, multiplying/dividing decimals by powers of 10, prime/composite/square numbers, integers in context, estimating with decimals/fractions/percentages, fractions-decimals-percentages of a quantity, finding unknown values in number sentences, growing patterns & rules, algorithms & number machines

- Financial: money maths (earning, saving, budgeting)

Pattern: they're clearly working year-by-year through the Australian Curriculum. Expect Year 7 and Year 8 batches in coming weeks - which is directly in our zone.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Worksheet-topic SEO is the live battleground.** Apex is the only mover but they moved hard. They're building a defensible library of granular, topic-level pages with "free worksheets & answers" framing. Our `/worksheets` path is the natural equivalent - if it isn't already structured as a per-year, per-topic index with internal linking, Apex will out-rank it within two quarters.
2. **Everyone else is static this week.** The big incumbents (Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Kip, Success, Alchemy) shipped nothing. Quiet week from a content standpoint, which makes Apex's push more notable.
3. **No new app or practice-tool activity detected** at any competitor. Our Tuterly iPhone-app positioning remains unchallenged in this dataset.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Pre-empt Apex at Year 7-8.** Apex has just finished Year 6; Years 7 and 8 are likely next. Build out Year 7 and Year 8 maths topic pages on `/worksheets` or `/learn` now, with the same granularity (one URL per topic: e.g. "Year 7 integers", "Year 7 algebraic expressions", "Year 8 linear equations", "Year 8 Pythagoras"). Tie each page to a "practise fresh versions in the Tuterly app" CTA - we have a product hook they don't.
- **Common-mistakes angle - a wedge Apex doesn't own.** Apex worksheets are generic practice. Our app's premise is "questions students got wrong." Lean into content Apex can't credibly copy: "Top 10 mistakes Year 8 students make with linear equations", "Why Year 9 students lose marks on surds", etc. This is a defensible editorial angle tied directly to the product.
- **Year 6 gateway content.** Since Apex is pulling Year 6 traffic, consider a small Year 6 maths section framed as "getting ready for Year 7" - captures the same searches but routes parents to the app before high school.
- **Topic coverage gap on our site.** Our top-level paths show no `/year-7-maths`, `/year-8-maths` etc. hubs. Even a minimal landing page per year level would give the app a proper SEO scaffold and a place to showcase sample questions.
- **Learnmate VCE expansion** reinforces that VCE subject pages still earn traffic - worth checking our `/vce-tutoring` page covers Economics and Accounting as subject sub-pages if we want to compete there, though this is secondary to the maths-app core.
