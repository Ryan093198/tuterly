# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-07-02

## Headline

Cluey has re-exposed roughly 1,290 URLs this week - not a genuine content blitz, but a large-scale sitemap resurfacing that includes live acquisition pages worth studying (a "win a year of free tutoring" competition, a grandparents discount funnel, a Year 7 parent guide, and a NAPLAN 2024 landing page). The rest of the competitor set was silent. The single most useful signal: **Cluey is running a 20%-off "tutoring for grandchildren" offer** - a customer segment none of the direct Melbourne competitors, or Tuterly, currently address.

## Cluey Learning

Sitemap ballooned from baseline to 1,703 URLs with 1,290 "new" entries. A meaningful chunk are 404s (staging/versioned URLs like `school-terms-dates-public-holidays-australia-2023ver-09-01-24/*` across every state, plus `partners-staging-01-25-24-ToBeDeleted` and quiz staging pages) - so treat the raw number with caution. That said, several live pages are worth flagging:

- **"WIN: One year of free school tutoring"** (`/win-tutoring-every-week`) - a lead-gen competition with viral referral framing ("I just entered this competition - you should enter it too"). Same referral copy pattern reused on `/free-maths-worksheets`.
- **Grandparents funnel** (`/grandkids`) - 20% off tutoring for grandchildren, positioned emotionally ("it hurts just as much as if they were your own kids"). Novel audience segment.
- **Year 7 parent guide** (`/year-7`) - transition-to-high-school content aimed at parents. Cluey is leaning into life-stage anxiety rather than subject-by-subject SEO.
- **Schools directory** (`/schools`) - an Australia-wide school listing/guide. Direct overlap with Tuterly's `/directory`.
- **NAPLAN landing pages** for both 2023 and 2024, with test dates and links to practice tests/programs.
- **School holiday activities** pages for Brisbane, Gold Coast, Canberra, Sydney - note the title/URL mismatches (Brisbane URL titled "Sydney", Gold Coast titled "Canberra"), suggesting a hastily templated rollout.
- **Maths quiz for parents** - engagement/gamification hook ("Are you as smart as your child?").
- Two "swag" staging pages (`/swag-tutors-staging`, `/swag-customers`) rendering the generic homepage - possibly upcoming merch or referral rewards program. Worth watching.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **The market is quiet.** Ten of twelve competitors published nothing this week. Cluey is the only one actively evolving public pages, and even most of their activity is old content resurfacing rather than new work.
- **Cluey is broadening beyond "student + parent" into adjacent buyer personas** - grandparents this week, potentially referral/swag mechanics next. Everyone else is still narrowly targeting the parent-of-student.
- **Engagement gimmicks are Cluey-only** so far: parent quizzes, competitions, referral-style copy on worksheet and competition pages.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Grandparent-buyer angle is wide open.** Cluey has one page and a discount. A Melbourne-specific "tutoring gift/support for grandchildren" page under `/parents` (or a new path) would be a cheap differentiator, especially given the demographic in Melbourne's east and bayside suburbs.
2. **Year-level transition guides.** Cluey's `/year-7` guide targets the "starting high school" anxiety moment. Tuterly's `/learn` and `/parents` don't appear to cover life-stage transitions (Year 6→7, Year 10→VCE, Year 12→ATAR). This is high-intent, low-competition content and pairs naturally with `/atar-planner` and `/vce-tutoring`.
3. **NAPLAN.** No Tuterly path addresses NAPLAN. Cluey has landing pages for consecutive years. Given Tuterly already has `/worksheets` and `/scholarship-test-prep`, a NAPLAN prep page is a logical adjacent.
4. **School holiday activities (Melbourne).** Cluey is targeting Brisbane, Gold Coast, Canberra, Sydney - not Melbourne. A Melbourne holiday programs / holiday tutoring page would fill an obvious geographic gap they've left open.
5. **Schools directory depth.** Cluey's `/schools` competes with Tuterly's `/directory`. Worth auditing whether Tuterly's directory ranks/converts against theirs, particularly for individual school-name searches.
6. **Referral/competition mechanic.** Cluey's "win a year of tutoring" is a lead-capture play. A Tuterly equivalent (e.g. free term of tutoring, or free ATAR planner premium) could be tested cheaply.
