## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **End-of-financial-year content silence.** The week before EOFY produced no new service pages, pricing updates, or campaign landing pages across any competitor. If Tuterly wanted to run an EOFY angle (prepaid packages, receipts for tax-deductible education spending where applicable, Term 3 enrolment push), there's no competing noise.

2. **Three sitemap failures in one week** (Begin Bright, Alchemy, Math Minds, plus Success Tutoring's suspicious 2-URL result). That's a quarter of the set. Worth confirming our crawler isn't the common factor before next week's report.

3. **Testimonial/story campaigns as a tactic.** North Shore's move is the only real activity. Structured customer-story collection - especially with parents of VCE students - is something Tuterly could execute better given the marketplace model gives access to far more story sources than a single coaching brand.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Customer story engine.** North Shore is clearly setting one up. Tuterly has more raw material (every tutor-student match is a potential story) and could productise this as a `/stories` or `/results` hub - something none of the named competitors currently have indexed.

- **Quiet week = publish week.** None of Cluey, Tutor Doctor, or Learnmate shipped new content. If there are drafts sitting in the queue for `/vce-tutoring` subject expansions, scholarship test variants, or new suburb pages, this is a cheap week to ship them.

- **Gaps still standing from prior weeks.** Worth re-confirming, but based on Tuterly's current paths, the `/atar-planner` and `/sample-report` tools remain distinctive - no competitor in this set appears to publish equivalent interactive or transparency assets. Doubling down on tool-led pages (planners, calculators, sample outputs) continues to be defensible territory.

- **Marketplace transparency angle vs Learnmate.** Learnmate's 6,430-URL footprint is mostly tutor profiles. Tuterly's `/directory` and `/tutors` paths are the equivalent surface - any improvement to profile depth, verification badges, or pricing clarity directly attacks their main asset while they're not iterating.
