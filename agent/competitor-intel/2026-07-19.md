# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-07-19

## Headline

A completely quiet week. Every competitor we successfully crawled shows zero new or removed URLs versus last week's baseline. No pricing pages moved, no new subject or location pages, no new comparison or landing pages. This is unusual across a set of 12 competitors and worth treating as a strategic window rather than a slow news week - if nobody is shipping, anything Tuterly publishes now has more room to land.

## Cross-competitor patterns

The only pattern this week is the absence of one. Twelve competitors, four weeks into winter school holidays territory, and nothing shipped. A few possible reads:

- Mid-year is a lull for content and product on the competitor side. If true, this repeats annually and August/September is when they push new VCE/HSC prep and Term 4 pricing.
- Marketplace-style competitors (Learnmate, Apex) grow through tutor and profile pages, not marketing pages, so weekly deltas will often be zero at the sitemap level even when supply is changing underneath. We may want a different signal for them - tutor count, subject coverage, suburb coverage - rather than URL diffs.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because there's no new competitor movement to react to, the useful lens this week is standing gaps in our own top-level structure versus what these competitors typically cover:

- **Selective/scholarship content depth.** We have /scholarship-test-prep and North Shore is a heavy player in that space at 984 URLs. Worth a manual pass on how their scholarship and selective pages are structured - it's the one area where a coaching-college competitor genuinely out-covers us.
- **Year-level landing pages.** Our top-level paths are strong on audience (/parents, /tutors, /centres), product (/worksheets, /directory, /atar-planner) and Melbourne SEO, but thin on year-level entry points (Year 7, Year 11, etc.). Learnmate and Apex both index heavily on year-level combinations. A gap worth filling before competitors resume publishing.
- **Subject pages beyond VCE.** We have /vce-tutoring but no visible top-level English, Maths Methods, Specialist, Chemistry etc. pages. If Learnmate is holding 6,000+ URLs largely on subject-location combinations, we're ceding that surface area.
- **Comparison pages.** We have Cluey and Tutor Doctor alternative pages. Kip McGrath is the obvious next one - national footprint, centre-based model, direct contrast with our marketplace positioning. Nobody moved on comparison content this week, so publishing a /kip-mcgrath-alternative now has clean air.

## Recommendation

Treat this as a build week rather than a react week. Ship the Kip McGrath comparison page and start scoping year-level landing pages while the competitor set is dormant. Also flag Begin Bright, Alchemy and Math Minds as needing a manual check next week - if they error again, we should switch to homepage scraping for those three rather than sitemap-based diffing.
