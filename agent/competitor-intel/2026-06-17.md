1. **Zero net change across nine working competitors.** This is the cleanest "do nothing" week we've seen. Treat it as a publishing window rather than a defensive one.

2. **Footprint hierarchy is unchanged**: Learnmate (6.4k) >> Alchemy (2.7k) > Cluey (1.7k) ≈ Apex (1.7k) > EzyMaths (440) > Tutor Doctor (390) > Kip McGrath (320) > Tutoring for Excellence (200) > Math Minds (66). ## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because nothing moved this week, the opportunity list is the same one I'd flag from the static picture:

- **Mid-year / Term 3 content gap.** None of the competitors pushed anything timed to end-of-Term-2 reports or VCE trial exam season. A `/learn` piece on "what to do with a mid-year VCE result" or a trial exam prep guide under `/vce-tutoring` would land in clean air.
- **Scholarship test prep timing.** Our `/scholarship-test-prep` page is well-positioned for the Feb 2027 testing cycle - July/August is when parents start searching. No competitor has shipped new scholarship content this week, so we're not behind.
