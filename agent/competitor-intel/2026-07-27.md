# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-07-27

## Headline

A very quiet week. Only Learnmate shipped anything of substance: two new online VCE subject landing pages (Economics and Accounting). Everyone else was static, and Begin Bright's site failed to fetch.

The strategic point: Learnmate is methodically building out per-subject VCE landing pages under an `/online-vce-{subject}-tutors/` pattern. This is a directory-style SEO play targeting VCE parents searching by subject. Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring` page is single-URL by comparison, so we're competing one page against what looks like a growing set of subject-specific doorways.

## Cross-competitor patterns

There isn't a broad pattern to draw from a single active competitor, but one signal is worth naming: the mid-year window (late July, right after Term 2 reports come home) is historically when parents shop for VCE tutors, and Learnmate is the only competitor visibly staking new ground for it. Everyone else - including Cluey and Tutor Doctor, our two named alternative pages - is coasting on existing inventory.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **VCE subject-level pages.** Learnmate is claiming subject-by-subject VCE search real estate. Tuterly has `/vce-tutoring` as a single page. Consider spinning up subject pages (Methods, Specialist, Chemistry, Physics, Biology, English, Economics, Accounting, Legal, Business Management) that go deeper than Learnmate's template - e.g. actual scaling data, past exam trends, sample study plans from `/atar-planner`. Learnmate's pages read as thin directory listings; we can outclass them on content depth.

2. **Economics and Accounting specifically.** These are the two Learnmate just launched, which means they're indexing fresh with no ranking history. A better Tuterly page published now can compete on equal footing rather than fighting entrenched authority.

3. **Mid-year timing content.** No competitor has published anything tied to Term 2 report cards or "is my child on track for VCE" - a natural angle for `/parents` or `/learn` right now, and one nobody is contesting this week.

4. **Cluey and Tutor Doctor stasis.** Both are static. Our `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative` pages face no new counter-messaging, so any updates we make (fresh testimonials, updated pricing comparisons) will land unopposed.

5. **Begin Bright uncertainty.** Their site failed to fetch. If they're having ongoing infrastructure issues, that's a soft moment to press on primary-years positioning where they compete - worth watching for two more weeks before acting.

## What to watch next week

- Whether Learnmate publishes more VCE subject pages (Methods and English are the tells for a full rollout).
- Begin Bright fetch status.
- Any Term 3 back-to-school messaging from Kip McGrath or North Shore, who typically refresh around this time.
