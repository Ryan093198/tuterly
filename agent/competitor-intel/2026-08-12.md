## Cross-competitor patterns

Two patterns worth naming even in a quiet week:

1. **Mid-August lull.** None of the ten successfully-crawled competitors published or removed anything. This lines up with the end of the winter school holidays and the run-in to Term 3 - they've likely done their content push in June/July and are now in acquisition mode rather than publishing mode. If we want to ship content that lands ahead of a Term 4 / VCE exam decision cycle, the next 3-4 weeks are an uncontested window.

2. **Two marketplaces, very different scale.** Learnmate sits at 6,424 URLs versus our directory footprint. Alchemy at 2,692 and Apex at 1,698 also dwarf the tuition-agency sites (Kip McGrath 323, Tutor Doctor 398). The scale gap is almost entirely tutor-profile and subject/location combination pages. Worth confirming how our /directory and /tutors sections compare in indexed page count.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because nothing shipped this week, these are standing gaps rather than reactions:

- **No competitor has an ATAR planner tool.** Our /atar-planner is a genuine differentiator none of them are contesting. Worth pushing harder in acquisition copy and considering a linkable embed or shareable version.
- **No competitor has a "vs [competitor]" comparison page structure visible in the data.** Our /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative pages are unopposed - the competitors themselves are not defending against these queries. Adding /learnmate-alternative and /kip-mcgrath-alternative would extend the same play into uncontested territory.
- **Worksheets.** None of the tracked competitors show a /worksheets equivalent in the baseline URL counts (though we'd need a deeper look to confirm). If that holds, /worksheets is a lead-gen asset none of them are matching.
- **Melbourne-specific pricing transparency.** Our /tutoring-prices-melbourne page has no visible equivalent at Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath or Learnmate. Given no one moved on this, it stays a defensible position.
- **Scholarship test prep.** Our /scholarship-test-prep page targets a high-intent parent segment; none of the competitors appear to have shipped anything in this space this week, and it's worth checking whether any of them have a comparable page at all.
