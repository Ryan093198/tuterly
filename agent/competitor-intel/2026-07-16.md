# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-07-16

## Headline

A genuinely quiet week. Across eleven successfully crawled competitors, there were zero new pages published and only one page removed (a single Learnmate tutor listing). No pricing shifts, no new positioning, no new content categories. This is worth noting in itself: the direct competitor set is in a holding pattern heading into the second half of the year, which gives Tuterly a clear window to publish net-new material without being drowned out.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Total content freeze.** Not a single new URL across ~15,000 tracked pages. Mid-July is normally quiet (school holidays, end of FY hangover), but this is unusually flat. If it persists another 2-3 weeks it's a signal that most competitors are under-investing in web content right now.

2. **Learnmate is trimming, not growing.** The TASC accounting removal fits a pattern we've seen from marketplaces where the long tail of state-subject combinations gets pruned when it doesn't rank or convert. Their directory is still by far the largest of any competitor (6,417 URLs) so this doesn't materially weaken them, but they're no longer expanding for expansion's sake.

3. **Nobody is publishing "alternative to" or comparison pages.** Tuterly's `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative` pages still have no direct counter-pages from those competitors, and no other competitor has published comparison content either. This remains uncontested ground.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Publish while they're silent.** A week where every competitor sits still is a cheap chance to ship content and be the only fresh voice in the category. Semester 2 planning content, Year 12 mock-exam prep, and VCE trial-exam guides would land into an empty field.

- **Topic gaps still open.** Reviewing our top-level paths against competitor coverage, we still don't have anything on:
  - Primary-school tutoring (Kip McGrath, Begin Bright, North Shore all lean here; we have nothing equivalent to `/primary` or year-level landing pages below Year 7).
  - Selective / scholarship content beyond `/scholarship-test-prep` - North Shore and Begin Bright both have deeper selective-exam funnels worth studying.
  - NAPLAN. None of our tracked paths mention it, and it's a recurring parent search intent that several competitors own.

- **Extend the "alternative to" play.** With no defensive response from Cluey or Tutor Doctor, and no comparison content from anyone else, adding `/learnmate-alternative` or `/kip-mcgrath-alternative` pages would extend a category we uniquely occupy.

- **Watch Learnmate's pruning.** If they keep removing state-subject long-tail pages, there may be an opening for Tuterly's `/directory` to pick up the search demand they're abandoning - especially in smaller states.
