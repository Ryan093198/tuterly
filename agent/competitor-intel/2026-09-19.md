## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's nothing reactive to do, here are standing gaps worth acting on while competitors are quiet:

- **Suburb/location pages.** Success Tutoring's 8k+ and Learnmate's 6k+ footprints are almost certainly location- and subject-cross pages. Tuterly's `/best-tutoring-melbourne` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` are single pages against their hundreds. A programmatic Melbourne-suburb layer sitting under `/tutoring` or `/directory` would close the biggest structural gap in the set.
- **Year-level and subject-specific pages beyond VCE.** We have `/vce-tutoring` and `/scholarship-test-prep`. Competitors at this size range typically also cover NAPLAN, selective schools, primary-year maths and English as dedicated pages. Worth auditing which of these Learnmate and Cluey rank for that we don't have a page for.
- **Comparison pages.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate is arguably our closest competitor by model, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is a clear missing piece. A `/kip-mcgrath-alternative` also makes sense given the franchise's brand recognition among parents.
- **Centre-operator content.** `/centres` is a distinctive Tuterly asset - none of the competitors above appear to court tutoring centres as customers. This remains a defensible wedge worth investing more content into while the field is quiet.
