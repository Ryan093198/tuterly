## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Publishing has stalled almost everywhere.** Nine of the twelve competitors either shipped nothing new or couldn't be checked. The only new page in the entire set is Learnmate's dyslexia landing. This is a window where new Tuterly content will face little competing signal.
- **Specialist-audience landing pages are the emerging shape of marketplace SEO.** Learnmate's move mirrors what they've done previously with subject and location tutor pages - they're now segmenting by learner profile, not just subject or suburb. Expect neurodiversity, EAL/ESL, gifted, and exam-anxiety pages to follow.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Learning-differences coverage is a real gap.** Nothing in Tuterly's top-level paths addresses dyslexia, dyscalculia, ADHD, autism or other learning profiles. A `/dyslexia-tutors-melbourne` or broader `/learning-support-tutors` page would both plug the gap and give us something distinctly Melbourne-flavoured that Learnmate's national page doesn't.
2. **Go one level deeper than Learnmate.** Their page is a generic tutor filter. Tuterly could pair it with a genuine parent-facing explainer (what to look for in a dyslexia-aware tutor, how sessions differ, what to ask centres) - fits naturally under `/parents` and `/learn`.
3. **Cross-subject framing.** Learnmate's smartest move is positioning dyslexia support across maths, science and other subjects, not just reading. If we build this out, mirror that framing - it doubles the audience versus a literacy-only pitch.
4. **Use the quiet week.** With Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath and Success all static, this is a good moment to refresh the two alternative pages (`/cluey-alternative`, `/tutor-doctor-alternative`) with anything new we've shipped since they were written. Low risk of them counter-publishing.
5. **Watch trial-offer inflation.** Learnmate is putting "free first lesson" front and centre on specialist pages. If Tuterly doesn't have a comparable trial mechanic surfaced on high-intent pages, the comparison will start to hurt on parent decision pages.

## Suggested next-week watchlist

- Whether Learnmate follows dyslexia with more learner-profile pages (ADHD, autism, gifted).
- Retry Begin Bright, Alchemy, North Shore and Math Minds - four blind spots is more than usual.
- Success Tutoring's publishing cadence - a full week of no changes on an 8k-URL site is unusual and worth confirming.
