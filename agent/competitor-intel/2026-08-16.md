# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-08-16

## Headline

North Shore Coaching College has published a large events/programs calendar this week - 100 new pages covering holiday programs, scholarship prep, selective test prep and term-based coaching across NSW, VIC, SA, WA, ACT and Hong Kong. This is the only material content movement across the tracked competitor set, and it points to a structural play Tuterly currently doesn't match: a dated, per-region "what's on" layer that captures parents searching for specific tests, exam periods and holiday intensives.

## Per-competitor findings

### North Shore Coaching College - 100 new pages
All new URLs sit under `/events/`. The pattern breaks down into a few distinct categories:

- **Holiday and term programs by state**: WA summer holiday program, VIC term 4, ACT term 1, plus a Hong Kong series (Easter, winter, summer, Chinese New Year, Xmas/NY, and all four terms).
- **Scholarship and selective test prep**: SA ACER Scholarship, SA IGNITE Test (Year 6 and Year 7), Selective Written Assessment Test (SWAT) for Years 3-5.
- **Competition prep**: SEAMO (Southeast Asian Mathematical Olympiad) landing pages for Randwick, Belconnen and Hornsby.

Pages have no titles/descriptions in the crawl, so I can't confirm whether these are event listings, sales pages, or informational calendars - but the sheer volume and the geographic segmentation (state-level, campus-level) suggests they're building a programmatic layer aimed at parents Googling specific tests and holiday windows.

## Cross-competitor patterns

The dominant pattern is stasis - nine of the eleven successfully crawled competitors made zero public changes. The one exception (North Shore) is doubling down on **calendar/event-based landing pages tied to specific tests, competitions and school terms**. This is a different content shape from Tuterly's current pages, which are mostly service pages (tutoring by subject/city), tools (ATAR planner, worksheets) and comparison pages.

Scholarship and selective test prep continues to be a real content battleground. North Shore is now publishing dedicated pages for ACER, IGNITE, SWAT and SEAMO by campus. Tuterly has `/scholarship-test-prep` as a single page.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Named-test landing pages.** North Shore is capturing ACER, IGNITE (SA), SWAT and SEAMO searches individually. Tuterly's single `/scholarship-test-prep` page likely leaves specific-test queries on the table. Splitting into per-test pages (ACER, Edutest, selective entry, scholarship) would match how parents actually search - and Tuterly can do this without the campus constraint North Shore has.

2. **Holiday program angle.** North Shore is publishing state-specific holiday and term-break intensives. Tuterly doesn't have a "school holiday tutoring" content hook. Given the Melbourne/VIC focus, a `/vic-holiday-tutoring` or term-break intensive page would fit naturally alongside `/vce-tutoring`.

3. **Competition prep (SEAMO, olympiads).** North Shore is going after maths competition parents by campus. This is a wealthy, motivated audience with no equivalent Tuterly page. A `/maths-competition-tutoring` or per-competition page (AMC, SEAMO, ICAS) would be a cheap test.

4. **Exam-period calendar content.** North Shore's SACE and languages exam pages hint at a calendar-driven content strategy. Tuterly could do the equivalent for VCE - a maintained VCE exam timetable and per-subject exam-prep pages tied to key dates - which pairs well with the existing ATAR planner tool.

5. **Watch Learnmate's VCE subject page pruning.** If more VCE subject landing pages disappear, it's a signal that Tuterly's marketplace supply for those subjects may be a differentiator worth leaning into on comparison pages.
