## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Zero movement industry-wide.** Eleven of twelve competitors published nothing new and removed nothing. That's a signal - not a data problem - and it means anything Tuterly ships in the next fortnight lands into empty air.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's nothing new to react to, this is a good week to look at standing gaps rather than weekly moves:

- **Term 4 / VCE exam-run content.** Competitors haven't moved yet on the September-October cycle. Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring` and `/atar-planner` pages are natural anchors for a burst of exam-prep material (subject-specific revision guides, practice exam breakdowns, study-plan templates). First mover here gets the September search interest.
- **Comparison pages.** Tuterly already has `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate is the largest competitor by footprint and the most direct marketplace analogue, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is the obvious gap. Kip McGrath is also a defensible target given its franchise centre model contrasts cleanly with Tuterly's marketplace.
- **Scholarship / selective test prep.** Tuterly has `/scholarship-test-prep`. North Shore and Begin Bright both lean heavily into this segment - worth auditing whether our single page competes with their depth, especially around specific school entry exams.
- **Worksheets as a moat.** None of the tracked competitors appear to have a free worksheet library at scale. `/worksheets` is a differentiator - expanding it (VCE-aligned, year-level indexed) would be hard for franchise competitors to match quickly.
- **Centres angle.** Tuterly's `/centres` path is unusual in this set - most competitors are either pure online (Cluey), pure franchise (Kip, Begin Bright), or pure marketplace (Learnmate, Alchemy). Leaning into the hybrid positioning in comparison content could sharpen the pitch.

1. Ship VCE Term 4 / exam-prep content in the next two weeks while competitors are silent.
2. Draft a `/learnmate-alternative` page.
3. Manually verify Begin Bright and Success Tutoring next cycle - two silent weeks in a row would mean we have a monitoring problem, not a competitor problem.
