## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since nothing new dropped, the useful exercise is what competitors *still* aren't covering that we could own before they do:

1. **VCE final-term / exam countdown content.** Nobody published anything exam-timed this week. Our `/vce-tutoring` and `/atar-planner` pages are well-placed to absorb a "6 weeks out from VCE exams" push - a dated, urgent piece would have zero direct competition right now.
2. **Comparison pages beyond Cluey and Tutor Doctor.** We already have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Learnmate is the most obvious next one given how similar the marketplace model is - and their apparent homepage rebuild is a good reason to have a comparison page live before their new positioning lands. Kip McGrath is another logical addition (very different model, easy contrast).
3. **Centre-brand alternatives.** Begin Bright, North Shore and Kip McGrath all serve the primary-school/coaching-centre segment that our `/centres` page targets. None of them are producing new content to defend that ground.
4. **Scholarship and selective-school prep.** We have `/scholarship-test-prep`; none of the competitors moved on this topic this week. Worth checking whether we're ranking and, if not, expanding the page with year-level-specific sub-pages while the space is quiet.
5. **Worksheets as a moat.** Our `/worksheets` path has no equivalent at any competitor crawled this week. Continuing to expand this is low-risk - nobody is chasing us there.
