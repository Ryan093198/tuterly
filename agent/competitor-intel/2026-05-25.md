## Cross-competitor patterns

It was an unusually quiet week. Eleven of twelve competitors made no public web changes at all. The only signal is Learnmate's cleanup, and the shape of what they cut is itself informative:

- The deleted Learnmate pages cluster around **generic subject + city combinations** (chemistry/physics/maths in Sydney, Adelaide, etc.) and **role-recruitment angles** ("be a math tutor online"). If Learnmate decided these pages were hurting more than helping, it weakens the case for Tuterly to chase the same template-driven city/subject matrix.
- No competitor is currently publishing anything VCE-specific, ATAR-planning, scholarship-test, or worksheet-related that wasn't already there. Tuterly's `/atar-planner`, `/worksheets`, `/scholarship-test-prep` and `/sample-report` remain genuinely differentiated assets — nobody else is investing in this space this week.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Pick up the Learnmate orphan traffic.** The 132 URLs they killed cover high-intent queries (online maths tutor, at-home tutor, chemistry tutor Sydney, physics tutoring). If those pages had any ranking equity, it's now redistributing. Tuterly's `/online-tutoring-melbourne` and `/tutoring` pages are the obvious beneficiaries — worth a quick check on whether to expand subject-specific landing pages (chemistry, physics) under the Melbourne umbrella while the gap is fresh.

2. **"Become a tutor" recruitment content.** Several of Learnmate's deleted pages targeted aspiring tutors ("be a math tutor online"). Tuterly's `/tutors` page is the equivalent, but there may be room for more substantive recruitment content — how the marketplace works, earnings, requirements — given Learnmate has just walked away from that keyword space.

3. **No competitor moved on VCE, ATAR or scholarship prep this week.** Tuterly's lead in these areas is uncontested. Good time to keep publishing in `/learn` and expand `/vce-tutoring` subject coverage rather than chasing competitor moves.

4. **Success Tutoring blind spot.** Still only seeing 2 URLs. Worth a manual look at their site to confirm whether they're genuinely thin or whether we're missing content behind JS — this has been the case for several weeks now.
