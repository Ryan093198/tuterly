## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Near-total content freeze.** Ten of eleven successfully crawled competitors published nothing new. Either the sector is in a winter-term lull (mid-year, between Term 2 and Term 3), or competitors are focused on paid acquisition rather than content. Either way, this is a window where any new Tuterly publishing will face very little competing signal.

2. **UCAT / admissions test prep is the only movement.** North Shore's one new page is in this category. Combined with the broader pattern that North Shore, Cluey, and Apex all maintain selective-test and scholarship content, this remains an active competitive front.

3. **No pricing or positioning shifts visible** on any homepage or service URL set this week.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **UCAT prep gap.** Tuterly has `/scholarship-test-prep` but no equivalent for UCAT or other tertiary admissions tests (e.g. medical entry). North Shore is actively building state-by-state UCAT pages. If Tuterly has tutors who cover this, even a single `/ucat-tutoring` or `/ucat-prep-melbourne` page would be a low-competition entry - especially with a Melbourne/VIC angle that North Shore's WA page does not address.

- **Quiet week = publishing window.** With competitors static, this is a good week to ship the kind of pages Tuterly already does well (alternative comparison pages, location pages, ATAR tools). Anything published now has clean air.

- **Begin Bright data gap.** Fetch failed - worth a manual check next week to confirm the site is up and to re-baseline if they've migrated.

- **Success Tutoring crawl gap.** Two URLs is implausible; recommend a one-off manual review of their site to confirm what we're missing, since they're a direct Sydney/Melbourne franchise-style competitor.
