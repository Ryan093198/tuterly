# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-09-30

## Headline

Alchemy Tuition has quietly restructured its entire Sydney suburb landing-page footprint, moving from flat URLs (`/english-tutoring-lane-cove/`) to a nested subject/city/suburb hierarchy (`/english-tutoring/sydney/lane-cove/`). Same content, cleaner architecture - a signal they're doubling down on suburb-level SEO as an acquisition channel and expect to extend the pattern across subjects and cities. Worth watching if we plan to compete on Melbourne suburb search terms.

## Per competitor

### Alchemy Tuition
200 new URLs, 200 removed - a like-for-like URL migration rather than new content. New pattern: `/english-tutoring/{city}/{suburb}/`, old pattern: `/english-tutoring-{suburb}/`. Sampled suburbs are all Sydney (Lane Cove through Maroubra), and the subject in the sample is exclusively English. Two implications:
- The `/sydney/` segment in the path implies they're preparing to add other cities under the same template (Melbourne is an obvious candidate).
- The `/english-tutoring/` root implies parallel subject trees (maths, science) could follow.

If they roll this out to Melbourne suburbs for English and maths, they become a more direct search competitor to our `/best-tutoring-melbourne` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` pages.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Very quiet week overall.** Nine of eleven reachable competitors published nothing new. The two that did (Alchemy, North Shore) were doing housekeeping rather than launching content.
- **Suburb-page arms race is the live front.** Alchemy already runs thousands of suburb pages and is now investing in cleaner URL architecture. Success Tutoring (8k URLs) and Learnmate (6.4k URLs) are also operating at that scale. Tuterly's suburb/city coverage is thinner - we have `/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne` but no suburb-level tree.
- **Selective/OC/scholarship** remains a recurring theme (North Shore's category list, and consistent with Cluey's stable positioning). We have `/scholarship-test-prep` but no visible OC or selective-school specific pages.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Pre-empt Alchemy in Melbourne suburbs.** If they extend `/english-tutoring/melbourne/{suburb}/` next, they'll intercept "English tutor [suburb]" queries we're not on. A programmatic Melbourne suburb layer under `/tutoring` - genuinely useful, tied to real tutors from the marketplace rather than boilerplate - would defend and differentiate. Our marketplace inventory is a real angle Alchemy's template pages can't match.
2. **Selective / Opportunity Class content.** North Shore's category list highlights persistent demand. We cover scholarship test prep; a companion piece for Victorian selective entry (Melbourne High, Mac.Rob, John Monash) would round out `/scholarship-test-prep` and address a topic no direct competitor owns strongly in the Melbourne market.
3. **Exploit competitor quiet.** With Cluey, Tutor Doctor and Learnmate publishing nothing, there's low-cost airtime for anything on `/learn` that targets VCE Term 4 / exam-block queries right now. Our `/vce-tutoring` and `/atar-planner` pages are natural hubs for that push.
