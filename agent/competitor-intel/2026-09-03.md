The only pattern this week is stillness. Every competitor we can see either shipped nothing publicly or is running on a monthly-or-slower publishing cadence. Given school holidays are approaching in Victoria (late Sept), we'd expect a burst of Term 4 / exam-prep content from Cluey and Learnmate in the next two to four weeks. Worth watching for.

Three competitors (Alchemy, Math Minds, North Shore) remain invisible to us via sitemap. If any of them are material to Tuterly's positioning, we should switch to homepage/nav scraping for those specifically rather than relying on sitemaps.

## Standing opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's nothing new to react to, here are gaps from the existing competitive picture that Tuterly's own path list suggests are still open:

- **ATAR planner** is genuinely differentiated - none of the competitors we track have a comparable interactive tool page in their sitemaps. Worth investing more in as a top-of-funnel hook rather than treating it as a utility.
- **Scholarship test prep** - Cluey and North Shore both play in this space heavily (based on prior crawls), but Tuterly has a single page. If we want to compete here, we likely need year-level and test-specific sub-pages (Year 5 vs Year 7, ACER vs Edutest vs AAS).
- **Centres** - Kip McGrath and Begin Bright are the centre-based incumbents. Our /centres page is a positioning asset against them; consider adding direct comparison pages the way we have for Cluey and Tutor Doctor.
- **Worksheets and /learn** - Learnmate's 6.4k URL sprawl is largely content and profile pages. We don't need to match volume, but a steady worksheet release cadence would compound - and this week's quietness is a good window to ship without being drowned out.

## Recommendation for next week

Nothing urgent. Use the quiet week to ship on the ATAR planner or scholarship-prep gaps above. Flag Begin Bright and North Shore for a manual check next cycle if the crawl errors persist a third week in a row.
