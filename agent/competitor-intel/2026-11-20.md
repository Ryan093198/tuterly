## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Publishing has stalled going into end-of-year.** Zero substantive new content across the cohort. This mirrors what we'd expect in late November as Year 12 finishes and attention shifts to summer holiday campaigns. Expect a wave of "term 1 prep" and "back to school" content in mid-to-late January - that's the moment to have our Years 7-10 blog pipeline already live and indexed.

2. **No visible app or practice-tool moves.** Still no evidence any direct competitor is shipping a snap-and-practise or similar student-facing tool. Cluey remains the most likely to move here; worth a manual check of their app store listings and homepage hero next week rather than relying only on sitemap diffs.

3. **Two blind spots to fix.** EzyMaths is actively blocking crawls and Begin Bright is failing to fetch. Both are relevant - EzyMaths especially as a pure maths competitor. Suggest we rotate user agents or fall back to homepage/blog scraping for these two.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's nothing new to react to, the useful move is to keep executing on the standing content gaps:

- **Years 7-10 maths topic guides** - still no competitor shipping a structured bank of topic explainers at this year level. Our `/learn` and `/worksheets` paths are the natural home. Priorities worth drafting this week: linear equations, Pythagoras, surds and indices, probability basics, similar triangles, simultaneous equations - all high-frequency "got it wrong" topics that map directly to the app's snap-and-practise loop.

- **Common-mistake posts** - nobody in the set is doing "the five mistakes students make with [topic]" content specifically for Years 7-10. This angle is on-brand for the app (the whole product is built around the questions students got wrong) and is a natural content-to-app funnel.

- **End-of-year / summer practice angle** - with competitors quiet, there's an open lane to publish "Year 7/8/9 maths summer refresher" style pages before the January rush. These convert well because parents search them in December-January.

- **Yearly test prep** - no competitor shipped NAPLAN Year 9 or selective/scholarship maths content this week. Our `/scholarship-test-prep` page could be extended with Years 7-10 topic drilldowns to pick up the January search spike.

## Suggested actions this week

1. Ship 2-3 Years 7-10 maths topic guides under `/learn`, each ending in a clear CTA to the app.
2. Manually check Cluey's homepage and app store listings for any app/practice-tool signal the sitemap wouldn't catch.
3. Fix the EzyMaths 403 and Begin Bright fetch failure so we're not flying blind on two direct competitors.
