# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-11-19

## Headline

Apex Tuition is making a serious push into free primary maths worksheets - 39 new pages this week, almost all Year 1-3 maths worksheet landing pages plus a matching worksheet hub for each year level. This is a direct play at the same "free practice content" territory Tuterly's `/worksheets` sits in, just aimed younger. If they roll the same template up into Years 7-10, they become a much more direct content competitor to the app's target audience.

## Per competitor

### Apex Tuition Australia (39 new pages)

Two clear workstreams:

**Primary maths worksheet build-out.** New hub pages for Year 1, Year 2 and Year 3 maths worksheets, each promising "free worksheets & answers". Underneath those hubs they've shipped ~20 individual Year 1 and Year 2 worksheet pages on curriculum-aligned topics:

- Year 1: numbers to 120, partitioning tens and ones, skip counting, repeating patterns, adding/subtracting within 20, word problems ("adding and taking away stories"), equal sharing and grouping, measuring length with informal units, comparing length/mass/capacity/time, familiar shapes, giving and following directions, picture and block graphs, collecting and recording data, years/months/weeks/days/hours.
- Year 2: adding and subtracting two-digit numbers, addition/subtraction facts to 20, calendars and days between events, comparing and sorting shapes.

The pattern (one worksheet per sub-strand, year-level hub above) suggests Year 3+ pages are queued next. Worth watching whether Years 7-10 follow.

**"How I scored" student testimonial blog series.** Three new posts: IB Mathematics Analysis and Approaches HL (scored 7), HSC Chemistry (96), SACE Specialist Mathematics (A+ merit). Clearly a template they'll reuse - credibility content aimed at senior students shopping for tutors.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Worksheet-as-SEO is becoming table stakes.** Apex is now sitting on thousands of URLs and this week's build is explicitly worksheet landing pages with answers. Tuterly has `/worksheets` but if Apex's template extends to Years 7-10 we'll be out-indexed on long-tail topic queries ("year 8 maths linear equations worksheet" etc.).
2. **Nobody shipped an app or homework/practice tool this week.** No competitor launched anything resembling the snap-a-question-practise-again loop. The product category remains uncontested in this dataset.
3. **Senior-years testimonial content is the other active format** (Apex's "How I scored"). No competitor is publishing Years 7-10 maths topic guides or "common mistakes" content.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Years 7-10 maths topic guides are wide open.** No competitor published anything in this band this week, and the app's core audience is exactly here. Blog targets that map to app topics: linear equations, algebraic fractions, Pythagoras, surface area and volume, simultaneous equations, indices, probability, Cartesian geometry. Angle each as "common mistakes in [topic]" or "how to practise [topic] when you keep getting it wrong" - which also plugs directly into the app's value prop.
- **NAPLAN and Year 9 numeracy test prep** - Apex is covering primary but nobody is owning upper-secondary test prep content this week. Low-competition window.
- **Counter Apex's "how I scored" format with a Years 7-10 angle** - e.g. "How I went from 50% to 85% in Year 9 maths" framed around practising wrong questions. Fits the app story better than ATAR flex pieces.
- **Worksheet gap check:** Apex's Year 1-2 topics (patterns, partitioning, graphs) foreshadow a Year 7-10 build. Pre-empt by making sure `/worksheets` has strong Year 7-10 coverage on the Victorian curriculum sub-strands before they get there.
- **Keep an eye on Apex's next worksheet drop.** If Year 4-6 appears next week, Year 7-10 is probably two to three weeks away. That's when competitive pressure on the worksheet side goes up.
