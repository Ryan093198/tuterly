# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-06-30

## Headline

A genuinely quiet week. Across the eight competitors we successfully crawled, zero new pages were published and zero were removed. No pricing pages moved, no new subject or location pages appeared, no positioning shifts in headline copy that surfaced through URL changes. This is the second signal in a row that the larger players (Cluey, Learnmate, Apex) are in a content-hold pattern rather than actively expanding footprint - worth watching, because it opens a window for Tuterly to publish into uncontested territory before the new school term.

## Cross-competitor patterns

The dominant pattern is stasis. Nothing was published, nothing was retired. There's no new exam prep content despite us being mid-year and approaching the VCE/HSC trial period - normally a time when at least Cluey or Apex push fresh study guide material. If that absence continues another week or two, it's a real opening for Tuterly's /learn and /vce-tutoring sections.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because there's no new competitor content to react to, the opportunities this week are about gaps in their standing footprint that we don't yet cover:

1. **Scholarship test prep at scale.** We have /scholarship-test-prep as a single page. Competitors don't appear to be expanding here either, so this is a low-pressure window to build out school-specific scholarship guides (Melbourne High, MacRob, selective entry) before anyone else moves.

2. **ATAR planning tools.** /atar-planner is genuinely differentiated - no competitor in this crawl has a comparable interactive tool surfaced in their URL structure. Worth promoting harder rather than building more.

3. **Centres positioning.** /centres is a path none of the marketplace competitors (Learnmate, Apex) have. Kip McGrath and Begin Bright are the centre-based players, and Begin Bright is currently unreachable. If their site is genuinely down rather than just our fetch failing, that's worth a manual check - and an opportunity to capture searches around their brand if they're having uptime issues.

4. **Worksheets.** /worksheets remains a content moat. None of the competitors crawled this week added worksheet or downloadable resource pages. Continuing to publish here is unopposed.

## What to do next week

- If Learnmate is still showing zero new tutor profiles next week, that's a meaningful signal about their supply side and worth a closer look.
- Otherwise: use the quiet week to ship rather than react.
