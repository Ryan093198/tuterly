# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-09-15

## Headline

Apex Tuition has pushed out 21 new Year 10 maths worksheets in a single week, all under `/worksheets/`. This is a direct challenge to Tuterly's `/worksheets` surface area, and the topic coverage is unusually specific (conditional probability simulations, logarithmic scales, bivariate investigations, network diagrams). If Apex keeps this cadence, they will out-index Tuterly on Year 10 maths worksheet queries within a quarter. This is the week's one action item.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Worksheet libraries are becoming the content battleground.** Apex is now at ~1,800 URLs, most of which are worksheet pages. Tuterly's `/worksheets` is a headline product surface; Apex is scaling their equivalent aggressively and topic-by-topic. Worth checking whether Tuterly's Year 10 maths worksheet coverage matches the 21 topics Apex just shipped.

2. **Location pages remain Learnmate's growth lever.** Only Learnmate is publishing regional/suburb tutor pages. Tuterly has `/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne` and `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` but no equivalent suburb or NSW city coverage. Learnmate's Parramatta/Wollongong/Central Coast push shows the template still works - it's cheap to replicate and captures long-tail intent.

3. **Everyone else is quiet.** Cluey, Tutor Doctor, EzyMaths, Kip McGrath, Alchemy, Math Minds published nothing this week. No pricing shifts, no positioning changes visible in the sitemap data.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Match Apex's Year 10 maths worksheet drop.** The 21 topics are a ready-made content brief. Tuterly can ship equivalents with proper titles and descriptions (Apex's are blank) and win on quality of the actual worksheet.
- **NSW location pages.** Tuterly's location coverage is Melbourne-only. If national expansion is on the roadmap, Parramatta/Wollongong/Central Coast and Sydney suburbs are unclaimed by most competitors and being actively taken by Learnmate.
- **Year 10 as a wedge above VCE.** Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring` is strong for Years 11-12. Apex is building Year 10 maths depth - the year where parents start booking VCE prep. A dedicated Year 10 maths landing page feeding into `/vce-tutoring` could catch intent earlier than competitors do.
- **Alternative pages.** Tuterly has `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate's aggressive expansion, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is worth considering.
