No pricing pages moved, no new landing pages, no fresh content clusters. This is a good window to ship rather than react - if anything shifts next week, we'll be positioned to respond from a stronger content base.

## Cross-competitor patterns

The obvious pattern is stillness - four competitors we can see clearly (Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath, EzyMaths) plus the larger players (Learnmate, Apex, North Shore) all published nothing new. Mid-August tends to be a low-publishing window in the tutoring calendar (schools mid-term 3, no exam urgency yet), so this is consistent with seasonal behaviour rather than a signal of strategic retreat.

Three simultaneous crawl failures (Begin Bright, Alchemy, Math Minds) is more than usual - if this repeats next week, worth checking whether it's our crawler or whether those sites have made structural changes.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's nothing new to react to, the useful lens this week is standing gaps we already know about:

1. **ATAR planner is a genuine differentiator.** None of the competitors surfaced this week have an equivalent interactive tool. Worth doubling down on visibility of `/atar-planner` in the lead-up to Term 4, when Year 12 anxiety spikes.

2. **Scholarship test prep** - Cluey, Tutor Doctor and Kip McGrath don't have a dedicated scholarship page in the same way we do at `/scholarship-test-prep`. North Shore does address this space heavily (it's core to their positioning), so if we want to compete there directly, that's the page to benchmark against - not the online-first players.

3. **Worksheets as a free acquisition channel** - none of the marketplaces (Learnmate, Apex) run a `/worksheets` equivalent. This is a moat we should keep widening rather than defending.

4. **Comparison pages** - our `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative` pages remain unopposed. Neither Cluey nor Tutor Doctor has published a counter-comparison page this week or historically visible in our crawls. Consider adding an `/ezymaths-alternative` or `/kip-mcgrath-alternative` if the search demand justifies it - both had zero activity this week, suggesting neither is investing in defensive content.

5. **Melbourne-specific pages** - our geo pages (`/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne`, `/vce-tutoring`) remain a structural advantage against the national players. No competitor moved on Melbourne geo content this week.

## Recommended action

Use this quiet week to ship rather than watch. Priorities: refresh `/atar-planner` for the Term 4 audience, and audit whether we should stand up one more `-alternative` page against a competitor that clearly isn't defending the space. Re-check Begin Bright, Alchemy, and Math Minds next week - if the fetch failures persist, we need alternate monitoring for those three.
