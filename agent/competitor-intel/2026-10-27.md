# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-27

## Headline

Cluey appears to have re-exposed roughly 1,290 URLs this week - but reading the sample, this is almost certainly a sitemap re-indexing event rather than a real content push. The URLs include old NAPLAN 2023/2024 pages, staging artefacts marked "ToBeDeleted", and dead 404s. The genuinely interesting signal buried in there is Cluey's **grandparent-targeted acquisition page** offering 20% off tutoring for grandchildren - a persona segment nobody else in this set addresses.

## Cluey Learning

The 1,290 "new" URLs are mostly historical pages reappearing in the sitemap. Filtering out noise, the pages worth noting:

- **/grandkids** - a dedicated landing page selling tutoring to grandparents paying for their grandchildren, with a 20% discount hook. This is a distinct buyer persona (payer ≠ decision-maker ≠ student) and is a smart play given Australian grandparent childcare spend.
- **/schools** - an Australian schools directory/guide. Overlaps directly with Tuterly's /directory.
- **/year-7** - a "starting high school" guide for parents. Transition-year content aimed at anxious parents.
- **/free-maths-worksheets** and **/maths-quiz-for-parents** - lead-magnet style interactive content. The parent maths quiz ("are you as smart as your child?") is a shareable hook.
- **/win-tutoring-every-week** - a competition offering a year of free tutoring as a lead-gen mechanism.
- City-based **school holiday activities** pages (Brisbane, Gold Coast - though titles are mis-tagged as Sydney/Canberra, suggesting template sloppiness).
- A **/request-call** page - indicates they're pushing more leads toward phone consults rather than self-serve signup.

Several staging URLs ("swag-tutors-staging", "swag-customers", "partners-staging...ToBeDeleted") are leaking into their sitemap - minor hygiene issue, not strategic.

## Learnmate

Only two genuinely new pages, both narrow subject landing pages:
- **/tutors/online-vce-economics-tutors/**
- **/tutors/online-vce-accounting-tutors/**

They're continuing to expand their programmatic VCE subject coverage one subject at a time. Both are online-only variants. Given Tuterly's /vce-tutoring page is a single hub, Learnmate is winning on long-tail subject-specific search.

## North Shore Coaching College

Removed 234 URLs - the entire **/news/** blog archive is gone, including scholarship announcements, exam prep posts, parenting advice, and award pages dating back to 2020. Either a site restructure, a migration, or they've abandoned content marketing. Worth watching next week to see if these reappear under a new path or stay dead. If dead, there's a window to pick up long-tail queries around NSW selective schools, scholarship prep, and AMC that they used to rank for.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Programmatic subject pages are the dominant growth tactic.** Learnmate adds subject-by-subject VCE pages. Cluey maintains deep year-level and NAPLAN-year pages. Tuterly's /vce-tutoring is a single page competing against dozens of narrower pages per subject.
2. **Lead magnets and quizzes are back.** Cluey's "maths quiz for parents" and worksheet downloads are classic top-of-funnel plays. Tuterly has /worksheets - worth checking whether we treat it as a lead magnet or just a resource dump.
3. **Persona-specific landing pages** (grandparents, Year 7 starters, "request a call") suggest Cluey is segmenting its funnel more aggressively than everyone else.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Grandparent persona** - no Melbourne competitor touches this. A /grandparents page would be a cheap test, especially tied to the ATAR planner or VCE tutoring.
- **Year-level transition guides** - Cluey owns /year-7. Tuterly has no equivalent "starting Year 7 / Year 11 / VCE" parent guides, which are high-intent search queries for anxious parents in Nov-Jan.
- **Programmatic VCE subject pages** - the gap vs Learnmate keeps widening. One page per VCE subject (Methods, Specialist, Chem, Bio, Eng, Econ, Accounting, etc.) linked off /vce-tutoring would be a focused build.
- **North Shore's abandoned content** - if their scholarship/selective content stays gone, we have a window on NSW selective and AMC-adjacent queries. Less relevant for Melbourne-focused Tuterly, but /scholarship-test-prep could absorb some of it.
- **Interactive lead magnet** - a "guess your child's ATAR" or "VCE study load quiz" would be more Tuterly-flavoured than Cluey's generic maths quiz, and ties naturally to the ATAR planner.
