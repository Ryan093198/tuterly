## Patterns

The only pattern is the absence of one. Four competitors are unreadable (Begin Bright, Alchemy, North Shore, Math Minds) and the other eight show zero deltas. If this repeats next week, the diff pipeline itself needs investigation - it's statistically unlikely that eight independent sites all published nothing in the same seven-day window.

## Standing opportunities (unchanged from prior weeks, flagged again given quiet week)

A quiet competitor week is a good time to push on gaps rather than react:

- **Learnmate's tutor-profile scale (6,430 URLs) vs our `/directory`** - they're winning on sheer indexed profile count. Worth auditing whether our directory pages are being indexed at parity and whether individual tutor pages carry enough unique content to rank.
- **Success Tutoring's location footprint (8,149 URLs)** - almost certainly a suburb-page strategy. We have `/best-tutoring-melbourne` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` but no granular suburb coverage. A templated but genuinely useful suburb layer (local schools, travel times, local tutor counts from our directory) would be defensible.
- **No competitor appears to have an ATAR planning tool** - our `/atar-planner` remains a differentiator. Worth making sure it's surfaced more prominently in the `/parents` and `/vce-tutoring` journeys.
- **`/worksheets` and `/sample-report`** are also competitor-gap assets. These are the kinds of pages that get linked and shared; a weekly addition to the worksheet library would compound.

## Recommended actions this week

1. Have engineering verify the diff/crawl pipeline is actually detecting new URLs - eight consecutive zeros is suspicious.
2. Fix or replace the sitemap-based crawl for Begin Bright, Alchemy, North Shore and Math Minds. Four blind spots out of twelve is too many.
3. Use the quiet news week to ship a suburb-level page or two and expand the worksheet library, rather than reactive content.
