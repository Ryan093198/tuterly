# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-11-17

## Headline

Apex Tuition is aggressively building a free maths worksheet library, publishing 19 Year 3 maths worksheets this week on top of new Year 4 and Year 5 worksheet hub pages. They are directly colonising the "free maths practice" territory that sits right next to Tuterly's snap-and-practise product. This is the most important move to watch - they are the only competitor this week investing in structured, curriculum-aligned maths practice content.

## Per competitor

- **Primary maths worksheet build-out.** New Year 4 and Year 5 worksheet hub pages ("Free Worksheets & Answers"), plus 19 individual Year 3 worksheet pages covering a full curriculum spread: addition/subtraction with place value, mental strategies, inverses, multiplication facts for 3/4/5/10, arrays, numbers beyond 10,000, dollars and cents, time to the minute, metric units, maps and plans, frequency tables, graphs, chance experiments, statistical investigations, modelling. First-person high-achiever narratives spanning four state/international curricula. Notably one is an IB HL Maths post - angle-worth noting for senior maths positioning.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Worksheet libraries are the active battleground.** Apex is the only mover but they are moving hard. Nobody else shipped maths content this week.
- **Programmatic city/subject landing pages may be falling out of favour.** Learnmate's mass deletion of "1-on-1 X tutor" pages is the week's loudest signal. If they have decided these pages hurt more than they help (Google Helpful Content, thin content), that's a useful data point for how Tuterly scales its own `/tutoring-prices-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne` style pages - depth over breadth.
- **The rest of the market is quiet.** Five established competitors made zero changes. Content velocity in this category is low, which means consistent weekly publishing from Tuterly compounds fast.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Beat Apex to Years 7-10 maths worksheets.** Apex is working upward from Year 3. Tuterly's product is Years 7-10 maths; the worksheet library at `/worksheets` should be racing to own that band with topic-specific pages (linear equations, Pythagoras, surds, quadratics, probability, trig ratios) before Apex gets there. Each worksheet page is a natural landing spot for "snap a question like this and practise fresh ones in the app."
2. **Common-mistakes content as a product-aligned blog angle.** Tuterly's whole premise is practising questions students got wrong. No competitor is publishing "common mistakes in Year 9 algebra" or "the five errors examiners see in Year 10 trigonometry" style guides. This is a defensible content lane that ties directly to the app's value proposition.
3. **Year 7-10 topic guides with worked examples.** Apex's Year 3 pages are curriculum-descriptor-aligned (place value, inverses, arrays). Mirror that structure for Victorian/NSW Years 7-10 maths - one page per curriculum strand.
4. **"How I got a 40+ in VCE Methods / Specialist" first-person posts.** Apex's new testimonial format is working for them across four curricula but they have not yet done VCE Methods or Specialist Maths. Tuterly is Melbourne-based and maths-focused - this is a natural fit and plays to the VCE tutoring page.
5. **Watch Learnmate's cleanup before copying their old playbook.** If Tuterly is considering spinning up more suburb- or subject-level landing pages, Learnmate just told you they regret doing 5,000 of them. Keep the ones Tuterly has deep and useful; don't scale thin.
6. **No competitor has a student-facing app.** Still true this week. The iPhone app remains a differentiated wedge - no one else is publishing anything that acknowledges mobile practice tools exist.
