## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Almost total content freeze.** Of nine competitors we could read, only Kip McGrath shipped anything, and it was a location page rather than editorial or product. Mid-August is deep in Term 3, so this is consistent with an operational focus (delivering to enrolled students) rather than acquisition content.
2. **Physical footprint vs. digital marketplace.** Kip McGrath's only move this year keeps being new centres. It underlines the gap in the market Tuterly sits in — no competitor is aggressively building out online tooling, planners, or parent-facing resources right now.
3. **Learnmate holding steady.** For a marketplace with 6,400+ URLs to have zero delta in a week is unusual. If it repeats next week, we should verify their sitemap is being fully crawled before drawing conclusions about their tutor supply.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Localised landing pages.** Kip McGrath's Sans Souci page is a plain template — centre name, "English and Maths," free assessment. Tuterly's `/best-tutoring-melbourne` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` pages could be extended into a set of Melbourne suburb-specific pages that are richer than Kip's (real tutor availability, transparent pricing, VCE-specific). This is a low-effort way to compete for the same local intent Kip is farming.
- **Free-assessment counter-offer.** Every Kip centre page leads with "book a free assessment." Tuterly doesn't have an obvious equivalent hook on `/parents`. A free diagnostic — even a lightweight version tied to `/atar-planner` or `/sample-report` — would give parents the same "try before you commit" reassurance without needing a physical visit.
- **Fill the content vacuum.** With competitors publishing almost nothing, any well-timed Term 3 / VCE trial-exam content from Tuterly will have an unusually clear field. Neither Cluey nor Learnmate ran anything new this week to compete for parent attention.
- **Marketplace transparency.** If Learnmate's stasis is real, and given they're our closest structural competitor, this is a moment to double down on things a marketplace can uniquely offer that Kip/Cluey can't: `/directory` depth, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` transparency, and tutor-side acquisition via `/tutors`.
