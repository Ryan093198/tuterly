# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-08-20

## Headline

**North Shore Coaching College is the only competitor that moved this week**, and their pattern is worth watching: they're already publishing Term 3 newsletters (NSW + VIC) and building out a full WA Year 12 calendar for the back half of 2026, including an ATAR "Accelerated Success Program" and October holiday intensive. Everyone else - Cluey, Tutor Doctor, EzyMaths, Kip McGrath, Learnmate, Apex, Alchemy, Success, Tutoring for Excellence, Math Minds - published nothing new.

The strategic read: North Shore treats seasonal ATAR/exam prep as a productised calendar of programs with dedicated landing pages, tied to state and term. Tuterly's ATAR planner is the closest equivalent but is a single tool rather than a rolling set of dated program pages.

## Per-competitor findings

### North Shore Coaching College
Six new pages, one removed. The removed page (WA July holiday program) rolling off is normal churn. What matters is the new stack:

- `/news/nsw-term-3-2026-newsletter/` and `/news/vic-term-3-2026-newsletter/` - parent-facing term newsletters used as content and lead capture.
- `/news/wa-2026-yr-12-atar-accelerated-success-program/` - a named ATAR program aimed at WA Year 12s. This is a positioning move: "accelerated success" branding around final-year ATAR prep.
- `/news/wa-2026-term-4-calendar/` - forward-published Term 4 calendar for WA.
- `/news/wa-2026-oct-holiday-program/` plus a mirrored URL under `/tutoring/exam-preparation/wa-2026-oct-holiday-program/` - the same October holiday intensive indexed under both news and exam-prep silos.

The WA focus is notable. It suggests either a new market push or a strong existing presence they're doubling down on. The dual-URL structure for the October program (news + exam-prep) is deliberate cross-linking to catch both browse paths.

### Cluey, Tutor Doctor, EzyMaths, Kip McGrath, Learnmate, Apex, Alchemy, Tutoring for Excellence, Math Minds
No new or removed URLs this week. Learnmate remains the largest footprint at 6,424 URLs (marketplace pages) and Alchemy second at 2,693. Success Tutoring's crawlable footprint is still just 2 URLs, which continues to look like a crawl-blocking or JS-rendering issue rather than a real site.

### Begin Bright
Fetch failed again this week - no data. Worth checking manually if this persists another week.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Quiet week industry-wide.** Only one of twelve competitors shipped anything. This is consistent with the tail end of Term 3 when marketing teams are executing rather than publishing.
2. **Seasonal calendar pages as an asset class.** North Shore is the only one leaning into dated, state-specific program pages (Term 4 calendar, October holidays, Year 12 accelerated program). No other competitor is running this play visibly.
3. **ATAR/Year 12 branding.** "Accelerated Success Program" is a stronger product name than the generic "VCE tutoring" / "HSC prep" language most competitors use.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Turn `/atar-planner` into a program calendar, not just a tool.** North Shore's WA stack shows there's appetite for "here is exactly what happens in Term 4 and the October holidays." Tuterly could publish a VIC-focused Term 4 / October holiday ATAR sprint page and beat North Shore on the Melbourne market where they appear less focused.
- **Term newsletters as a parent channel.** North Shore's `/news/vic-term-3-2026-newsletter/` is a light-touch parent-facing page. Tuterly's `/parents` section could host a Melbourne-specific term newsletter - useful trust signal and repeatable content.
- **Named programs beat generic tutoring pages.** Consider a branded VCE final-term product (e.g. a named October holiday intensive) rather than relying on `/vce-tutoring` alone. It gives you an asset to point paid traffic at with clear scope and dates.
- **WA gap is not our problem, but the template is.** North Shore's URL pattern (`/tutoring/exam-preparation/[state]-[year]-[term]-[program]/`) is a clean model. Tuterly could apply the same to VIC-specific exam prep landing pages under `/vce-tutoring` or `/online-tutoring-melbourne`.
- **Competitor comparison pages remain a differentiator.** Tuterly already has `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Neither Cluey nor Tutor Doctor published anything this week to respond to, and neither runs comparable pages against Tuterly. That asymmetry is still live.
