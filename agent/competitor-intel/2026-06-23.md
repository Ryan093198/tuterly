# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-06-23

## Headline

EzyMaths Tutoring appears to have rebuilt or re-exposed a large chunk of its site this week - 198 new URLs detected, including a substantial library of free Year 11 HSC Advanced Maths topic pages (functions, calculus, trigonometry, logarithms, polynomials, statistics) and a VCE past papers hub. This is a meaningful content moat play in maths that overlaps directly with our /worksheets and /vce-tutoring territory, and it's worth a closer look.

## EzyMaths Tutoring

The big mover this week. 198 new URLs surfaced, which looks more like a site relaunch or sitemap re-crawl than 198 fresh posts, but the shape of what's now public is the interesting part:

- **Free topic-level maths content under `/year-11-hsc-advanced-maths/`**: dedicated pages for absolute value functions, piecewise functions, exponential/logarithmic/polynomial functions, calculus, trigonometry, mean/expected value, variance and standard deviation. These are study-resource pages aimed at HSC students but the maths content is curriculum-agnostic enough to rank for VCE search intent too.
- **`/past-papers/vce-maths/`** is now live - direct overlap with VCE-aligned families we target.
- **Commercial/funnel pages** worth noting: `/payment-pricing/`, `/refer-a-friend/`, `/for-tutors/`, `/for-schools/`, `/ezy-impact/` (looks like a social impact / scholarship angle), plus split success stories for clients vs tutors.
- A `/paypal-unsuccessful/` page suggests PayPal is a live payment rail for them.

Opportunities for us:
- We have /worksheets and /vce-tutoring but no equivalent topic-by-topic free study pages. A VCE Methods/Specialist topic library mirroring their HSC structure would be a defensible content build.
- `/for-schools/` and an impact page are both angles Tuterly doesn't have publicly - relevant if we want B2B or grant-funded routes.
- Their split of client vs tutor success stories is a simple format we could copy on /parents and /tutors.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Quiet week across the board except EzyMaths.** Eight of twelve competitors had literally zero changes. Either the sector is in winter holiday lull or our usual movers (Cluey, Learnmate, Alchemy) are between content cycles.
2. **The free-topic-content moat is the live battleground in maths.** EzyMaths joins the pattern we already see implicitly with Cluey and Learnmate of building large, evergreen, topic-keyed study pages. Our /worksheets path is the closest analogue but the topic-page format (one URL per concept, with explainer + examples) is where competitors are pulling ahead in organic reach.
3. **HSC-first content, VCE as afterthought.** EzyMaths' new library is explicitly HSC-labelled. For a Melbourne-anchored brand like Tuterly there's a clear gap to own the equivalent VCE Methods / Specialist / Further topic library under Australian-curriculum naming - something /vce-tutoring currently doesn't deliver at concept depth.
4. **"For schools" is becoming standard furniture.** EzyMaths now has it; Cluey and Tutor Doctor already do. Tuterly doesn't have a /schools or /partnerships page - worth considering even as a stub if B2B is on the roadmap.

## Recommended next moves

- Audit EzyMaths' new HSC topic pages in detail (depth, format, internal linking) before they start ranking - decide within two weeks whether to build a VCE equivalent under /learn or /worksheets.
- Add a VCE past papers landing page; this is now table stakes.
- Consider splitting tutor and parent/student testimonials, mirroring EzyMaths' structure.
- Begin Bright failed to fetch - re-check next week before assuming it's a stable signal.
