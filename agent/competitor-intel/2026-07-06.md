## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **The market is quiet.** Eleven of twelve tracked competitors published zero new pages this week. Mid-year (early July) is a natural lull between Term 2 and Term 3, and it shows.
2. **Competitions as centre-launch marketing.** North Shore is the only mover, and they're using a giveaway to drive interest into a specific centre. Physical-centre competitors appear to lean on promotional hooks tied to catchment areas rather than content.
3. **No content-marketing activity from the big players.** Cluey, Learnmate, Alchemy, and Apex - the four with the largest URL footprints - all sat still. No new subject guides, no new location pages, no pricing changes visible.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Promotional lead-gen.** Tuterly has no equivalent to a giveaway or free-trial hook on the site. A "free first session" or "win a term of tutoring" style entry point could sit naturally alongside `/parents` and would be cheap to test. North Shore's template gives us a reference for structure (landing page + T&Cs + location variants).
- **Location-specific landing pages.** North Shore's Chatswood Chase page reinforces a pattern we already partially exploit (`/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne`, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne`). Suburb-level Melbourne pages would extend this and outflank Sydney-centric competitors on local intent.
- **Fill the quiet window.** With competitors publishing nothing, any new content Tuterly ships in the next fortnight faces minimal competing signal. Priority candidates: expanding `/vce-tutoring` into subject-specific children (VCE Methods, VCE English, VCE Chemistry), and building out the `/directory` if underdeveloped.
