# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-06-04

## Headline

A quiet week, with one signal worth acting on: **Learnmate is extending its per-subject VCE landing page strategy into Accounting and Economics**. Both are subjects where Tuterly has no dedicated landing page despite a strong VCE positioning (/vce-tutoring is a single hub). If Learnmate keeps building out the long tail of VCE subjects, they'll own the "VCE [subject] tutor" search intent that Tuterly's single hub page can't realistically capture.

## Per-competitor findings

### Learnmate
Two new pages, both fitting an existing template:
- `/tutors/online-vce-accounting-tutors/`
- `/tutors/online-vce-economics-tutors/`

Same title pattern ("Hire the Best Online VCE [Subject] Tutors"), same meta copy. This looks like a systematic rollout rather than one-off content - expect more VCE subject pages in coming weeks (Legal Studies, Business Management, Further Maths are likely candidates). The "online" framing in the URL is notable: they're positioning these as remote-first, which overlaps directly with Tuterly's /online-tutoring-melbourne angle.

### Apex Tuition Australia
One new blog post: "How Does the WACE Work? A Complete Guide for WA Parents and Students." WA-focused, which is outside Tuterly's Melbourne/VCE core. Worth noting only as evidence Apex continues to invest in state-curriculum explainer content (we've seen ATAR/HSC-style guides from them before). Tuterly's /atar-planner is the closest equivalent but is a tool, not a long-form parent guide.

### North Shore Coaching College
One new page: `/news/wa-2026-personal-presentation-interview-skills-2/`. Title and description didn't render, but the slug suggests interview prep content tied to WA selective/scholarship entry for 2026. Adjacent to Tuterly's /scholarship-test-prep. Worth keeping an eye on if they build out a 2026 interview-prep series - that's an angle Tuterly's scholarship page doesn't currently address (it's test-focused, not interview-focused).

### Cluey Learning, Tutor Doctor, EzyMaths, Kip McGrath
No new or removed pages. All four have been static for the week. Cluey at 1,703 URLs and Tutor Doctor at 389 remain the two direct comparison points for the /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative pages - no content changes there to react to.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Per-subject VCE/HSC/WACE landing pages are the dominant content play.** Learnmate is the most aggressive here, but Apex's WACE guide fits the same intent: capture state-curriculum search traffic at the subject or qualification level. Tuterly's /vce-tutoring is a single hub competing against marketplaces that have dozens of per-subject pages.

2. **State-specific content is creeping in.** Two of the three new pages this week are WA-focused (Apex WACE, North Shore WA interview skills). Not a Melbourne threat directly, but a reminder that competitors are expanding geographically while Tuterly's positioning is Melbourne-anchored.

3. **Very low publishing velocity overall.** Four out of seven successfully-crawled competitors shipped zero changes. The marketplaces (Learnmate, Apex) are the only ones actively expanding the surface area of their site; the franchise/centre brands (Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath, EzyMaths) are static.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Build out per-subject VCE pages before Learnmate locks in the search intent.** Priority candidates based on what Learnmate just added: Accounting and Economics. Both are subjects where Tuterly tutors likely exist but have no dedicated landing page. Methods (Maths Methods), Specialist Maths, English, Chemistry, Physics, Biology, Legal Studies and Business Management would round out a defensible set.
- **Consider an interview-skills companion to /scholarship-test-prep.** North Shore is signalling demand here for 2026 entry. Tuterly's scholarship page is test-focused; adding interview prep would broaden the funnel without major new content investment.
- **Audit /vce-tutoring as a hub.** If Tuterly does ship per-subject pages, /vce-tutoring should evolve into a directory linking to them - the current single-page approach won't scale against Learnmate's structure.

Nothing this week requires an urgent response, but the Learnmate VCE-subject rollout is the trend to track over the next 2-4 weeks.
