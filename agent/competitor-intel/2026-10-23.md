# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-23

## Headline

Cluey Learning appears to have republished or restructured almost its entire site this week - 1,290 "new" URLs against a total of 1,702, with zero removals. Every sampled page returned a fetch error, so we can't read content yet, but the URL patterns point to a large content/staging reshuffle rather than a single campaign. Worth watching closely next week once pages are crawlable.

## Cluey Learning

The URL list is dominated by three signals:

- **Staging and legacy artifacts leaking into the sitemap**: multiple `*-staging-01-25-24-ToBeDeleted` and `*-staging-01-29-24-ToBeDeleted` URLs (partners, NAPLAN quiz, maths quiz, swag tutors). These shouldn't be public - a small hygiene miss on their end, but it tells us they've recently redeployed site infrastructure.
- **Lead-gen and acquisition pages**: `/win-tutoring-every-week`, `/request-call`, `/grandkids` (a new audience segment angle - targeting grandparents who fund tutoring), and a `/schools` page suggesting a renewed B2B/schools push.
- **Seasonal and evergreen SEO plays**: `/school-holiday-activities-brisbane`, `/school-holiday-activities-gold-coast`, `/free-maths-worksheets`, `/maths-quiz-for-parents`, `/naplan-2023`, `/naplan-2024`, `/year-7`, and a full state-by-state `/school-terms-dates-public-holidays-australia-2023...` set (SA, NT, WA, NSW, QLD, TAS, ACT, VIC).

**Opportunities for Tuterly:**
- The "grandkids" angle is a positioning we haven't touched - grandparents as the paying decision-maker is a real segment, especially around scholarship prep and VCE.
- Cluey is doubling down on state-level school term/holiday hub pages. We have `/worksheets` but no equivalent Victoria-specific school calendar / holiday activities resource. A Melbourne-focused "school holiday tutoring" and "VIC term dates" page would compete directly for a stream of seasonal traffic.
- `/maths-quiz-for-parents` is a lead-magnet format. Our `/parents` page could do something similar - a diagnostic quiz that routes into our directory.
- `/schools` suggests Cluey is pitching institutions. If we're not already, our `/centres` page could extend into a schools-partnership story.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **The week was quiet everywhere except Cluey.** When one competitor moves 1,290 URLs and everyone else moves zero, the signal is concentrated.
- **State- and city-level landing pages remain the dominant SEO playbook.** Cluey's holiday-activities-by-city and school-terms-by-state pages echo the model Success Tutoring and Learnmate already run at scale. Tuterly's Melbourne focus is a defensible wedge, but we're thin on Melbourne-suburb or VIC-term-cycle pages that would catch the same intent.
- **Audience segmentation is widening.** Cluey's `/grandkids` and `/schools` pages suggest the market is being sliced beyond "parent of student." Worth deciding whether Tuterly wants to add dedicated pages for grandparents (paying) and schools (B2B referrals), or stay tightly focused on parents + tutors.
