The only pattern this week is the absence of one. Zero net additions or removals across ~12,000 tracked URLs suggests a coordinated seasonal slowdown rather than any single competitor going dark. Expect a surge when term 3 planning content lands, likely late July into early August - ATAR mid-year check-ins, trial exam prep, and semester 2 subject selection are the predictable themes.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since nothing new was published, the opportunities this week are structural gaps rather than reactive ones. Comparing our path list to what we know these competitors typically rank for:

- **Trial exam / mid-year review content.** We have `/atar-planner` and `/vce-tutoring` but no visible landing page for the July-August trial exam window. Cluey, Apex and North Shore all historically own this seasonal moment. A `/vce-trial-exams` or `/mid-year-vce-check-in` page shipped this week would beat competitors to the punch given no one is publishing.
- **Subject-specific VCE pages.** `/vce-tutoring` is a single page. Apex and North Shore run subject-by-subject VCE pages (methods, specialist, chemistry, English). This is a gap worth filling while competitors are quiet.
- **Primary and early-years positioning.** Kip McGrath and Begin Bright own the primary/early-learning conversation. Tuterly's paths skew heavily secondary and VCE. If we want a slice of the primary parent market, we don't currently have a landing surface for it.
- **Comparison pages beyond Cluey and Tutor Doctor.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate's size and direct marketplace overlap, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is the most obvious missing comparison. Kip McGrath is also worth considering for the parent-of-primary audience.
- **Scholarship and selective content.** North Shore is strong here. We have `/scholarship-test-prep` but no selective-schools-specific page - a natural adjacent build.

## Recommended action this week

Use the quiet week to ship one or two of the above rather than react. The `/learnmate-alternative` page and a trial-exam seasonal page are the two highest-leverage moves given the calendar and the competitive silence. Fix the three broken crawls before next Wednesday so we don't miss the term 3 content wave when it lands.
