## Cross-competitor patterns

The whole set is static this week. When an entire cohort of 11 sites publishes nothing in a week, the usual explanations are mid-year school holidays (which lines up with late July) and teams holding content for a term 3 push. Expect a burst of new pages in the first two weeks of August as Term 3 marketing kicks in - worth watching closely then.

Structurally, the footprint sizes tell a consistent story we've seen before:

- Marketplace-style competitors (Learnmate 6.4k, Alchemy 2.7k, Apex 1.7k, Cluey 1.7k) carry huge URL counts, mostly driven by tutor profiles and subject/location combinations.
- Franchise and centre models (Kip McGrath 323, Tutor Doctor 397, North Shore 984) sit an order of magnitude smaller.
- Math Minds (66) and Tutoring for Excellence (203) are effectively brochure sites.

Tuterly sits closer to the marketplace group in intent but with a much thinner public URL surface today. That's a positioning choice, not necessarily a gap - but it's the structural reality behind SEO share.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's nothing reactive to chase this week, a few standing gaps worth using the quiet week to close:

1. **Scholarship and selective-entry content depth.** We have /scholarship-test-prep as a single page. North Shore and Apex both build out extensive selective/scholarship trees (this hasn't changed this week, but remains a durable gap). A cluster of year-level and test-specific pages under /scholarship-test-prep would be defensible.

2. **Suburb-level Melbourne pages.** Learnmate's URL count is largely powered by suburb pages. Our /best-tutoring-melbourne and /online-tutoring-melbourne cover the city-level query but not the long tail. Even 15-20 well-written suburb pages would meaningfully shift our visibility against Learnmate.

3. **Primary-years content.** Kip McGrath and Begin Bright both anchor around primary. Our top-level paths skew heavily VCE/ATAR. If we want to broaden the parent audience, a primary-focused hub under /parents or a new /primary-tutoring path is missing.

4. **Comparison pages beyond Cluey and Tutor Doctor.** We have alternative pages for two competitors. Learnmate is the more structurally similar competitor and arguably the more valuable comparison page to build next.

## Suggested action

Use this week to ship rather than react. Specifically: prioritise a Learnmate comparison page and start scoping a suburb page template, so we have inventory in place before the expected Term 3 content push from competitors in early August.
