## Cross-competitor patterns

There aren't any genuine patterns to report from this week's deltas - the dataset is essentially flat. The one structural observation worth restating: the marketplace-style competitors (Learnmate, Alchemy, Apex, Cluey) carry between 1.6k and 6.3k indexed pages, while the bricks-and-mortar brands (Kip, North Shore, Tutor Doctor) sit between 300 and 1,000. Tuterly's content surface needs to be measured against the former group, not the latter.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's no new competitor activity to react to, here are standing gaps visible from Tuterly's current top-level paths versus what's typical in the category:

- **No state coverage outside Melbourne.** Every competitor with a marketplace model carries Sydney, Brisbane, Perth and Adelaide landing pages. Our `/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` are Melbourne-locked. Even if we don't operate elsewhere, the `/online-tutoring-*` angle is defensible nationally.
- **No NAPLAN or selective-school hub.** We have `/scholarship-test-prep` but not a dedicated NAPLAN landing page. Kip, North Shore and Begin Bright all lead with NAPLAN; it's a major parent search term we're under-serving.
- **No HSC / QCE / WACE equivalents to `/vce-tutoring`.** Same point as above - even if we're Victoria-first, having thin interstate exam pages captures online enquiries.
- **No primary-school subject pages.** Competitors like Math Minds and Begin Bright build entire businesses on primary maths and English. Our `/worksheets` and `/learn` paths suggest we have the content; the question is whether we have dedicated landing pages for "primary school maths tutor" style intent.
- **No competitor comparison beyond Cluey and Tutor Doctor.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Learnmate is arguably the more direct marketplace competitor and warrants a `/learnmate-alternative` page. Kip McGrath too, given their footprint.
- **Adult / entrance-exam prep.** Kip's exit from firefighter prep aside, there's a sparse field for police, defence, nursing and UCAT-adjacent prep that no marketplace owns cleanly. Low priority but cheap to test.

## Recommendation

Skip a reactive cycle this week. Use the quiet to ship one or two of the standing gaps above - the NAPLAN hub and a `/learnmate-alternative` page would be the highest-leverage moves and require no new product work. Also worth manually checking Success Tutoring and Begin Bright outside the crawler so we're not flying blind on them next week.
