## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Publishing has essentially stopped everywhere except Learnmate.** Ten of eleven live competitors shipped zero new pages this week. Mid-year school holidays likely explain it, but Learnmate is treating this window as a content-building opportunity while everyone else coasts.
- **Learnmate's playbook is worth understanding.** Their new pages are consistently: single VCE subject, specific concept or comparison, tied to VCAA past papers, dated to the current study year. It's a template Tuterly could either compete with directly or route around by owning a different content shape (planning tools, price transparency, tutor-matching content) that Learnmate doesn't do.
- **Site-size gap keeps widening.** Learnmate 6,422 → Alchemy 2,691 → Cluey 1,703 → Apex 1,697. The top four are all content-heavy plays. Kip McGrath, Tutor Doctor and the coaching-college players are running much leaner sites and clearly compete on brand and physical presence instead.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **VCE Business Management content is a live topic.** Learnmate's new piece sits under general VCE study help. Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring` page could be extended with subject-specific hubs (Business Management, Legal Studies, Psychology) where we currently have none visible in the top-level paths. Learnmate is showing there's appetite for concept-comparison content, not just subject overviews.
- **"Theory vs theory" as a content format.** The Force Field vs Three-Step framing works because it matches how students actually get stuck. Tuterly's `/learn` and `/worksheets` sections could adopt this format across maths (e.g. differentiation rules) and English (e.g. essay structures) where we can beat Learnmate on visual clarity or interactive worksheets.
- **Own the ground Learnmate ignores.** They don't have anything equivalent to `/atar-planner`, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne`, `/sample-report`, or the competitor-alternative pages. Those are defensible Tuterly assets. Worth investing in them now while Learnmate is heads-down on subject content.
- **Study-year versioning.** Learnmate's "(2026)" title tag is a small but smart move. Tuterly's VCE and ATAR pages could be explicitly dated to signal currency - especially the ATAR planner and any VCE subject content.
