# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-07-30

**Headline: A completely quiet week across the tracked set.** No competitor published, removed, or restructured a single URL that our crawler could detect. This is unusual enough to be worth noting on its own - either the market is genuinely in a mid-winter holding pattern, or several competitors are working on unshipped changes. Either way, it is a low-noise window in which anything Tuterly publishes has an open field.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Zero publishing activity everywhere.** Seven competitors that we can see clearly all held steady. Late July is school-holiday territory in most states, so this likely reflects a seasonal lull in content and page ops, not a coordinated strategy shift.

2. **Footprint gap is enormous.** Learnmate (6,419 URLs), Cluey (1,703) and Apex (1,698) dwarf the specialist/franchise players (Kip 322, Tutor Doctor 398, EzyMaths 439, Tutoring for Excellence 203). The marketplaces are winning on breadth of indexed pages; the franchises are competing on brand and physical presence. Tuterly sits closer to the marketplace model, so Learnmate and Apex remain the more relevant benchmarks for page-scale strategy.

3. **Four competitors are effectively invisible to us** (Begin Bright fetch failure, Alchemy and Math Minds empty sitemaps, North Shore 404). If any of these matter, we should stand up a fallback - either a homepage-diff check or a small hand-curated URL list per competitor - so a quiet week is not confused with a broken crawl.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because there is no new competitor content to respond to, the opportunity this week is offensive rather than defensive. Two angles based on our own site structure vs. what the larger competitors publish at scale:

- **Suburb and subject depth.** Learnmate's URL count implies heavy programmatic coverage of tutor/subject/suburb combinations. Our /directory and /online-tutoring-melbourne pages are the equivalent surface. A quiet week from them is a good time to ship additional suburb or subject landing pages before they resume publishing.

- **Comparison pages.** We already run /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative. Given Learnmate is the largest competitor by footprint and the closest structural match to Tuterly, a /learnmate-alternative page is a visible gap. Apex is a second candidate. Neither competitor moved this week, so positioning content against them can be built without a moving target.

- **ATAR planner and worksheets.** Nothing in the tracked competitor set has equivalents to /atar-planner or /worksheets that we've seen surface as new pages. These remain differentiated assets - worth pushing harder in acquisition rather than treating as utilities.

## Recommended next step

Fix the crawl for Begin Bright, Alchemy, Math Minds and North Shore before next week's report, so a repeat of "everyone is quiet" is trustworthy rather than partially blind.
