## Cross-competitor patterns

There isn't a meaningful trend this week - it's a holiday-period lull (end of June, mid-year school break in most states), which likely explains the across-the-board inactivity. The only signal is the Learnmate pruning, which is a single data point.

One persistent pattern worth noting from the snapshot sizes: Learnmate (6,430 URLs) and Alchemy (2,691) continue to operate at a scale of content that dwarfs the rest. Cluey and Apex sit in the ~1,700 band. The branded centre operators (Kip McGrath, Tutor Doctor, EzyMaths, Begin Bright, Math Minds) all run lean sites under 500 URLs. Tuterly's competitive content surface should be benchmarked against the marketplace tier (Learnmate, Alchemy, Apex), not the franchise tier.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Capitalise on the Learnmate VCE Accounting/Economics removal.** Tuterly has `/vce-tutoring` but no evidence of per-subject VCE landing pages. If Learnmate is pulling back from these two subjects specifically, there's a window to rank for "VCE Accounting tutor" and "VCE Economics tutor" Melbourne searches with dedicated pages. Even if Learnmate is just restructuring, building out per-subject VCE pages remains a structural gap - Learnmate's broader pattern of `/tutors/online-vce-{subject}-tutors/` shows the demand exists.

2. **Sitemap reliability monitoring.** Four competitors couldn't be crawled this week. Success Tutoring and Tutoring for Excellence have repeatedly returned 415s - we should consider an alternative fetch method (HTML crawl, robots.txt parse) so we're not flying blind on them. Not urgent, but a recurring blind spot.

3. **No new competitor moves to defend against.** This is a good week to ship rather than react. The `/atar-planner` and `/sample-report` pages are differentiators none of these competitors have analogues for - worth pushing harder on those in marketing while the field is quiet.

## What I'd watch next week

- Whether Learnmate's VCE Accounting/Economics pages reappear under a new URL structure, or stay gone.
- Whether the franchise sites (Kip McGrath, Tutor Doctor) come out of their long-running stasis as Term 3 approaches.
- Begin Bright's fetch failure - if it persists, we may need to switch source.
