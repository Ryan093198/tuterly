## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Programmatic tutor-profile pages remain the dominant growth lever.** Apex, Learnmate (6.4k URLs), Success Tutoring (8.1k URLs) and Alchemy (2.7k URLs) all run heavy tutor-profile or suburb-profile footprints. The sheer URL counts suggest these pages drive long-tail SEO. Tuterly's `/tutors` and `/directory` sit in the same lane but we don't appear to be publishing at the same velocity.

2. **Suburb-level targeting is being pushed to Melbourne's outer rings.** Apex indexing Carrum Downs is a reminder that the inner-Melbourne battle (Hawthorn, South Yarra, etc.) is saturated and the real whitespace is outer-south-east and outer-west. Our `/best-tutoring-melbourne` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` pages are singular - we could consider suburb-expansion variants.

3. **Nobody is publishing meaningful editorial content.** Zero blog or guide changes across the entire set this week. Reinforces the opportunity around `/learn`, `/worksheets`, `/atar-planner` and `/sample-report` - these are differentiated utility pages competitors aren't matching.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **VCE tutor profile velocity.** Apex is drip-publishing VCE profiles with suburb modifiers. If our `/vce-tutoring` and `/tutors` hubs aren't generating individual profile pages at a similar cadence, we're ceding long-tail VCE + suburb queries. Worth checking our current publishing rate against Apex and Learnmate.
- **Suburb expansion for Melbourne pages.** Consider child pages under `/best-tutoring-melbourne` or `/online-tutoring-melbourne` for the outer suburbs competitors are now targeting (Carrum Downs-style).
- **Utility-page moat.** `/atar-planner`, `/worksheets`, `/sample-report`, `/scholarship-test-prep` have no equivalents from any competitor this week - these remain genuine differentiators and are worth promoting more prominently on the parents/tutors landing pages.
- **Centres positioning.** Nothing from Kip McGrath or Begin Bright this week to react to, but our `/centres` page has no direct comparable from the pure-marketplace competitors - potential wedge against the franchise brands.
