# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-07-09

## Headline

Learnmate has quietly pulled its entire QCE and SACE tutor landing page set, plus most of its IB tutor page and its main `/find-a-tutor/` hub. This looks like a deliberate retreat from Queensland and South Australia interstate SEO, and possibly a restructure of their core tutor-discovery flow. If confirmed over the next few weeks, it's a meaningful positioning shift from Australia's largest marketplace competitor - and an opening in QCE/SACE that no one else in this set covers.

## Per-competitor findings

### Learnmate
30 URLs removed, zero added. The removals cluster tightly:

- **Entire QCE tutor set gone**: accounting, biology, chemistry, English, general maths, legal studies, maths methods, physics, psychology, specialist maths.
- **SACE tutor set being pulled**: accounting, biology, chemistry visible in the sample; likely more in the remaining 15 removals not shown.
- **IB tutors page removed** (`/tutor/ib-tutors/`).
- **`/find-a-tutor/` removed** - this is (or was) a core navigation destination, not a long-tail page.

Two readings: either Learnmate is consolidating to focus on VCE/HSC where they have real supply, or they're mid-restructure and these URLs will reappear under new paths. The loss of `/find-a-tutor/` suggests the latter is plausible, but we should watch next week. Either way, they are not currently ranking for QCE/SACE/IB subject terms.

## Cross-competitor patterns

It is an unusually quiet week. Every competitor whose site we could read is either flat or, in Learnmate's case, subtracting. No new subject pages, no new location pages, no new pricing pages, no new comparison pages. This is consistent with mid-year school holidays - competitors are not shipping content right now.

The only real signal is Learnmate's contraction, and it's a one-sided one.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **QCE and SACE gap is now wide open.** Learnmate was the only competitor in this set with meaningful QCE and SACE subject landing pages, and they've just removed them. Tuterly's current footprint is Melbourne/VCE-focused (`/vce-tutoring`, `/best-tutoring-melbourne`, etc.). If interstate expansion is on the roadmap, QCE maths methods, QCE chemistry, and the SACE sciences are momentarily uncontested by the marketplace player most likely to reclaim them. Even a lightweight set of QCE/SACE subject pages would face little competition today.

2. **IB tutoring is also uncovered.** Learnmate's `/tutor/ib-tutors/` is gone. Nobody else in the tracked set has a dedicated IB page. Tuterly doesn't either. IB families are a high-value niche in Melbourne (Wesley, Tintern, MLC, Carey all run IB). A `/ib-tutoring` or `/ib-tutoring-melbourne` page would sit naturally alongside `/vce-tutoring`.

3. **Scholarship test prep positioning.** Tuterly already has `/scholarship-test-prep`. None of the competitors touched this space this week and, based on their stable sitemaps, none appear to have dedicated equivalents. Worth reinforcing while the category is uncontested.

4. **`/find-a-tutor/` gap on Learnmate.** If Learnmate's core tutor-discovery URL is genuinely broken (not just moved), their organic traffic to the tutor directory will suffer. Tuterly's `/directory` and `/tutors` pages should be in good shape and internally linked - now is a good week to double-check they're indexable and fast.

## Watch list for next week

- Does Learnmate's `/find-a-tutor/` reappear, or is this a permanent architectural change?
- Do the removed QCE/SACE pages get replaced with a consolidated interstate hub, or are they gone for good?
- Get Begin Bright, Alchemy, North Shore and Math Minds back online in the crawler - four errors in one week is too many to keep ignoring.
