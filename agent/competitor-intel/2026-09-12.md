## Cross-competitor patterns

Four of twelve competitors (Begin Bright, Alchemy, North Shore, Math Minds) are effectively invisible to our current monitoring, and Success Tutoring is only partially visible. That's a third of the set. Before drawing conclusions from "quiet weeks" we should shore up collection - otherwise we risk missing exactly the kind of launches (new suburb pages, new pricing tiers, new subject verticals) these reports exist to catch.

The one real pattern in the working data: everyone is sitting still. No back-to-Term-4 campaign pages, no VCE-final-push landers, no scholarship-season content despite this being the natural window for it. Either they're all publishing off-sitemap (paid landing pages, campaign microsites) or the sector genuinely isn't pushing new web content right now.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because there are no new competitor pages to react to, the useful lens this week is structural gaps between our top-level paths and theirs:

- **Scholarship test prep timing.** We have `/scholarship-test-prep` live. None of the competitors shipped anything on this topic this week despite it being the seasonal moment. If we haven't already, a push on this page (fresh content, ads, outreach) has an uncontested field.
- **ATAR planner is still a differentiator.** No competitor has shipped a tool-style page recently. `/atar-planner` remains a distinctive asset - worth linking more aggressively from `/vce-tutoring` and `/parents`.
- **Worksheets as a moat.** `/worksheets` is a content type none of the tracked competitors appear to be building out. If Learnmate's 6,428 URLs are mostly profile pages, our worksheet library is a genuinely different content strategy and worth expanding while they're not looking.
- **Comparison pages.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate is arguably our closest structural competitor (marketplace model, large footprint), a `/learnmate-alternative` page is the obvious next comparison to build.
- **Centres angle.** `/centres` is a category the tutoring-centre players (Kip McGrath, Begin Bright, North Shore) all occupy but none refreshed this week. Worth auditing whether our centres page positions clearly against the franchise-centre model.
