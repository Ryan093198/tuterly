## Cross-competitor patterns

There is no pattern this week beyond stillness. One observation worth holding on to: across roughly 22,500 tracked URLs from eleven competitors, not a single new Years 7-10 maths topic guide, mistake explainer, or test prep page went up. This is consistent with what we've seen - the general tutoring marketplaces publish operational and SEO content (term calendars, suburb pages, tutor profiles), not actual maths pedagogy. The maths-content vacuum in this competitive set remains Tuterly's clearest content opening.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's nothing to react to, here are standing opportunities the quiet week reinforces:

- **Maths topic guides for Years 7-10.** No competitor in the set is producing this. A library under `/learn` covering things like integer operations, linear equations, Pythagoras, surds, indices, probability, and simultaneous equations - each with worked examples and the common mistakes students make - would sit in open territory and feed directly into the app's "snap the question you got wrong" loop.
- **Topic-level "common mistakes" pages.** Nobody is covering these. They map tightly to the app's core value prop and are highly linkable from inside the app itself.
- **NAPLAN Year 7 and Year 9 maths prep.** Not seeing any competitor investment here this week or in recent weeks. A dedicated hub would be defensible.
- **State term calendars.** North Shore keeps milking these. If we care about parent-intent search traffic for `/parents`, a cleaner Victorian-focused version with a planning angle (e.g. "when to start catching up before Term 1") would outrank a bare calendar page.
- **EzyMaths visibility.** They're actively blocking crawlers. Worth a manual browse to see if they've launched anything app-like, since they're the closest thing to a direct product competitor.

## What I'd do next

Nothing this week requires a defensive move. Use the quiet to ship one or two Years 7-10 maths topic guides on `/learn` - every week competitors don't publish maths content is a week we can compound an unanswered content moat.
