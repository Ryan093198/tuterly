## Cross-competitor patterns

The only pattern worth calling out is the uniform flatness. Eight separate competitors with zero deltas in the same week suggests either a quiet pre-exam lull, or that our diffing is comparing against a very recent baseline that already absorbed their recent publishing. Worth sanity-checking the baseline date on next week's run - if we see another all-zero week, we likely have a snapshot issue rather than a market-wide freeze.

Three crawl failures in one week (EzyMaths, Begin Bright, North Shore) is also higher than usual. Begin Bright and North Shore have been intermittent before; EzyMaths returning a 403 on the sitemap specifically may mean they've added bot protection. Flagging in case we want to switch to a different fetch method for them.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Nothing new surfaced this week, so the opportunity list is unchanged from prior weeks. For the Years 7-10 maths app, the standing gaps we've previously identified in competitor content remain worth working through on Tuterly's own `/learn` section:

- Topic-by-topic Year 7-10 guides (linear equations, indices, Pythagoras, trig ratios, quadratics, probability) framed around common student mistakes - a natural fit for the "snap a wrong question, practise fresh versions" loop.
- End-of-year / start-of-year topic refreshers for Years 7-10 heading into 2027.
- Head-to-head or feature pages for any competitor homework/practice tool. We already have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`; nothing new this week justifies another one.

No competitor app launches or new practice tools were detected.

## Recommended action

Nothing urgent. Confirm the baseline is dating correctly before next week's run, and consider a manual check of EzyMaths, Begin Bright and North Shore to see whether they've shipped anything the crawler missed.
