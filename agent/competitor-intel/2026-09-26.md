# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-09-26

## Headline

Alchemy Tuition is quietly restructuring its Sydney suburb landing pages from a flat `/tutoring-{suburb}/` URL pattern to a nested `/tutoring/sydney/{suburb}/` structure. The 20 new / 20 removed pages this week are the same suburbs (Kenny Hill through Kurrajong Heights) under new URLs - this is a mid-migration snapshot, not new content. If they finish the migration, they'll end up with a much cleaner city-then-suburb hierarchy that scales to Melbourne, Brisbane, etc. Worth watching whether Melbourne suburbs appear under `/tutoring/melbourne/` in coming weeks - that would put them in direct geographic competition with Tuterly's `/best-tutoring-melbourne` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` pages.

## Per competitor

**Alchemy Tuition** - See above. The migration is alphabetical (K-suburbs this week), suggesting they're working through the full list systematically. No content or positioning changes visible in the descriptions - still leading on "15 years of experience", "award-winning", "K-12". Handling redirects during a bulk URL swap is risky; if they mess it up, they'll shed rankings temporarily.

**North Shore Coaching College** - Added eight state-level news index pages: ACT, NSW, QLD, SA, VIC, WA, plus Hong Kong and a "principal campus" hub. Titles and descriptions are blank, so these look like auto-generated taxonomy pages rather than genuine content. The HK page is the only notable signal - confirms they're actively promoting to an international/expat audience. Nothing here to react to yet, but worth checking back if they start populating the Victoria news feed with campus openings.

**Learnmate** - Removed two VCE subject tutor pages: Accounting and Economics. Given Learnmate's marketplace model, this likely means they lost enough tutors in those subjects to depublish the landing pages, or they're pruning thin pages. Either way, it's a small crack in their VCE coverage - a subject-by-subject opportunity for Tuterly's VCE positioning.

**Cluey Learning, Tutor Doctor, EzyMaths, Kip McGrath, Apex Tuition, Success Tutoring, Tutoring for Excellence, Math Minds** - No changes this week.

**Begin Bright** - Fetch failed, no data.

## Cross-competitor patterns

Quiet week overall. The only real activity is structural (Alchemy's URL migration, North Shore's taxonomy pages) rather than new content, pricing, or positioning. No competitor launched new subject pages, new location pages (in the true sense), or shifted messaging.

The Alchemy migration is the second time in recent memory a competitor has invested heavily in suburb-level landing pages. Combined with Learnmate's massive URL footprint (6,430 pages), the direction of travel in the market is clear: scale via programmatic suburb + subject pages. Tuterly's current geographic coverage is essentially three Melbourne-level pages.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **VCE Accounting and VCE Economics** - Learnmate just removed both. If Tuterly has (or can recruit) tutors in these subjects, dedicated `/vce-accounting-tutoring` and `/vce-economics-tutoring` pages could pick up demand from ex-Learnmate traffic. These are also underserved on most competitor sites.

2. **Melbourne suburb pages** - Alchemy is Sydney-only in this migration, and none of the Melbourne-focused competitors have obvious suburb-page programs running this week. A tightly-scoped set of high-value Melbourne suburb pages (Kew, Brighton, Balwyn, Glen Waverley, Doncaster) would sit in a genuinely uncontested space and reinforce Tuterly's Melbourne positioning.

3. **Watch Alchemy's next migration wave** - If `/tutoring/melbourne/{suburb}/` URLs appear in the next 2-4 weeks, that's a direct incursion. Worth setting a specific alert.

4. **International / expat angle** - North Shore's Hong Kong news hub hints at expat demand for Australian curriculum tutoring. Not a core Tuterly play, but if any tutors already work with expat families preparing to return, a small page on this could be differentiated.

Nothing urgent this week. Good week to ship rather than react.
