## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Stillness across the board.** Eight of the nine successfully-scanned competitors made zero visible changes. Either the sector is in a pre-EOFY content lull, or the early-June window (post-Term 2 mid-point, pre-July school holidays) is genuinely a publishing dead zone. Worth remembering when timing Tuterly's own launches - there is very little competing noise to fight through right now.

2. **NAPLAN content is in flux.** NAPLAN 2026 testing wrapped in March, results typically land mid-year. Learnmate pulling two NAPLAN pieces in early June is the only directional signal this week, and it points to a topic competitors are either updating or stepping back from rather than doubling down on.

3. **Three competitors have broken or unreliable sitemaps** (Begin Bright, Success Tutoring, Tutoring for Excellence). If this continues we lose visibility on roughly a quarter of the tracked set - flagging so we can adjust the scraping approach.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **NAPLAN is an open gap.** Tuterly has no `/naplan` path. Learnmate is shedding NAPLAN content; Cluey, Kip McGrath and Apex all rank for it but published nothing new this week. With results season approaching (typically August), a well-built NAPLAN parent hub - "what the bands mean", "what to do if your child underperformed", year-level prep guides - could be live and indexed before competitors refresh theirs. This is the single highest-leverage move suggested by this week's data.

- **Mid-year reporting window.** Term 2 reports land late June. None of the tracked competitors have published anything around interpreting school reports or planning a Term 3 response. Tuterly already has `/sample-report` and `/atar-planner` - a parent-facing "what to do with your child's mid-year report" piece would slot naturally between them and has no current competition in the dataset.

- **Quiet week = good launch window.** If there is anything queued (new landing pages, the centres product, a pricing update), shipping it into a silent competitive backdrop maximises share of attention.

- **Comparison page coverage.** Tuterly has `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate's URL footprint (6,329) and Alchemy's (2,691), both are sizeable competitors with no comparison page yet. Worth considering whether `/learnmate-alternative` is worth building - they are arguably the closest marketplace-model competitor.

## What to watch next week

- Whether Learnmate replaces the removed NAPLAN pages with new ones (consolidation vs retreat).
- Whether Begin Bright, Success Tutoring and Tutoring for Excellence come back online.
- Any movement from Cluey or Tutor Doctor heading into the EOFY / July holiday marketing window.
