# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-07-01

## Headline

Cluey Learning has performed a major site cleanup, dropping roughly 1,290 URLs (from a much larger footprint down to 413 live pages). This is the only meaningful movement across the tracked set this week, and the pattern of what's been removed is worth attention: legacy campaign pages, staging/draft leftovers, feedback funnels, and older subject content. Cluey appears to be consolidating around a leaner, more current site - likely ahead of a repositioning or a Google indexing cleanup. Worth watching next week for what they *add* back.

## Cluey Learning

- ~1,290 URLs removed, 0 new. Total live pages now 413.
- The removals include several staging and "ToBeDeleted" artefacts (e.g. `atar-quiz-for-parents-staging-01-29-24-ToBeDeleted`) that had been publicly crawlable - housekeeping rather than strategy.
- Genuine content losses include the year-based subject selection guides (`2023-options`, `2023-options-guide`, `2024-options`) and the parent-facing ATAR quiz. These were lead-gen tools tied to Year 10 subject selection season.
- Multiple feedback landing pages (`feedback-productreview`, `feedback-trustpilot`, `feedback-staging`) are gone - suggests they've moved review collection off-site or consolidated it.
- One notable subject page removed: `english-literary-techniques`. If this was a ranking asset for them, its disappearance is an opening.

**Opportunity for Tuterly:** The ATAR quiz for parents is now gone from Cluey. We have `/atar-planner` and `/parents` - a parent-facing "should my child do X subject" or "ATAR readiness" interactive tool could fill that vacated space directly. The `english-literary-techniques` gap is also worth a `/learn` or `/worksheets` piece.

## Learnmate

- 6 URLs removed, all HSC English variants (Advanced, Extension 1, Extension 2, English Studies) for Sydney and generic geo.
- No new pages.
- This is a small trim within a very large site (6,424 URLs total). Could be a category restructure or a response to thin/duplicate NSW English pages.

**Opportunity for Tuterly:** Learnmate remains overwhelmingly Sydney/HSC-weighted. Our Melbourne/VCE positioning (`/vce-tutoring`, `/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne`) is uncontested by them in-market. No action needed - just confirmation the geographic moat holds.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **The market is quiet.** Only two competitors moved at all, and one of them (Learnmate) trimmed six pages. No new product pages, no new landing pages, no pricing pages appearing. Mid-year is traditionally slow, but this is unusually flat.
2. **Cluey's cleanup is the only strategic signal.** They're shedding legacy season-based lead magnets (Year 10 subject selection guides for 2023 and 2024). Either they're building fresher 2026/2027 versions to launch later, or they're stepping back from that top-of-funnel play entirely. Track next week.
