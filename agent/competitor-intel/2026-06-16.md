# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-06-16

## Headline

Apex Tuition has rolled out a year-and-subject matrix of Melbourne landing pages (Years 5-10 × Maths/English/Science), 21 new URLs in one week. They're clearly going after the same Melbourne year-level intent we sit on top of with /vce-tutoring and /online-tutoring-melbourne, but extending it down into primary and middle school. We don't currently have year-specific Melbourne pages below VCE - that's the gap they're trying to fill.

## Apex Tuition Australia

21 new pages, all under `/subjects-form/`. The pattern is `year-{N}-{subject}-melbourne` for:

- **Year 5**: maths, english, science (plus a duplicate `year-5-maths-9c5ff-melbourne` that looks like a CMS slug collision)
- **Year 6**: maths, english, science
- **Year 7**: maths, english, science
- **Year 8**: maths, english, science
- **Year 9**: maths, english, science
- **Year 10**: maths, english, science

Plus two generic top-level forms: `/subjects-form/english` and `/subjects-form/maths`.

Notes:
- Titles are all the bare "Apex Tuition Australia" with no meta descriptions, suggesting these are lead-capture form pages rather than fully-built content pages. They may be enquiry funnels rather than SEO assets - worth checking how they're driving traffic to them (paid? internal links?).
- No VCE-level equivalents appeared this week. They're attacking the pre-VCE Melbourne market specifically.
- Science is being treated as a first-class subject alongside Maths and English. Tuterly's directory likely covers science tutors, but we don't have it as a marketing surface.

**Opportunity**: Mirror this with proper year-level Melbourne pages (Years 5-10, Maths/English/Science) that go further than a form - actual tutor previews from the directory, sample worksheets, pricing. Our /worksheets and /directory give us a content advantage Apex's form pages can't match.

## North Shore Coaching College

One new page: `/free-online-trial-assessment-nsw-exam-preparation/`. NSW-focused (selective/OC test prep territory), so not a direct Melbourne threat, but the "free online trial assessment" hook is interesting - it's a low-friction lead magnet tied to assessment. Our /scholarship-test-prep and /sample-report cover adjacent ground; a free trial assessment could be a stronger top-of-funnel offer than a static sample report.

## Learnmate

One removal: `/subject/vce-english/`. A marketplace de-listing its VCE English subject hub page is unusual - could be a restructure, could be a deliberate retreat. Worth a manual look next week to see if it's been replaced by a different URL or genuinely gone. If they're weakening on VCE English specifically, that's a category where we should be more aggressive (it's the highest-volume VCE subject).

## No movement this week

Cluey Learning, Tutor Doctor Australia, EzyMaths, Kip McGrath - all stable, no new or removed pages. Cluey in particular sitting still at 1,703 URLs is notable given how much content they normally push.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Year × subject × city landing pages are the active battleground.** Apex is the only one moving this week, but they're moving hard and in exactly the format that ranks for "year 8 maths tutor melbourne"-type queries. If we don't have these, we should.
2. **Science is being marketed alongside Maths and English by Apex.** Worth checking our directory inventory - do we have enough science tutors to credibly compete here, or is it a known weak spot?
3. **Lead-capture pages vs. content pages.** Apex's new URLs look thin (no meta, generic titles). Our defensible angle is depth: real tutor profiles, worksheets, transparent pricing. The competitor activity this week is volume; our response should be quality at the same URL shape.
