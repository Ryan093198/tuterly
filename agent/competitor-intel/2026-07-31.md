## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Discounting is entering the market.** North Shore's 20% new-student offer is the first overt price promotion we've picked up in recent weeks. If this spreads to Cluey or Tutor Doctor, Tuterly's price-transparency positioning (via `/tutoring-prices-melbourne`) becomes more valuable - but we may also need a response for parents comparing headline discounts against our marketplace rates.
2. **Content velocity is near zero.** With 8 of 12 competitors publishing nothing and one erroring, this is a slow week industry-wide. Consistent with school holiday / early Term 3 timing. It's a cheap window for Tuterly to publish and rank on new topics without competitive noise.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Counter-offer page or comparison angle.** North Shore's discount page is likely thin and promotional. A "North Shore vs Tuterly" or a broader "compare tutoring offers Melbourne" page would slot alongside our existing `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative` pages. Worth considering a `/north-shore-alternative` if they're advertising the 20% offer via paid channels.
- **Mid-year assessment content.** With competitors quiet and Term 3 starting, there's an open window for Tuterly to publish under `/learn` around mid-year VCE check-ins, GAT prep, and Year 11-to-12 transition planning - none of the competitors are moving on this.
- **Pull the North Shore T&Cs manually.** Before next report, capture what "20% off" actually means (number of lessons, subjects covered, in-centre only?). That determines whether it's a real threat or a headline-only offer.
