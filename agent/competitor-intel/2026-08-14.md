Either way, there's no defensive fire to fight this week, which makes it a good moment to look at the standing gaps rather than reactive moves.

## Cross-competitor patterns

The only pattern this week is the absence of one: zero net URL movement across ten successfully-crawled competitors is itself the signal. In practice this means no new landing pages, no new location or subject pages, no new comparison content, no removed pages. If this continues for another week or two, we should assume the diff-based signal is saturated and shift to periodic full-content audits (headlines, pricing, positioning copy) rather than URL-set diffs.

## Standing opportunities worth acting on while the market is quiet

Since there's nothing reactive to chase, here's what stands out when comparing our known page set to the scale of competitor footprints:

1. **Competitor-comparison coverage is thin on our side.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative` live. Given Learnmate is the largest player in the panel and a direct marketplace analogue to Tuterly, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is the most obvious gap. Apex and Alchemy would be secondary candidates.
2. **Subject-specific landing pages.** We have `/vce-tutoring` but no equivalent for the individual high-demand subjects competitors typically build around (maths methods, specialist maths, English, chemistry, physics). EzyMaths and Math Minds exist as single-subject brands - that's a defensible signal that subject-level intent is worth capturing.
3. **Primary / early-years coverage.** Kip McGrath and Begin Bright both trade heavily on primary-age tutoring. Our public paths skew ATAR/VCE/senior. No dedicated primary or "Year 3-6 tutoring" page appears in our top-level set.
4. **Location depth outside Melbourne.** Our location pages are Melbourne-anchored (`/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne`). Learnmate and Alchemy's large URL counts almost certainly come from suburb-level pages. If Tuterly is planning any interstate or suburb-level expansion, the quiet week is a good window to ship that structure.
5. **Scholarship prep is a genuine differentiator.** We have `/scholarship-test-prep`; none of the tracked competitors are known for owning this niche. Worth reinforcing with more supporting content (school-specific guides, sample questions) rather than treating as a single page.
