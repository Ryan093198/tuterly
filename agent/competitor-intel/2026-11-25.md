## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Total publishing freeze.** Nine competitors crawled, zero changes. This is unusual and probably reflects the pre-December lull before end-of-year/summer campaigns. Expect a burst in the first two weeks of December around holiday programs and Year 6-into-7 transition content - worth watching closely.
2. **No one has shipped an app or practice tool response.** Nothing in the new-URL data suggests any competitor is moving toward a snap-a-question or spaced-practice product. Our wedge is uncontested on the surface this week.
3. **Three competitors unreadable** (EzyMaths, Begin Bright, North Shore). EzyMaths 403ing the sitemap is becoming a pattern - we should set up a lightweight HTML-level fallback crawl for them specifically, since they are the most maths-focused of the three.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because there is no new competitor content to react to, the opportunities this week are about pressing into known gaps rather than chasing fresh moves:

- **Years 7-10 maths topic guides.** We still have no `/learn` sub-pages visible for the obvious high-intent topics: linear equations, Pythagoras, surds, index laws, quadratics, trigonometry basics, simultaneous equations, probability trees. Every competitor with a blog covers at least some of these; we should be the one that pairs each guide with "snap a question like this in the app."
- **Common-mistakes content.** None of the competitors crawled have a dedicated common-mistakes-per-topic library. This is directly aligned with the app's premise (students snap questions they got wrong) and would be defensible content no one else is producing.
- **Test prep for Years 7-10.** We have `/scholarship-test-prep` but nothing for NAPLAN Year 7/9 numeracy, end-of-year maths exams, or Year 10 into VCE/HSC readiness. Kip McGrath and Success Tutoring both rank for adjacent terms with thin content - beatable.
- **Homework helper angle.** No competitor is publicly positioning a homework/practice tool. We could own "stuck on a maths question?" search intent before anyone else frames it that way.
- **Deeper Success Tutoring audit.** With 7,515 URLs and no diff noise this week, it is a good week to manually map which Years 7-10 maths topics they already rank for, so our content roadmap targets their weakest pages first.

## Suggested next step

Use the quiet week to ship two or three Years 7-10 topic guides with embedded "common mistakes" sections and clear app CTAs. If December brings the expected competitor publishing surge, we want our evergreen pages indexed before theirs land.
