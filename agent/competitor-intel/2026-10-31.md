## Opportunities for Tuterly

**Selective and scholarship prep (near-term, high-intent):**
- Tuterly already has `/scholarship-test-prep`. North Shore's 2027 guides are a reminder that parents are searching *now* for next-cycle info. Consider a Victorian-focused equivalent: a "2027 Victorian Selective Entry High Schools guide" and a "2027 scholarship test calendar" page. Nothing in Tuterly's current paths covers selective entry specifically for VIC.
- The maths component of scholarship/selective tests is a natural bridge to the Tuterly app - snap a practice question, get fresh versions. Worth making that connection explicit on the scholarship page.

**Years 7-10 maths content (ongoing gap in the market):**
- No competitor published anything on Years 7-10 maths topic guides, common mistakes, or test prep this week. This remains open territory for Tuterly's blog. Topic guides tied to Victorian Curriculum Year 7-10 strands (algebra, linear equations, Pythagoras, trigonometry basics, probability) with "common mistakes students make" angles would both support SEO and double as marketing for the snap-and-practise loop.
- Nothing visible under Tuterly's current top-level paths covers per-topic maths explainers. `/learn` and `/worksheets` could host this, or a dedicated `/years-7-10-maths` hub.

**Competitor practice tools:**
- No competitor shipped a practice/homework tool or app update this week. Tuterly's iPhone app remains differentiated. Keep watching Cluey and Learnmate here - they're the most likely to move into app territory.
