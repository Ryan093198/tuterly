# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-09-20

**Headline: a genuinely quiet week.** Every competitor we successfully crawled published zero new pages and removed zero pages against last week's baseline. No pricing pages moved, no new subject or suburb landing pages appeared, no positioning shifts detected. This is unusual across a 10-competitor set and worth watching - either crawlers caught a lull between term-3/term-4 content pushes, or several sites are holding content while planning Q4 campaigns around VCE/HSC exam season.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Universal stillness.** Ten competitors, zero net page changes. Either our diffing is missing something (worth a spot-check on Cluey and Success Tutoring, the two most active publishers historically) or the market is genuinely in a pre-Q4 holding pattern.
2. **Two sitemap failures in one week** (Alchemy, Math Minds) plus one fetch failure (Begin Bright). Not a pattern yet, but if Alchemy and Math Minds stay dark for another week it may reflect real site neglect rather than config drift - potentially useful for competitive positioning against smaller operators.
3. **No visible movement on exam-season content.** With VCE written exams starting in about a month, we'd normally expect competitors to be pushing exam prep, past paper, and study planner pages. Nobody did this week.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because there are no new competitor pages to react to, this week's opportunities are about pressing existing gaps while the field is quiet:

- **Own the VCE exam run-up.** Your `/atar-planner`, `/vce-tutoring` and `/worksheets` paths are already differentiated. If competitors are silent through late September, publishing exam-week resources now (subject-by-subject exam checklists, ATAR scenario tools, past-paper walkthroughs) buys reach before anyone else moves.
- **Scholarship test season.** `/scholarship-test-prep` is a page most of this set doesn't have a strong equivalent for. Worth a content push now, ahead of 2027 selective/scholarship intake decisions.
- **Comparison pages.** You already have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate's scale (6,400+ URLs, mostly tutor profiles) and Success Tutoring's footprint (8,100+), a `/learnmate-alternative` and `/success-tutoring-alternative` page would round out the set against the biggest players. Neither published anything new this week to complicate the positioning.
- **Centre-facing content.** Your `/centres` path is a category none of the marketplace or franchise competitors address directly. No movement from them this week means no reason to hold back.

## What to watch next week

- Whether Cluey and Success Tutoring resume publishing (if yes, this was a lull; if no, something structural is happening).
- Whether Alchemy and Math Minds' sitemaps come back online.
- First signs of exam-season landing pages from any competitor - whoever moves first will set the template.
