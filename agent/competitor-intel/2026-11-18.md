## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Pre-2027 positioning starts in WA, not the east coast.** North Shore is the first to publish new-year transition content, and they've pointed it at WA. None of the Melbourne- or Sydney-facing competitors have shipped equivalent "moving into Year 8/9/10 in 2027" pages yet. That window is open for Tuterly.
2. **Content machines are idle this week.** Success Tutoring, Alchemy, Apex, Learnmate and Cluey - collectively 15,000+ URLs - published nothing new. Either they're in a pre-summer freeze or their publishing cadence is lumpier than it looks. Something to watch over the next 2-3 weeks.
3. **No competitor app or practice-tool announcements** appeared this week. Tuterly's "snap a question, get fresh versions" positioning remains uncontested in the tracked set.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Year-transition content for Years 7-10 maths.** North Shore is proving there's appetite for "moving into next year" pages right now. Tuterly could ship guides like "What Year 8 maths actually covers in Victoria" or "Year 9 to Year 10 maths: the jump students underestimate" - tied directly to the app's snap-and-practise loop. Nothing in Tuterly's current top-level paths addresses year-level transitions.
- **Common-mistake topic guides.** No competitor shipped Years 7-10 maths topic explainers this week, but North Shore's exposed exam-preparation category is a reminder they own that search territory with older content. A `/learn` sub-tree of "most common Year 8 algebra mistakes", "Year 9 linear equations - where students lose marks" etc. would feed the app's core promise and fill a gap versus our current `/learn` and `/worksheets` sections.
- **Selective and OC adjacency.** North Shore keeps investing in selective/OC content. Tuterly has `/scholarship-test-prep` but no selective-school-specific page. Worth considering whether the app's practice loop extends naturally into selective maths prep as a landing page angle.
- **EzyMaths blocking scrapers** is a small signal they may be tightening the shop. Worth a human check of their homepage and pricing next week.
