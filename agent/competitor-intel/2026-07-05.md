## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Zero net movement everywhere that resolved.** Nine competitors, zero new pages, zero removed. Given the date (early July, mid school-holiday period in most states), this is plausibly real - marketing teams paused - but I'd want to see next week's diff before assuming the tracker is healthy. If week of 2026-07-12 also shows all zeros, we likely have a caching problem, not a competitor problem.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's nothing new to react to, these are standing gaps worth acting on in a quiet week:

- **Marketplace-scale landing pages.** Learnmate and Alchemy's page counts imply they're generating subject × suburb or subject × year-level pages at scale. Tuterly's top-level paths show strong Melbourne-specific pages (`/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne`, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne`, `/vce-tutoring`) but no visible subject-level or suburb-level programmatic layer. Worth prototyping VCE-subject pages (Methods, Specialist, Chemistry, English) and inner-Melbourne suburb pages before competitors extend further.
- **Primary/early-years angle.** Kip McGrath, Begin Bright and Math Minds all sit in the primary-school and foundational-numeracy space. Tuterly's public paths lean secondary/VCE. If we want to broaden the parent audience, a primary-focused hub (reading, times tables, NAPLAN prep) would fill an obvious gap.
- **NAPLAN and selective/scholarship content.** We have `/scholarship-test-prep` but no visible NAPLAN page. Given the primary-focused competitors above, and that scholarship/selective prep season builds through Term 3, this is a timely addition.
- **Use the quiet week to harden the comparison pages.** `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative` face static competitor content - a good moment to refresh pricing tables and testimonials without worrying about them changing underneath us.
