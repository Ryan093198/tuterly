# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-02

## Headline

Alchemy Tuition has restructured its entire location-page system this week - roughly 400 old URLs retired and 400 new ones minted - moving from flat slugs like `/albury-maths-tutoring/` to a nested structure under `/maths-tutoring/[suburb]/` and `/english-tutoring/[suburb]/`. This is a deliberate push to dominate "{suburb} maths tutoring" and "{suburb} english tutoring" nationally, including deep regional coverage (Birdsville, Yeppoon, Yass, Whyalla, Apollo Bay). If Tuterly cares about being found outside metro Melbourne or wants to defend Victorian regional terms, Alchemy just planted a flag in hundreds of towns in a single week.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **National suburb-level land grab.** Alchemy is building out every town in Australia; Apex is extending from NSW into Perth/Adelaide/Brisbane. Both are betting on geo-specific landing pages as the primary acquisition channel. Tuterly's current geo pages are Melbourne-centric (`/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne`).

2. **Primary school is being productised.** Apex, historically senior-focused, launched dedicated primary tutor pages in four cities. Tuterly's top-level IA doesn't clearly surface a primary-school entry point - `/parents` and `/tutoring` are generic.

3. **Student-voice content as proof.** Apex's "How I scored X in [subject]" first-person blog format is a credibility play (and lead gen for aspiring high-ATAR students). Tuterly has `/learn` but no obvious equivalent of ex-student testimonials structured by subject and score.

4. **Everyone else is quiet.** Seven of ten reachable competitors shipped nothing. The two that moved both moved aggressively on geographic coverage - that's where the competitive heat is right now.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **VCE score-breakdown content.** Mirror Apex's WACE format with "How I got a 45 in [VCE subject]" posts under `/learn`. Tuterly already owns the VCE angle (`/vce-tutoring`, `/atar-planner`) and has credibility assets Apex doesn't (the ATAR planner itself). This would be a differentiated, defensible content line.
- **Victorian regional pages.** Alchemy now has dedicated pages for Ballarat, Bendigo, Geelong-adjacent towns, Warrnambool, Wodonga, Wangaratta-area etc. If Tuterly has tutors in those regions via the marketplace, directory-driven pages at `/directory/[suburb]` or `/tutoring/[suburb]` would contest Alchemy's new footprint cheaply.
- **Primary school landing page.** Neither `/parents` nor `/tutoring` targets the "primary school tutor" query specifically. A dedicated page would catch what Apex is now actively chasing.
- **Watch Alchemy's redirect hygiene.** With 400 URL changes in a week, there's a real chance they've dropped rankings on migrated pages. Worth checking in 2-3 weeks whether their suburb pages hold position - if not, that's a window.

## Caveats

- Begin Bright and North Shore both failed to fetch; treat their "silence" as unknown, not confirmed inactive.
- Learnmate's crawl shows 6,430 URLs with zero deltas, which is plausible for a marketplace but worth sanity-checking if it repeats for several weeks.
