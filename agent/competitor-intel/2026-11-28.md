# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-11-28

## Headline

Alchemy Tuition has rebuilt their site this week - 2,466 new URLs, 398 removed. They've shifted from a blog-heavy content strategy (dumping old evergreen posts like "10 best learning apps for kids", "5 tips for selective school exam success") to a massive city-by-subject location grid. Every capital and regional hub now has dedicated ATAR and subject-specific landing pages (ATAR Melbourne, Biology Ballarat, etc.). This is a deliberate pivot toward local SEO at scale, and it directly overlaps Tuterly's Melbourne-focused location pages (/best-tutoring-melbourne, /online-tutoring-melbourne, /vce-tutoring).

## Alchemy Tuition

The restructure is the story. Key patterns in the new URLs:

- **ATAR tutoring city pages**: Adelaide, Albury, Ballarat, Bendigo, Brisbane, Cairns, Canberra, Central Coast, Darwin, Geelong, Gold Coast, Hobart, Melbourne, Newcastle, Perth, Sunshine Coast, Sydney, Toowoomba, Townsville, Wollongong. Each pitches "subject-matched senior tutors, plus honest advice on scaling and subject choice."
- **Subject × city pages**: Biology tutoring is live for Albury, Ballarat and (based on the pattern) likely every city above. Expect the same grid for Maths, English, Chemistry, Physics.
- **New /about/ and founder page** (/about/nic-rothquel/) - leaning on the founder's 10,000 hours of tutoring as credibility.
- **/australian-curriculum-tutoring/** - a new top-level positioning page.

Removed: a large chunk of their old blog (roughly 400 posts), including Year 3 maths activities, study foods, back-to-school posts, selective school tips. They're deliberately cutting low-value content.

Implication for Tuterly: Alchemy will start ranking harder in Melbourne, Geelong and Ballarat for ATAR and VCE queries. Their angle - "honest advice on scaling and subject choice" - is a positioning Tuterly's /atar-planner could own more aggressively.

## Cross-competitor patterns

Only one competitor moved this week, but the Alchemy move is a template others may copy: strip tired blog content, flood with city × subject × year-level landing pages. If Cluey or Tutor Doctor follow, the location-page land grab intensifies through summer.

Notably, **no competitor has published Years 7-10 maths topic content, common-mistakes guides, or practice tools this week**. The homework/practice-app space Tuterly occupies remains uncontested in this week's data.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Melbourne ATAR/VCE defence**: Alchemy's new ATAR Melbourne page leads with "honest advice on scaling and subject choice." Tuterly's /atar-planner and /vce-tutoring pages should make sure they're saying something sharper on scaling than Alchemy. A worked example ("Here's what Methods 35 actually scales to") would beat generic promises.

2. **Years 7-10 maths topic hub gap**: No competitor is producing what the Tuterly app is built around - topic-by-topic Year 7-10 maths content with common mistakes and fresh practice. A /learn hub covering, say, linear equations, Pythagoras, surds, quadratics, trig ratios, each with "the 3 mistakes students make" and a snap-to-practise CTA, has no direct competitor content to outrank right now.

3. **Regional Victoria**: Alchemy now has ATAR Geelong, Ballarat, Bendigo pages. Tuterly is Melbourne-only in URL structure. If the app works online, regional VIC pages are cheap to build and currently uncontested by Cluey/Tutor Doctor.

4. **Blog pruning as a signal**: Alchemy cutting 400 generic parenting posts suggests the "study tips / foods for studying" blog genre is losing value. Tuterly should prioritise maths-specific, test-prep-specific content over generic study advice.

5. **Apex's removed curriculum hubs**: If Apex is retreating from /vce and /hsc hub pages, there's a short-term ranking window for Tuterly's /vce-tutoring page.
