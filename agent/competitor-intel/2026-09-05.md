# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-09-05

## Headline

Cluey's sitemap ballooned by 1,290 URLs this week (from a previous baseline to 1,703 total). On inspection this is almost certainly a sitemap regeneration or CMS migration rather than 1,290 pieces of new content - every sampled URL returned 403 (gated/blocked) or 404 (stale/staging). Nothing else moved: every other competitor with a working sitemap shows zero new or removed URLs. Treat this as a Cluey infrastructure event to keep watching, not a content offensive.

## Per-competitor findings

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Grandparent-funded tutoring angle.** Cluey has a `/grandkids` page. Tuterly's `/parents` hub doesn't address this buyer at all. A short page or section for grandparents paying for tutoring is cheap to produce and essentially unclaimed elsewhere in the set.
2. **NAPLAN 2024/2025 landing page.** Cluey clearly maintains year-versioned NAPLAN pages. Tuterly has no NAPLAN page in the top-level path list. Given NAPLAN drives high-intent parent search, this is a visible gap.
3. **School terms & holidays utility page (Victoria-focused).** Cluey's broken per-state term-dates pages suggest they see value here. Tuterly could own the Victorian version cleanly, tied naturally into `/atar-planner` and `/vce-tutoring`.
4. **School holiday activities / intensives.** Cluey targets Brisbane and Gold Coast. Tuterly could take the Melbourne slot with a school-holiday intensive page that leans on `/best-tutoring-melbourne` and `/vce-tutoring`.
5. **Year-level landing pages.** Cluey has `/year-7` (and presumably others behind the 403s). Tuterly's top-level paths are organised by product and city, not by year level. Year-level pages (Year 7-12) are a straightforward SEO and navigation gap.
