# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-09-24

## Headline

Alchemy Tuition has restructured its entire suburb and subject landing page architecture, moving from flat URLs (`/tutoring-abbotsford/`) to a nested hierarchy (`/tutoring/sydney/abbotsford/`, `/maths-tutoring/sydney/abbotsford/`, `/biology-tutoring/adelaide/`). 206 URLs swapped in a single week. This is a deliberate scaling play: the new structure lets them multiply pages along a subject × city × suburb matrix, and Sydney appears to be getting saturated first. If it works for them in search, expect Melbourne suburb pages next - which is Tuterly's home turf.

## Alchemy Tuition

- **URL restructure**: 206 pages removed, 206 added. Old pattern `/tutoring-{suburb}/` replaced by `/tutoring/{city}/{suburb}/`. Same for subject pages: `/english-tutoring-sydney/` became `/english-tutoring/sydney/`, and now extends to `/english-tutoring/sydney/abbotsbury/`.
- **New subject coverage**: Accounting (Melbourne) and Biology (Adelaide) pages appeared as top-of-funnel city pages, suggesting subject expansion beyond the maths/English core.
- **Suburb pages are templated**: Every new suburb page uses the same "#1 Tutoring {Suburb} // More than 15 years of experience // Award-winning private tuition for K-12 students" pattern. Low content depth, high volume.
- **Positioning language**: "Voted #1", "Australia's highest rated", "thousands of 5-star reviews", "award-winning" repeated across every page.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Suburb-page arms race is still the dominant SEO strategy.** Alchemy is doubling down at scale, and Success Tutoring already sits at 8,149 URLs and Learnmate at 6,430 - both almost certainly running similar city/suburb matrices. Tuterly has `/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne` and `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` but no suburb-level footprint visible in the paths list.
- **Subject × city pages are being layered on top of suburb pages.** Alchemy's new structure means one suburb can rank for "maths tutoring Abbotsford", "English tutoring Abbotsford" and "tutoring Abbotsford" independently. This is the pattern to study if we build a location layer.
- **Discount-led acquisition** remains standard at the centre-based competitors (North Shore's removed URLs show a whole library of percentage-off pages). Tuterly's marketplace model doesn't need to compete on this directly, but parents comparing us to centres will have been anchored on discount pricing.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Melbourne suburb pages.** Alchemy is focused on Sydney and Adelaide in this week's rollout. There's a window to own Melbourne suburb searches before they extend the template south. A `/tutoring/melbourne/{suburb}/` structure aligned with the existing `/best-tutoring-melbourne` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` pages would be a natural extension.
2. **Subject × Melbourne suburb combinations.** `/vce-tutoring` exists as a single page - splitting it into VCE-subject × suburb variants (e.g. VCE Methods in Balwyn, VCE English in Brighton) would out-specialise Alchemy's generic "maths tutoring {suburb}" pages, since we can lean on VCE-specific language they can't credibly write nationally.
3. **Accounting and Biology at senior level.** Alchemy just added Accounting Melbourne. We don't appear to have subject-specific landing pages beyond the VCE umbrella - a VCE Accounting page would intercept the same query with more curriculum credibility.
4. **Alternative-to pages are working for us and no one else is doing them.** `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative` are distinctive. Given Alchemy's aggressive "#1 / voted best" positioning and their new subject rollout, a `/alchemy-alternative` page is worth considering, particularly for Sydney parents who will start seeing more of their pages.
5. **Watch North Shore next week.** If the 472 removed URLs stay gone, that's either a real strategic contraction or a broken site - either is exploitable. If they return, ignore.
