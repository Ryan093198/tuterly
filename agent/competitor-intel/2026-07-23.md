## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Mid-winter content lull.** With school holidays wrapping and Term 3 starting, competitors are not pushing new marketing pages. Only two of eleven working sites published anything, and both were low-effort (a blog post, a tutor profile). This is a good window for Tuterly to ship content without immediate competitive response.

2. **Science as a soft-topic angle.** Tutor Doctor is the only competitor experimenting with a science-mindset angle this week. Tuterly's paths are heavily maths/VCE/ATAR-weighted with no visible science landing page. Worth noting but not urgent.

3. **Events and recognition as retention tools.** North Shore's awards-night content is the kind of community/loyalty asset marketplaces rarely build. Not a threat, but a reminder that traditional coaching colleges compete on ceremony and belonging, not just outcomes.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Content velocity advantage.** Effectively no competitor is publishing right now. If Tuterly ships new /learn or comparison pages this fortnight they'll land in a quiet field. Candidates: a Learnmate-alternative page (to match the existing Cluey and Tutor Doctor alternative pages - Learnmate is the largest competitor by URL count and has no counter-page from us), and an Apex Tuition or Alchemy alternative page.

- **Science and STEM gap.** Tuterly's top-level paths include /vce-tutoring but no subject-specific science, physics, chemistry, or biology entry points. Tutor Doctor's post is thin, but it hints at parent demand for "how to think like a scientist" framing. A /vce-physics or /vce-chemistry page (or a broader /science-tutoring page) would fill a gap none of the tracked competitors own strongly.

- **Tutor profile SEO.** Learnmate's 6,400+ URLs are largely tutor profiles, and they're still adding more. Tuterly has /tutors and /directory but it's worth checking whether individual tutor pages are indexable and structured for search - Learnmate is quietly compounding here every week.

- **Scholarship/competition prep.** North Shore's calendar revolves around events like the Australian Maths Competition. Tuterly has /scholarship-test-prep but nothing visible on maths competitions (AMC, ICAS, etc.). A dedicated competition-prep page could pull demand North Shore currently owns through offline events.
