## Cross-competitor patterns

The only real pattern is the absence of movement. A full freeze across Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath, Learnmate, Apex, Success and North Shore in the same week is unusual. Possible explanations: school holiday lull, end-of-term content calendars winding down before Term 4 push, or our diff baseline needs a sanity check. If next week also shows zero changes everywhere, the pipeline itself is worth auditing.

Three sites (Begin Bright, Alchemy, Math Minds) are effectively invisible to us right now. That's a quarter of the competitor set we have no signal on - worth fixing before drawing confident conclusions about the market.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since nobody shipped anything new, the useful exercise this week is on standing gaps rather than fresh moves:

- **Scale asymmetry.** Success Tutoring (8.1k URLs) and Learnmate (6.4k URLs) dwarf Tuterly's footprint. Both are winning through location × subject × year-level matrices. Our `/tutoring`, `/vce-tutoring` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` hubs could be systematically expanded along the same axes - Melbourne suburb pages crossed with VCE subjects is the obvious wedge, and we already have `/best-tutoring-melbourne` and `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` as proof we can rank in that lane.
- **Comparison pages.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate is the closest structural competitor (marketplace model, large footprint, Melbourne presence) and currently has no comparison page targeting us, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is low-hanging. Same logic for Success Tutoring if they're pushing into Victoria.
- **Scholarship/selective test prep.** We have `/scholarship-test-prep`. North Shore and Begin Bright historically own this category - with Begin Bright offline to us and North Shore static, there's a window to publish deeper content (practice tests, school-specific guides) before they respond.
- **Worksheets as a moat.** `/worksheets` is a format none of the nine active competitors appear to prioritise in their URL structure. Worth leaning into harder - it's a parent acquisition channel they're not contesting.
- **ATAR planner tool.** `/atar-planner` is genuinely differentiated. No competitor in this set has a comparable interactive tool surfaced in their sitemap. Underweighted in our own marketing based on how distinctive it is.
