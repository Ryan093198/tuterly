## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Zero publishing activity across all readable competitors.** In a normal week we'd expect at least Learnmate to churn tutor profiles and Cluey or Apex to push a blog post. The complete flat line suggests either a mid-August lull (school holidays in some states just ended, Term 3 is settling in) or that several sitemaps are cached and not reflecting reality. If next week is also flat everywhere, we should assume the latter and adjust our detection method.

2. **Three competitors are effectively invisible to us right now** - Begin Bright (fetch failed), Alchemy Tuition and Math Minds (empty sitemaps), plus Success Tutoring which only exposes 2 URLs. That's a quarter of the tracked set. Recommend a one-off manual audit of these four so we're not flying blind into VCE crunch season.

3. **Surface-area gap remains dramatic.** Learnmate at 6,424 URLs vs Tuterly's ~16 top-level paths is the structural competitive fact of the market. They index heavily on tutor profiles and subject/location combinations. This isn't new but it's the backdrop for every other decision.
