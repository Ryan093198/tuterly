## Patterns across competitors

1. **Almost everyone is sitting still.** Of 11 competitors successfully crawled, 10 published nothing new. Either we're in a winter content lull (school holidays around this date) or competitors are pausing before a Term 3 push. Either way, this is a low-friction window for Tuterly to ship content.
2. **Learnmate is the only one actively expanding programmatically.** Their per-subject VCE template is now the dominant pattern of new pages from any competitor we track. Two subjects in one week suggests a rolling rollout rather than a one-off.
3. **No pricing, positioning, or homepage-level shifts detected** anywhere in the set.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Beat Learnmate to the VCE subject-page pattern.** Our /vce-tutoring page is a single hub. Learnmate is splitting traffic by subject (Accounting, Economics added this week, on top of whatever they already had). Consider building dedicated pages under /vce-tutoring/ for the high-volume subjects - English, Methods, Specialist, Chemistry, Biology, Accounting, Economics, Legal Studies, Business Management. We have the tutor supply data and ATAR planner to make these pages materially better than Learnmate's thin directory pages.
2. **Differentiate on depth, not just listings.** Learnmate's two new pages read as generic directory copy ("experts in effective online tutoring..."). If we build subject pages we can pair them with: study design summaries, exam date timelines, our worksheets, and ATAR planner integration. That's a defensible angle they can't easily copy.
3. **VCE Accounting and VCE Economics specifically** - Learnmate just published these. If we don't have equivalent or better landing pages, build them in the next 2-3 weeks while their pages are still ranking-fresh and we can compete on freshness.
4. **Quiet week = good week to ship.** With Cluey, Apex, Alchemy, Tutor Doctor and Kip McGrath all flat, content shipped now faces less competing freshness signal. Worth pulling forward any planned /learn or comparison pages (e.g. an Apex or Alchemy alternative page, sibling to /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative).
