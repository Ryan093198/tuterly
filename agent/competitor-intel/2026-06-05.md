## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Promotional pricing is the only lever moving.** North Shore is the lone competitor pushing a campaign this week. With end of financial year approaching in Australia, expect more of this from the centre-based players (Kip McGrath, Begin Bright, Success Tutoring) over the next 2-3 weeks.
2. **Subject-page expansion has stalled.** Learnmate, the most aggressive page-builder in the set, is now trimming rather than adding. Combined with zero new content from Cluey, Apex, Alchemy and Kip, the marketplace category is in a content-quiet phase.
3. **No new VCE/ATAR content from anyone.** Mid-Term 2 timing - competitors are presumably focused on operations rather than top-of-funnel content.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Counter-position against North Shore's discount.** A 35%-off centre promo is a blunt instrument. Tuterly's marketplace model can credibly argue "you don't need a discount when you can pick the right tutor at the right price" - worth a short comparison angle, possibly on `/best-tutoring-melbourne` or as a fresh page in the alternative series (`/north-shore-alternative` would slot in next to the existing Cluey and Tutor Doctor alternative pages).
2. **Pick up the VCE Accounting and Economics ground Learnmate just vacated.** If they've pulled those pages, the SERP for "VCE accounting tutor Melbourne" / "VCE economics tutor" is softer than it was. Tuterly has `/vce-tutoring` but no per-subject VCE pages visible in the path list - a small set of subject pages (Accounting, Economics, Methods, English, Chemistry, Physics) would map directly onto demand and onto Tuterly's directory.
3. **Free-term giveaway as a lead-gen mechanic.** North Shore is testing this. Tuterly's equivalent would be a free trial session or a credit on a first booking - cheaper to deliver in a marketplace model than a full term, and a natural fit for the parents funnel.
4. **No competitor owns "EOFY tutoring spend" content.** With June approaching, a parents-facing piece on planning Semester 2 tutoring (budget, hours, choosing subjects after mid-year reports) would be timely and uncontested. Fits under `/parents` or `/learn`.
