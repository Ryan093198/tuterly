# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-13

## Top observation

North Shore Coaching College has rolled out a large events calendar - 59 new event pages this week - covering state-by-state school holiday programs, scholarship/selective test sittings (ACER, IGNITE, SWAT), and competition dates (SEAMO). This is a clear bet on being the go-to destination for *dated, actionable* test and holiday-program information across NSW, VIC, SA, WA, ACT and Hong Kong. Tuterly has no equivalent events or test-calendar surface, and this is the strategic gap worth closing first.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Overwhelmingly quiet week.** 10 of 12 competitors had zero sitemap changes. The entire week's signal comes from North Shore.
- **Events / test calendars as a content category.** North Shore is the only competitor investing visibly in this surface, and it's a category Tuterly doesn't address at all. Our closest adjacent pages are `/atar-planner` and `/scholarship-test-prep`, both of which are evergreen rather than date-driven.
- **State-by-state scaffolding.** North Shore is publishing per-state (WA/SA/VIC/ACT) and per-region (Randwick/Hornsby/Belconnen) pages. Tuterly's geo pages are Melbourne-only (`/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne`). If we ever expand beyond Melbourne, this is a template to study.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **A dated scholarship/selective test calendar.** North Shore's event pages cover ACER, IGNITE, SWAT and SEAMO sitting dates. We already have `/scholarship-test-prep` - adding a maintained calendar of upcoming test dates (with prep links back into our product) would both outrank them on specifics and give parents a reason to return. Low content effort, high intent traffic.
2. **School holiday program landing pages.** North Shore is targeting "VIC term 4 holiday program" style intent. Even without running centres ourselves, a Melbourne-focused "VCE holiday intensive" or "term break tutoring" page under `/vce-tutoring` or `/tutoring` would plug an obvious gap.
3. **SEAMO and competition prep.** None of our pages address maths competitions. Worth considering a `/learn` or `/worksheets` angle on AMC/SEAMO/ICAS prep - North Shore is treating this as high enough intent to build regional pages for.
4. **SACE and interstate curricula.** North Shore is publishing SACE exam-period content. Our senior-secondary content is VCE-only. Not an immediate priority, but a reminder that `/vce-tutoring` is a ceiling on our addressable intent unless we broaden.
