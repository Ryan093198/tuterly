# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-13

## Headline

Apex Tuition is systematically mining the 2025 VCE, HSC and QCE examiner reports to publish subject-by-subject "how to improve your score" guides. This is a sharp, time-sensitive content play aimed at exactly the ATAR-anxious parents and students Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring` and `/atar-planner` pages target. We should match it this cycle, not next year.

Note: the Cluey "1,290 new URLs" number is misleading - it's a sitemap re-index of mostly old or 404'd pages (NAPLAN 2023/2024, staging URLs marked "ToBeDeleted", 2022 quizzes). Treating as noise except where flagged below.

## Apex Tuition Australia

14 genuinely new pages, almost all editorial:

- **2025 examiner report breakdown series** - full set across HSC Biology, Chemistry, English Advanced, English Standard, Maths Advanced, Maths Extension 1, Maths Standard 2, Physics; VCE Business Management and Economics; QCE Maths Methods (both external exam and internal assessment).
- Two new VCE tutor profiles (Christian D in Oakleigh, Jasmine E).

Why it matters: these are high-intent, evergreen-for-12-months pages that will catch Year 11/12 students searching for "VCE economics 2025 examiner report" over summer and into Term 1. Apex is now covering VCE, HSC and QCE in one coordinated push - a stronger national ATAR footprint than they had.

**Opportunity for Tuterly:** we have `/vce-tutoring` and `/atar-planner` but no equivalent VCE 2025 examiner-report content. A Tuterly version per VCE subject (Methods, Specialist, Chemistry, Physics, English, Further) would plug straight into the ATAR planner and would be more Melbourne/VCE-focused than Apex's spread-thin approach.

## Alchemy Tuition

Significant restructuring: 25 new URLs, 55 removed. They've migrated from flat `/suburb-tutoring/` URLs to a nested `/tutoring/{city}/{suburb}/` structure.

- Old flat pages killed: Albury, Ballarat, Ballarat, Bendigo, Bunbury, Bundaberg, Coffs Harbour, Hervey Bay, Ipswich, Launceston, Mackay, etc.
- New nested pages added across Sydney (Box Hill, Canterbury, Cremorne, Fairfield, Newport, Paddington, Palm Beach, Sandringham, Richmond), Brisbane (Ipswich, Capalaba), Perth (Rockingham), Newcastle (Maitland), Canberra (Queanbeyan), and Wollongong.
- New **year-level maths tutoring** pages: Year 3, 4, 8, 9, 10.
- New **online** subject hubs for maths and English.
- New **QCE Brisbane** hub.

Positioning cue: they're going hard on year-level + subject + suburb combinations, plus explicit online pages. The year-level maths pages (Year 3-10) are a template Tuterly could beat with a Melbourne/VCE lens.

**Opportunities for Tuterly:**
- We have `/online-tutoring-melbourne` but no subject-specific online hub (online maths, online English).
- No year-level maths or English landing pages exist on Tuterly's top-level paths. Alchemy clearly thinks these convert.
- `/best-tutoring-melbourne` could be complemented by Melbourne-suburb pages - Alchemy is building this inventory nationally and will outrank us on long-tail suburb searches.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Examiner-report content is the Q4 play.** Apex has moved first and hard. Window to publish Tuterly VCE equivalents is open now - results drop + summer holiday research period aligns perfectly with our ATAR planner audience.
2. **Suburb × subject × year-level matrices** are the SEO land-grab shape. Alchemy is building it; Learnmate has had it for years; Tuterly has very little of this inventory beyond the Melbourne pricing/online/best-of pages.
3. **Online-specific subject pages** (not just "online tutoring") are now standard - Alchemy added online maths and online English hubs this week. Tuterly's single `/online-tutoring-melbourne` page is thinner than the category now expects.
4. **Nobody is competing on comparison pages.** Our `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative` remain uncontested - worth adding `/apex-alternative` and `/alchemy-alternative` while they're actively pushing content.
