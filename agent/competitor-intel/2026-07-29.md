## North Shore Coaching College

Two new pages appeared, both tied to a single campaign:

- `/book-your-free-trial-class/`
- `/book-your-free-trial-class-terms-conditions/`

The paired terms & conditions page suggests this is a formal, ongoing offer rather than a one-off promo - they've built a dedicated funnel with legal scaffolding, not just a homepage banner.

**Opportunity for Tuterly:** North Shore is leaning on "try before you buy" as an acquisition lever. Tuterly's `/parents` and `/tutoring` entry points don't currently surface an equivalent low-commitment trial offer in the URL structure. Worth considering whether a visible trial/first-session page would sharpen conversion, particularly against centre-based competitors where a trial class is the standard hook.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Almost total dormancy.** Eleven of twelve competitors published nothing new. Mid-year winter break timing likely explains it - families aren't shopping, so competitors aren't publishing.
- **Trial offers as the live battleground.** The one movement of the week was a trial-booking funnel. Combined with the fact that Kip McGrath and North Shore both centre their marketing on free assessments/trials, this remains the dominant conversion mechanic in the tutoring category. Tuterly doesn't have a dedicated trial or first-lesson page in the top-level path list.
- **No pricing moves visible.** Nothing changed on any pricing-adjacent URLs. Tuterly's `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` page continues to have a relatively uncontested position - most competitors still don't publish transparent pricing.
- **Marketplace competitors (Learnmate, Alchemy, Apex) all static.** No new tutor profiles or location pages were surfaced this week, which is unusual for sites of that size and may reflect crawl-side sampling rather than genuine stasis. Worth watching next week.

## Suggested actions

1. **Consider a trial-focused landing page.** North Shore's move reinforces that a bookable-trial URL is table stakes among centres. A Tuterly equivalent - even framed as "first lesson guarantee" or "risk-free match" - would close a visible gap.
2. **Retry Begin Bright next week** before assuming the fetch failure is meaningful.
3. **Use the quiet week to push content Tuterly owns uniquely** - `/atar-planner`, `/worksheets`, `/sample-report`. None of the competitors have equivalents, and a low-noise week is a good time to build authority on those before the Term 3 push.
