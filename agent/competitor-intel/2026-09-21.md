## Cross-competitor patterns

The obvious pattern is stillness. Nine sites, zero deltas. A few possibilities: it's genuinely a quiet publishing week (plausible given we're deep in Term 3 delivery mode for most operators), or crawls landed on cached sitemaps. Either way, no competitor made a visible strategic move this week.

The standing structural pattern remains: Success Tutoring (8.1k URLs) and Learnmate (6.4k URLs) are running high-volume programmatic strategies, while Cluey and Apex sit in the 1.7-1.8k range with more curated footprints. The franchise/centre operators (Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath, Tutoring for Excellence) all sit under 500 URLs and are not competing on content surface area.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Nothing new to react to, so this is a week to press on our own gaps. Standing observations worth acting on:

1. **We have no equivalent to Learnmate's or Success Tutoring's volume of subject-location or tutor-profile pages surfacing via `/directory`.** If we want to compete on organic discovery for "[subject] tutor [suburb]" queries, the gap remains wide. A quiet competitor week is a good time to ship a batch of directory pages without them noticing.

2. **We already have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`.** Given Learnmate is the closest structural competitor (also a marketplace, also Melbourne-active, 6.4k URLs), a `/learnmate-alternative` page is the obvious next comparison asset. Success Tutoring is the other candidate given their centre-based model contrasts cleanly with our marketplace positioning.

3. **Scholarship test prep and ATAR planning remain ours to own** - none of the competitors surfaced changes in these areas this week, and we have dedicated pages (`/scholarship-test-prep`, `/atar-planner`) that most of them don't match.

4. **Manual check next week**: Alchemy Tuition, Math Minds, Begin Bright and North Shore all failed to return data. If any of them are quietly rebuilding, we'd miss it. Worth 15 minutes of manual review before next report.

## Bottom line

No competitor moves to defend against. Use the week to ship directory depth and add a Learnmate comparison page.
