## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Universal standstill.** Eleven sites, zero deltas. This is unusual enough to call out. Possible explanations: mid-year-exam period reducing marketing output, end-of-financial-year freeze on web projects, or simply a genuinely slow content week. Either way, it's a publishing opportunity for us.

2. **Footprint hierarchy is unchanged.** Learnmate (6.3k) > Alchemy (2.7k) > Cluey (1.7k) > Apex (1.7k) > North Shore (1.0k) > everyone else. The marketplace-style players (Learnmate, Alchemy, Apex) carry the deepest content libraries — mostly tutor profiles and subject/location combinations. This remains the structural pattern Tuterly should plan against.

3. **No pricing movement anywhere.** Worth watching given our `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` page is a differentiator — competitors continue to mostly avoid published pricing.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because nothing shifted, the opportunities are the same standing ones, but with extra runway this week:

- **Centres positioning.** None of the tracked competitors appear to have a `/centres` equivalent aimed at tutoring centre operators. This remains a distinct B2B angle we own.
- **ATAR planner tool.** No competitor shipped any tool-like or interactive page this week. Our `/atar-planner` continues to be a category of one in this set.
- **Worksheets library.** Of the larger sites, the bulk of their URL counts are tutor profiles and SEO landers, not downloadable resources. `/worksheets` is still a thin-competition lane — worth expanding while competitors are quiet.
- **Comparison pages.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate is the structurally closest competitor (marketplace model) and has the largest footprint, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is the obvious next addition. Same logic could apply to Apex and Alchemy.
- **Scholarship test prep.** North Shore and Kip McGrath play in this space conceptually but didn't publish anything new. Our `/scholarship-test-prep` page can be deepened without competitive response risk this week.
