# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-09-10

## Headline

Apex Tuition has quietly launched a large, structured Year 7 maths worksheet library this week - at least 82 new URLs, almost all under `/worksheets/`, each targeting a specific curriculum topic (integers, ratios, prime factorisation, mean/median/mode, probability sample spaces, financial maths modelling, etc.), plus a `/resources/year-7-maths-worksheets-free-worksheets-answers` hub page. This is a direct challenge to Tuterly's `/worksheets` offering and looks like the start of a systematic year-level rollout. Worth a close look before they extend to Years 8-12.

## Per-competitor findings

### Apex Tuition - 82 new pages
- New hub: `/resources/year-7-maths-worksheets-free-worksheets-answers` (positioned as free worksheets with answers).
- New worksheet pages cover the full Year 7 Australian Curriculum maths spine, including:
  - Number: integers, operations with fractions/decimals, equivalent fractions/decimals on the number line, percentages, rounding/estimation, expanded notation & powers of 10, prime factorisation & index notation.
  - Algebra: patterns/tables of values & the Cartesian plane, manipulating formulas, graphs of relationships from data.
  - Measurement & geometry: angle sums of triangles/polygons, angles on parallel lines, area of triangles/parallelograms, classifying triangles/quadrilaterals/polygons, circles, representing objects in 2D.
  - Statistics & probability: mean/median/mode/range, data displays & distributions, chance experiments & simulations, probability sample spaces & outcomes.
  - Applied: ratios, ratio modelling in practical contexts, financial maths modelling.
- Titles on the individual worksheet pages are still the default "Apex Tuition Australia" - suggests these were shipped fast and page-level metadata isn't finalised. Window to move before they polish.

### Learnmate - 1 new page
- `/tutors/bsss-psychology-tutors/` - a curriculum-specific ACT landing page (BSSS = Board of Senior Secondary Studies). Angle: "no external exam, so every mark comes from college assessment." Continues Learnmate's pattern of drilling into state/territory curriculum subjects one at a time.

### Alchemy Tuition - 1 new, 1 removed
- Migrated `/brisbane-tutoring/` → `/tutoring/brisbane/`. Pure URL restructure into a `/tutoring/{city}/` pattern, likely signalling more city pages to follow. Positioning unchanged ("Voted #1", "award-winning", "thousands of 5-star reviews").

### No change this week
Cluey (1,702 URLs), Tutor Doctor (401), EzyMaths (495), Kip McGrath (324), Tutoring for Excellence (203), Math Minds (66), Success Tutoring (2 - site appears near-empty).

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Worksheet libraries as an acquisition play.** Apex is going hard on free, topic-level worksheets with answers - the same territory Tuterly's `/worksheets` occupies. Nobody else moved this week, but Apex alone at 82 pages/week is a serious pace.
2. **City-slug consolidation.** Alchemy is moving to `/tutoring/{city}/`. Tuterly already owns `/online-tutoring-melbourne` and `/best-tutoring-melbourne` but doesn't have a clean `/tutoring/{city}/` structure for expansion into Sydney, Brisbane, Perth etc.
3. **Curriculum-specific landing pages by jurisdiction.** Learnmate keeps building single-subject pages tied to state boards (this week: ACT BSSS Psychology). Tuterly's VCE coverage is strong (`/vce-tutoring`) but there's no equivalent for HSC, QCE, SACE, WACE or BSSS.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Defend the worksheet vertical now.** Audit `/worksheets` against Apex's Year 7 topic list. If there are gaps (financial maths modelling, ratio in practical contexts, chance experiments & simulations look like modern-curriculum topics competitors often miss), fill them this month. Apex's individual worksheet pages currently have generic titles - a well-titled, well-described Tuterly page has a real chance to outrank them while they're still shipping.
- **Beat Apex to Years 8-12.** They've signalled a template. If Tuterly rolls out Years 8, 9, 10 worksheet hubs before Apex extends, you own the year-level search intent.
- **Interstate curriculum pages.** Learnmate is one page a week into BSSS territory. Tuterly could build HSC, QCE and BSSS equivalents to `/vce-tutoring` cheaply - even one page per state would broaden the top of funnel beyond Melbourne.
- **City structure.** Consider a `/tutoring/{city}/` pattern alongside the existing Melbourne pages, so Sydney/Brisbane/Perth expansion isn't blocked by URL debt later.
- **Comparison pages.** Tuterly has `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Apex's aggressive content push and Learnmate's size (6,429 URLs), `/apex-tuition-alternative` and `/learnmate-alternative` are logical next additions - especially an Apex one that leans on quality/curation vs. volume of free worksheets.
