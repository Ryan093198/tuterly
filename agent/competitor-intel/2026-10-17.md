## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Universal stillness.** Ten competitors, zero deltas. Either the sector is in a mid-term-holiday publishing lull, or our diffing is only catching sitemap-level changes and missing in-page edits (pricing, hero copy, package names). Worth sanity-checking by manually spot-reviewing one or two homepages before concluding it was truly a dead week.
2. **Footprint spread is enormous** - from Math Minds at 66 URLs to Success Tutoring at 8,149. The marketplace-style players (Success, Learnmate, Alchemy, Apex) all sit above 2,000 URLs, mostly from programmatic tutor-profile and suburb pages. Tuterly's `/directory` and `/tutors` paths are the equivalent surface area and are where scale will or won't be visible to Google over time.
3. **Two monitoring blind spots** (EzyMaths, Begin Bright) have now persisted - fix before they hide a real move.
