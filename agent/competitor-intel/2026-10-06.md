# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-06

## Headline

Apex Tuition has moved first on the 2025 VCE examiners' reports, publishing a coordinated set of nine "how to ace" guides across Biology, Chemistry, English, English Language, Physics, General Maths and Specialist Maths within the week. This is the most strategically relevant move of the week because the examiners' reports drop annually and define the next 12 months of VCE study content - whoever ranks on these topics owns a lot of VCE parent and student search traffic. Tuterly's /vce-tutoring and /learn hubs do not currently have equivalent subject-by-subject 2025 report breakdowns.

## Per-competitor findings

### Apex Tuition (9 new pages)
All nine new URLs are blog posts, tightly themed around two timely hooks:
- **2025 VCE Examiners' Report breakdowns**: Biology, Chemistry, English, English Language, Physics, General Maths, Specialist Maths.
- **2026 NAPLAN results analysis**: separate Year 7 and Year 9 posts.

This is a focused content push, not scattered blogging. Apex is positioning itself as the analytical VCE voice for the 2026 study year and simultaneously picking up NAPLAN results traffic from parents.

### Cluey Learning (1,290 "new" URLs - likely a crawl/baseline artefact)
The large new count appears to be a re-indexing event rather than genuine publishing. The sample contains:
- Multiple 404s (staging pages, old 2023 school-term pages marked "ToBeDeleted").
- Legacy pages (NAPLAN 2023, NAPLAN 2024, 2022 Maths Quiz) that are not actually new.
- Mislabeled holiday pages (URL says "brisbane", title says "Sydney"; URL says "gold-coast", title says "Canberra") - genuine QA issues on Cluey's side.

That said, a few pages worth flagging as present in Cluey's live set and absent from ours:
- **/schools** - an Australian schools directory/guide. Directly overlaps our /directory play.
- **/grandkids** - a 20% off tutoring offer aimed at grandparents paying for grandchildren. Novel buyer persona.
- **/win-tutoring-every-week** - a free-year-of-tutoring competition as a lead magnet.
- **/free-maths-worksheets** and **/maths-quiz-for-parents** - lead-gen content hooks.
- **/year-7** - a "starting high school" parent guide.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Timely exam-cycle content is where the only real movement is.** Apex is the single competitor publishing meaningfully, and 100% of its output is tied to two annual events (VCE examiners' reports, NAPLAN results). Everyone else is static this week.
2. **The rest of the market is quiet.** Seven competitors published nothing. That's an opening - there's little content noise to compete against right now for Term 4 / exam-season topics.
3. **Cluey's site hygiene is poor** - staging pages indexed, 404s in the sitemap, mismatched city titles on holiday-activity pages. Not an immediate threat but suggests their content ops are stretched.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Match Apex on VCE 2025 examiners' reports, but better.** Apex has text blog posts. Tuterly could publish subject-by-subject breakdowns under /vce-tutoring or /learn that combine the examiner insights with worked examples from our tutors and link to relevant /worksheets. We have a credibility angle (real VCE tutors) Apex doesn't lean on.
2. **NAPLAN 2026 results analysis for Year 7 and Year 9.** Apex has staked ground here already; if we want it we need to move fast, and ideally differentiate with a Victorian/Melbourne lens (Apex writes nationally).
3. **"Starting Year 7" parent guide.** Cluey has /year-7; we have nothing equivalent under /parents. High-intent audience for ongoing tutoring relationships.
4. **Schools directory depth.** Cluey's /schools competes with our /directory. Worth auditing how our directory compares on school coverage and whether we should add school-level landing pages.
5. **Grandparent-as-buyer angle.** Cluey's /grandkids page targets a persona nobody else does. Low-effort test: a /parents sub-page or landing page acknowledging grandparents as payers.
6. **Lead-magnet quizzes.** Cluey runs a parents' maths quiz and a competition. We have /sample-report and /atar-planner as our interactive assets - a "What ATAR do you need?" or VCE subject-selection quiz would be a natural extension.
