# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-06

## Headline

Apex Tuition has aggressively claimed the "2026 exam timetable" and "2027 school calendar" search territory this week, publishing 15 purpose-built guides covering every state curriculum (VCE, HSC, QCE, SACE, WACE, IB) and every capital city. This is a deliberate seasonal play aimed at parents and students searching right now as Term 4 exams approach. Tuterly has no equivalent content and is invisible for these queries.

## Apex Tuition Australia

15 new blog posts, all tightly themed around end-of-year planning and 2026 prep:

- **Exam timetables for 2026**: VCE, HSC, QCE, SACE, WACE, and IB each got a dedicated guide.
- **2027 school term dates and public holidays**: separate pages for Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Adelaide, and Perth.
- **NAPLAN**: results analysis for Year 3 and Year 5 (2026 cohort).
- **ATAR crossover**: "How Does Your IB Score Convert to ATAR in 2026?" - a high-intent query with limited quality competition.
- **VCE Maths Methods**: a breakdown of the 2025 examiner report with improvement tactics. This is the most defensible piece of the batch - genuinely useful for Methods students heading into exams.

The pattern is clear: Apex is building an evergreen "planning utility" layer on their blog that resets each year. These pages likely rank fast because the queries are seasonal and the SERP turns over annually.

## Cluey Learning

No new pages. Removed 1,290 URLs - a large cleanup. The removed sample is almost entirely old staging pages, dated 2023/2024 variants of option guides, ATAR quiz staging duplicates, and feedback page variants (`feedback-trustpilot`, `feedback-productreview`, etc.). This looks like site hygiene / consolidation rather than a strategic shift. Worth noting Cluey is reducing surface area, not expanding - consistent with their broader cost-cutting narrative over the past year.

## Cross-competitor patterns

Only one competitor shipped anything meaningful this week, so "patterns" is limited, but the Apex move highlights two gaps worth naming:

1. **Seasonal utility content** (exam timetables, term dates, NAPLAN result commentary) is being treated as a legitimate acquisition channel by Apex. No one else is competing hard on it.
2. **IB-to-ATAR conversion** is a specific, high-intent query Apex now owns. Tuterly's `/atar-planner` is the natural home for this but doesn't currently address IB.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **VCE exam timetable 2026 and 2027 Victorian school term dates**: Tuterly's Melbourne/VCE positioning makes these a natural fit. A single well-structured `/vce-tutoring` sub-page or `/learn` entry could outrank Apex's generic multi-state treatment by being Victoria-specific and linking into the ATAR planner.
- **Extend `/atar-planner` to handle IB conversion**: Apex has published a static blog post. Tuterly could ship an actual interactive IB-to-ATAR converter - a functional tool beats a listicle on this query every time.
- **VCE Maths Methods examiner report breakdowns**: Apex is doing this at the blog level. Tuterly could do it better by tying each insight to a worksheet in `/worksheets` or a specific tutor in `/directory`. This is a "product beats blog" angle.
- **NAPLAN results commentary**: Apex is building year-on-year authority here. If Tuterly wants a parent-acquisition lever on `/parents`, Year 3/5/7/9 NAPLAN analysis is the kind of content parents actively share.
- **Cluey's content cleanup**: Cluey has removed its ATAR quiz staging pages and English literary techniques page. If the live versions have also quietly gone, there may be ranking opportunities on queries like "english literary techniques" where Cluey previously held territory. Worth a manual check before next week.

## What to ignore

Cluey's 1,290-URL removal looks dramatic but is housekeeping (staging dupes, feedback variants). Not a strategic signal on its own, though combined with their broader retrenchment it reinforces that Cluey is contracting, not expanding - useful context for the `/cluey-alternative` page positioning.
