# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-04

**Headline: a genuinely quiet week.** None of the nine competitors we successfully crawled published or removed a single URL. No new landing pages, no new blog posts, no pricing or positioning shifts visible at the sitemap level. This is unusual enough to be worth noting on its own - either our crawl window caught everyone between content pushes, or the sector is collectively coasting into Term 4.

With no changes to analyse, the useful work this week is a structural reminder of where each competitor sits and where our own gaps remain visible.

## Cross-competitor patterns

The only pattern is the absence of one: zero net publishing across the entire tracked set in a single week. A few possibilities worth keeping in mind as context for coming weeks:

- Term 4 in Victoria/NSW tends to be an operational period for tutoring businesses (exam delivery, not marketing). Content pushes often resume in late November / early December ahead of the summer enrolment window.
- Three sitemap failures in one week (Begin Bright, Alchemy, Math Minds) suggests we should add a lightweight homepage-diff fallback for competitors whose sitemaps are unreliable, otherwise we'll keep flying blind on the smaller players.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Nothing new triggered by competitor moves this week, but the quiet period is itself an opening:

1. **Pre-empt the summer enrolment cycle.** If competitors are about to start publishing again in 4-6 weeks, anything we ship now (worksheets, ATAR planner updates, suburb pages) has a clearer run at ranking and sharing before the noise returns.
2. **Comparison-page coverage.** We have /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative. The other competitors with meaningful search presence - Learnmate (6.4k URLs), Success Tutoring (8.1k URLs), and Apex Tuition (1.8k URLs) - have no equivalent Tuterly page. Learnmate in particular is the closest structural comparison to us (Melbourne-based marketplace model) and the absence of a /learnmate-alternative page is the most obvious gap.
3. **Centres angle.** Success Tutoring and Kip McGrath both operate centre networks and together account for a huge share of the tracked URLs. Our /centres page exists but isn't positioned against them. A "centres vs online 1:1" explainer would differentiate against the two biggest footprints in the set.
4. **Scholarship test prep.** Still no visible movement from North Shore or Apex on this topic this week, despite both historically owning it. Our /scholarship-test-prep page has a clear window.
