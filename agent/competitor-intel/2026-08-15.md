# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-08-15

## Headline observation

Learnmate is quietly extending its VCE subject-specific tutor landing pages to commerce subjects (Economics and Accounting). This is the same page pattern they've built dominance on for years - one URL per subject, tightly focused on "online VCE [subject] tutors" - and it directly overlaps Tuterly's /vce-tutoring territory. If Tuterly's VCE hub is a single page rather than a subject-by-subject set, Learnmate will keep winning long-tail parent searches for specific VCE subjects.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **A quiet week almost everywhere except Learnmate.** Eight of the eleven successfully-crawled competitors published zero new pages. Learnmate is the only one still shipping meaningfully - and they're compounding the same VCE-subject strategy they've run for years.

2. **The VCE subject-page arms race is one-sided right now.** Nobody else is building per-subject VCE landing pages at Learnmate's rate. That means the topic is contested by Learnmate essentially alone - and Tuterly's single `/vce-tutoring` page has to compete against dozens of Learnmate URLs each optimised for one subject.

3. **Housekeeping over expansion.** North Shore's 100-URL prune and Alchemy's stray staging page suggest mid-year site maintenance rather than a content push. Historically August is a lull between Term 3 start and the pre-exam surge, which matches what we're seeing.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Build out VCE subject pages to match Learnmate's coverage.** Tuterly has `/vce-tutoring` as a single page. Learnmate is filling in Economics and Accounting this week on top of the mainstream subjects. Priority subject pages to consider: VCE Maths Methods, Specialist Maths, Further/General Maths, English, Chemistry, Biology, Physics, Economics, Accounting, Legal Studies. Angle to differentiate: pair each with a real tutor list from the /directory and pricing transparency (Learnmate's pages don't lead with price).

- **Commerce-stream VCE is a live gap Learnmate just noticed.** If they're only now shipping Economics and Accounting pages, the SERPs likely haven't consolidated. Tuterly could publish equivalents quickly and compete on freshness plus a stronger offer (worksheets, ATAR planner integration).

- **Events and competitions content is being abandoned by North Shore.** If Tuterly has any interest in scholarship-test, AMC, or academic-competition audiences (adjacent to `/scholarship-test-prep`), there's a small opening as North Shore reduces its footprint here. Worth confirming next week whether they rebuild or genuinely exit.

- **No competitor is publishing pricing-transparency content.** Tuterly's `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` remains a differentiated angle - none of the tracked competitors added or changed pricing pages this week.
