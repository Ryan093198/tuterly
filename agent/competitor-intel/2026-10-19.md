# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-19

## Headline

Cluey's sitemap jumped from a near-empty baseline to 1,702 URLs this week, surfacing 1,290 "new" pages. Most of this is a crawler catching up rather than a genuine content blitz - a lot of the sample is dated (NAPLAN 2023/2024, "2022 Maths Quiz"), 404s, or staging junk. But buried in it are a few genuinely interesting plays worth copying or countering: a full Australian **schools directory**, a **grandparents** acquisition page with 20% off, a **"win a year of free tutoring"** competition, and city-specific **school holiday activities** pages. The rest of the market was quiet.

## Cluey Learning

The surge is mostly archival (pages that existed but weren't previously indexed by our crawler), so treat the 1,290 figure with caution. The strategically interesting items in the sample:

- **/schools** - an "Australian Schools List, Guide and Directory". Positioned as a parent-facing lookup tool. This overlaps directly with our /directory concept and is worth benchmarking.
- **/grandkids** - "20% Off Tutoring For Grandchildren". A dedicated landing page targeting grandparents as the paying customer. Unusual segmentation and we have nothing comparable.
- **/win-tutoring-every-week** - a competition offering "one year of free school tutoring" as a lead magnet. Note the viral-style framing ("I just entered this competition - you should enter it too!").
- **/free-maths-worksheets** - framed with the same viral referral hook. Overlaps with our /worksheets - worth checking whether their offering is deeper than ours.
- **/maths-quiz-for-parents** - interactive "are you smarter than your child" quiz as a top-of-funnel hook.
- **/year-7** - a Year 7 high school transition guide for parents. We don't have year-level parent guides.
- **/school-holiday-activities-brisbane**, **/gold-coast** (titles mis-labelled "Sydney" and "Canberra" - their templating is broken). City-level holiday activity pages used as SEO funnels.
- **/request-call** - a dedicated call-request page, suggesting they're pushing a phone-sales motion.
- Significant hygiene issues: multiple `-ToBeDeleted` and `staging` URLs are publicly reachable (even if 404), and the NAPLAN 2023 and school-terms-2023 pages are still live. Their site maintenance is slipping.

## Apex Tuition Australia

One new page: **/vce-tutors/nathalie-m**, a VCE tutor profile in Plenty (northern Melbourne). Consistent with their ongoing roster expansion into Melbourne VCE - directly competitive with our /vce-tutoring and /online-tutoring-melbourne positioning. Worth noting the depth of their tutor-profile pages as a volume SEO strategy.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- The market was essentially static this week. Only Apex shipped net-new content, and only Cluey showed meaningful (if partly archival) movement.
- Cluey's visible playbook leans on **lead-magnet funnels** (worksheets, parent quizzes, competitions) and **audience-segmented landing pages** (grandparents, Year 7 parents) rather than programmatic location/subject SEO. Apex is the opposite: tutor-profile volume.
- No pricing changes observed anywhere.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Grandparents angle.** Cluey has a dedicated grandparent page with a discount. We have /parents but no equivalent. Low-effort landing page opportunity, and arguably a better fit for a marketplace that can match a specific tutor than for Cluey's standardised product.
2. **Schools directory depth.** Cluey's /schools is a parent lookup. Our /directory is tutor-facing. A school-indexed landing structure (e.g. "tutors near [school]") would compete directly and plays to marketplace strengths.
3. **Year-level parent guides.** Cluey's /year-7 fills a transition-anxiety search intent. A Victorian-specific version (Year 7, Year 10 subject selection, Year 11 VCE transition) would complement /atar-planner nicely.
4. **Interactive parent quiz.** Cluey's maths quiz is a cheap but sticky top-of-funnel asset. We have nothing in that register.
5. **Competition / referral mechanic.** The "win a year of tutoring" + "I just entered, you should too" framing is a viral loop they're clearly investing in. Worth a small test.
6. **School holiday landing pages.** Cluey is targeting these at a city level but executing sloppily (wrong city names in titles). A clean Melbourne-focused version is an easy win.
7. **Hygiene jab.** Cluey's live staging URLs and 2023-dated NAPLAN pages are a credibility weak point if we ever do direct comparison content on /cluey-alternative.
