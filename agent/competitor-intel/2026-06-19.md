## Patterns

1. **Universal stillness.** Eleven of eleven crawled competitors made zero detectable changes in the past week. Either it's a genuinely slow period (mid-year, post-exam lull is plausible) or several of them are sitting on content updates that didn't ship. Either way, this is a low-risk week to push our own changes without being immediately overshadowed.

2. **Footprint asymmetry.** Learnmate (6,430), Alchemy (2,691), Apex (1,697) and Cluey (1,703) dwarf everyone else. Tuterly's top-level path list is lean by comparison - the depth those competitors have is almost certainly in tutor profile pages, subject pages, and location pages, which is where a marketplace accumulates URLs. If our /directory and /tutors aren't generating comparable indexed depth, that's a structural gap, not a content one.

3. **Two suspect crawls.** Success Tutoring (2 URLs) and Begin Bright (fetch failed) both need eyeballs. Don't treat their "no change" as signal.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Refresh the comparison pages while targets are static.** /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative are aimed at sites that haven't moved in at least a week. Good window to tighten claims, update pricing comparisons, and add fresh testimonials without the competitor counter-moving.

- **Own the planning/tools angle.** /atar-planner and /worksheets are genuinely distinctive - none of the competitors we track lead with interactive planning tools or downloadable worksheets at the top level. This is an underexploited wedge; consider expanding it (e.g. subject-specific planners, exam countdown tools) before someone copies it.

- **Centres positioning.** /centres is unusual for a marketplace - most competitors are either pure marketplace (Learnmate) or pure chain (Kip McGrath, North Shore, Begin Bright). The hybrid story is a differentiator worth sharpening.

- **Location depth.** We have /best-tutoring-melbourne, /tutoring-prices-melbourne, /online-tutoring-melbourne. The chains (North Shore, Kip McGrath) and the larger marketplaces likely have per-suburb pages contributing to their URL counts. Worth auditing whether expanding into suburb-level Melbourne pages would close the indexed-depth gap.

- **Scholarship test prep** is a genuine niche for us (/scholarship-test-prep). Confirm none of the chains have quietly launched equivalents - a manual sample of Kip McGrath, North Shore and Begin Bright next week would be worth doing regardless of the diff.
