# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-08-07

## Headline

No competitor published or removed a single tracked URL this week. Across nine successfully crawled sites, the delta is zero. This is unusual enough to be the observation itself: either the market is genuinely quiet mid-August, or our crawl is missing changes that don't surface in sitemaps (landing page edits, pricing tweaks, homepage repositioning). Worth a manual spot-check of Cluey and Learnmate homepages before assuming stasis.

## Cross-competitor patterns

The only pattern this week is the absence of activity. Nothing new, nothing removed, no visible pricing or positioning shifts through the sitemap layer. Three sites (Alchemy, Math Minds, Begin Bright) remain effectively invisible to this monitor and have been for consecutive weeks - if any of them matter competitively, we should switch to a homepage-diff approach for those specifically rather than relying on sitemaps.

Learnmate's flat 6,424 is the anomaly to keep watching. A marketplace that size normally adds or drops profile pages weekly.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's no new competitor content to react to, this is a good week to press on gaps we already know exist:

- **Scholarship test prep depth**: we have `/scholarship-test-prep` but none of the tracked competitors appear to compete seriously here based on prior weeks. Worth expanding while the field is quiet.
- **ATAR planning tool**: `/atar-planner` remains a differentiator - no competitor has a comparable interactive tool visible in their URL structure. Consider promoting it more prominently on `/vce-tutoring` and `/parents`.
- **Worksheets library**: `/worksheets` is another asset none of the tracked competitors match. Kip McGrath and EzyMaths would be the natural threats here and neither has moved.
- **Comparison pages**: we have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate is our closest structural competitor, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is the obvious next addition - especially while they're not publishing anything defensive.

## Caveats

Three competitors weren't readable this week (Begin Bright, Alchemy, Math Minds) and Success Tutoring's two-URL sitemap continues to make them opaque. If any of these are strategically important, we need a different monitoring method for them.
