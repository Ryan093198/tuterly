## Cross-competitor patterns

The only pattern is stillness. Mid-August sits after the July school-holiday push and before Term 4 / VCE final-run marketing, so a quiet publishing week is consistent with normal cycles. Expect competitor activity to pick up from late August as VCE exam prep and 2027 enrolment campaigns start.

Two structural observations worth keeping in mind even in a quiet week:

- **Learnmate's 6.4k URLs vs Alchemy's 2.7k vs Apex's 1.7k** almost certainly reflects tutor-profile and subject-location directory pages. Tuterly's `/directory` is the equivalent surface, and directory depth is the main lever these players are pulling to own long-tail search.
- **Success Tutoring's crawlable footprint (2 URLs)** remains an anomaly. If we ever want to benchmark against them we'll need a different method (manual review or rendered crawl).

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's nothing new to react to, this is a good week to push on gaps that were already visible rather than chase competitors:

- **Scholarship and selective-entry content.** Tuterly has `/scholarship-test-prep`; North Shore and Begin Bright are the natural competitors on this topic and neither moved this week. Good window to expand this hub (year-level guides, school-specific pages) before anyone else does.
- **ATAR planning tool.** `/atar-planner` is a differentiated asset - none of the tracked competitors have an equivalent interactive tool in their URL sets. Worth surfacing it more prominently from `/vce-tutoring` and `/parents` while the space is uncontested.
- **Comparison pages.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate's size and Apex's growing footprint, a `/learnmate-alternative` and `/apex-alternative` page would round out the set. Neither competitor published anything defensive this week, so we're not walking into a fight.
- **Worksheets.** `/worksheets` is another asset none of the tracked competitors match in their URL structures. Under-marketed relative to its potential as a parent acquisition channel.

## What to watch next week

- Whether Begin Bright comes back online or the fetch failure persists.
- Any Cluey or Tutor Doctor movement - they're the two brands we have dedicated comparison pages against, so new positioning from them is highest-priority signal.
- First signs of Term 4 / VCE-exam campaign pages from Apex, Learnmate and North Shore.
