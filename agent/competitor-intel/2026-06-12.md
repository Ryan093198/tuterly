# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-06-12

## Headline

Learnmate has quietly extended its VCE subject-tutor landing page set with two new pages this week: **Online VCE Accounting Tutors** and **Online VCE Economics Tutors**. These are exactly the kind of subject-by-subject pages that compete head-on with our `/vce-tutoring` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` hubs, and they are pages Tuterly does not currently have dedicated equivalents for. A very quiet week otherwise - most competitors made no public changes at all.

## Per-competitor findings

### Learnmate
Two new pages, both following the same template:
- `/tutors/online-vce-accounting-tutors/` - "Hire the Best Online VCE Accounting Tutors"
- `/tutors/online-vce-economics-tutors/` - "Hire the Best Online VCE Economics Tutors"

Both are positioned around online 1:1 tutoring for specific VCE subjects. This continues Learnmate's pattern of building a wide matrix of `{online} × {VCE subject}` landing pages. Their total indexed URL count (6,334) is the largest in our set by a wide margin, reflecting that programmatic approach.

### North Shore Coaching College
- Two new pages: a WA Term 3 re-enrolment notice and a terms-and-conditions page for a free online trial assessment.
- Two removed: author archive pages (`/news/author/adrian/`, `/news/author/yammy/`).
- The trial-assessment T&Cs page is the more interesting one - it implies they are actively running or relaunching a free online trial offer as a lead magnet. Worth checking the corresponding landing page to see what the offer actually is.

### Cluey Learning
No changes detected (1,703 URLs unchanged). Worth noting given we run a `/cluey-alternative` page - nothing new from them to react to.

### Tutor Doctor Australia

### EzyMaths, Kip McGrath, Apex Tuition, Alchemy Tuition, Tutoring for Excellence, Math Minds

### Success Tutoring

## Patterns across competitors

1. **It was a quiet week.** Of 12 competitors, only Learnmate and North Shore made any material content changes. The bigger players (Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath) sat still.
2. **Learnmate is the only competitor still systematically expanding subject-specific VCE landing pages.** This is now a multi-week trend in our tracking and is the single competitive vector worth watching closely.
3. **North Shore is building lead-capture infrastructure** (trial assessment, term re-enrolment) rather than content. Different play to Learnmate's SEO matrix.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Fill the VCE subject-page gap.** Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring` is a single hub. Learnmate now has dedicated landing pages for individual VCE subjects including Accounting and Economics - both of which are smaller, higher-intent subjects with less competition than Maths or English. Worth considering child pages under `/vce-tutoring/` for at least: Accounting, Economics, Legal Studies, Business Management, Methods, Specialist, Chemistry, Biology, Physics, English. We can out-position Learnmate by including Melbourne-specific signals (ATAR scaling notes, VCAA exam patterns, links to `/atar-planner` and `/worksheets`) rather than the generic "find a tutor" copy they use.
2. **Subject-page angle to beat Learnmate on.** Their pages read as thin marketplace templates. A Tuterly VCE Accounting page that includes a sample study plan, a worksheet preview, and tutor profiles with actual VCE Accounting study scores would be materially stronger than what they shipped.
3. **Free trial / assessment offer.** North Shore is investing in this. Tuterly has `/sample-report` but no obvious free-trial-lesson or free-assessment landing page. If we have any equivalent offer, it deserves its own dedicated page - this is a proven conversion play in the category.
4. **Quiet week = good time to ship.** With Cluey and Tutor Doctor static, any new comparison content or VCE subject pages we publish now will land into a low-noise environment.

## Things to ignore

- North Shore's removed author archive pages - housekeeping, not strategic.
- The Begin Bright fetch failure - retry next week before reading anything into it.
