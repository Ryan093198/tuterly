# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-09

## Headline

Alchemy Tuition has restructured its entire location/subject URL system into a nested hierarchy (e.g. `/english-tutoring/sunshine-coast/noosa/` instead of `/noosa-english-tutoring/`). This is a deliberate taxonomy upgrade - subject → region → suburb - and it's the kind of structure that scales to hundreds of long-tail suburb pages cleanly. If Alchemy pushes this across their full footprint, they'll outrank anyone doing flat suburb pages for "[subject] tutor [suburb]" searches, including several Melbourne suburbs Tuterly cares about (Box Hill, Fairfield, Pakenham, Springvale, Caroline Springs, Derrimut, Aintree).

## Per competitor

### Alchemy Tuition
- 25 new URLs, 23 removed - almost all are the same pages migrated from flat slugs to nested paths. Not new content so much as an information-architecture overhaul.
- New Melbourne suburb pages live: Box Hill, Fairfield, Pakenham, Springvale.
- New VCE Chemistry suburb pages: Aintree, Caroline Springs, Derrimut (plus a Melbourne-wide Chemistry hub). This is a deliberate push into VCE Chemistry at the suburb level - a subject/level combination Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring` page covers generically but not by suburb.

- Expansion beyond Melbourne/Sydney: Wagga Wagga, Townsville, Hobart (Kingston, New Norfolk), Sunshine Coast (Caloundra, Maroochydore, Noosa), Adelaide Hills (Bridgewater). They're building a national suburb grid.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Suburb-level subject pages are the active battleground.** Alchemy is the only mover this week, but they're moving decisively. Flat suburb pages are being replaced with nested subject/region/suburb structures - a sign the format is maturing.
- **VCE Chemistry at suburb granularity** is now contested in Melbourne's west (Aintree, Caroline Springs, Derrimut). Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring` is a single page competing against targeted suburb+subject landings.
- **Year-level hubs** (Alchemy's Year 12, Cluey's Year 7) suggest competitors see parents searching by year, not just subject. Tuterly has no year-level landing pages visible in the top-level paths.
- **Non-student payer segments** (Cluey's grandparents angle, competition giveaways) show competitors experimenting with who writes the cheque, not just who gets tutored.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Build nested VCE subject + suburb pages before Alchemy finishes rolling them out.** Priority: Chemistry, Methods, English in Melbourne's west and east (Box Hill, Caroline Springs, Derrimut, Pakenham). Tuterly has `/vce-tutoring` and `/best-tutoring-melbourne` but nothing at suburb+subject granularity.
2. **Year-level hub pages** - Year 7 (transition), Year 11, Year 12. These are high-intent parent search queries that Tuterly's current taxonomy doesn't catch. The `/atar-planner` is a tool, not a landing page.
3. **Audit `/worksheets` vs. Cluey's `/free-maths-worksheets`.** Cluey is actively promoting theirs as a lead magnet with social-proof copy ("I just downloaded some for my child"). Worth checking whether Tuterly's version converts.
4. **Audit `/directory` vs. Cluey's `/schools`.** If Cluey has built a richer school-by-school guide, it may be pulling parent search traffic Tuterly's directory could capture.
5. **Consider a competition or referral hook.** Cluey's "win a year of tutoring" is a cheap acquisition experiment; marketplaces can run tutor-sponsored variants.
6. **The grandparent payer angle** is a niche but underserved segment - could be a simple landing page off `/parents`.
