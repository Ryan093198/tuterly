# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-09

## Headline

Alchemy Tuition is making a serious push into regional Australia, launching ~25+ new location pages this week covering towns well outside the capital cities (Ballarat, Bathurst, Bunbury, Bundaberg, Coffs Harbour, Geraldton, Griffith, Hervey Bay, Ipswich, Kalgoorlie, Launceston, Mackay, Maitland, Mandurah, Port Lincoln, Port Macquarie, Queanbeyan, Rockingham, Shepparton, Albury, Tamworth, Toowoomba, Warrnambool, Wodonga and more). At the same time they've retired ~180 older subject-plus-suburb pages (e.g. "english-tutoring-box-hill", "brisbane-tutoring-paddington"). This is a deliberate restructure from subject+suburb combos to a cleaner "Tutoring {City}" template, scaled nationally. If they execute, they'll own a lot of regional search intent that no Melbourne-centric player (including us) touches.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Two moves this week, both about scale, not messaging.** Alchemy is scaling locations; Apex is scaling tutor profiles. Nobody launched a new product page, pricing page, or positioning piece.

2. **Regional Australia is being claimed.** Alchemy is explicitly going after towns most urban tutoring brands ignore. There's no equivalent push from Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath or Learnmate.

3. **VCE-branded tutor directories are growing.** Apex now has a visibly larger `/vce-tutors/` roster. Our `/vce-tutoring` and `/directory` pages are competing against a growing supply story on their side.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **VCE tutor directory depth.** Apex is actively adding VCE tutor profiles weekly. If our `/directory` and `/vce-tutoring` pages don't visibly surface tutor counts, named profiles, and recent additions, we look thinner than we are. Worth auditing how many VCE tutors we display vs Apex's growing count.

- **Regional Victoria gap.** Alchemy just launched pages for Ballarat, Bendigo-adjacent markets, Shepparton, Warrnambool, Wodonga, Albury - all Victorian regional centres. We have `/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne` and `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` but nothing for regional VIC. Even a lightweight "Online tutoring for regional Victoria" page tied to our existing online offering would plant a flag before Alchemy's pages gain authority.

- **Comparison page extension.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Apex and Alchemy are now visibly the more active competitors - an `/apex-tuition-alternative` or `/alchemy-alternative` page would catch brand-vs-brand searches these two are generating as they scale.

- **Beat Alchemy's template.** Their location pages are formulaic ("#1 Tutoring {City}! More than 15 years…"). Any Tuterly location page with real local detail (schools, VCE subject coverage, average hourly rates, named local tutors) will out-convert theirs on the same keywords.

- **Tutor profile SEO.** Apex's new tutor pages have no descriptions and generic titles. If our tutor profile pages carry proper titles, subjects, and ATAR/results data, we out-rank them on tutor-name searches by default.
