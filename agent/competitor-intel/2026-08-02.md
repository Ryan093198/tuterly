## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Total publishing standstill.** Seven competitors with working crawls, zero net URL changes between them. If this holds another week it's a genuine pattern worth acting on; if next week snaps back to normal churn, treat this as a one-off.

2. **Four sitemap failures in one week** (Alchemy, Success, Tutoring for Excellence, Math Minds) plus one fetch failure (Begin Bright). That's five of twelve competitors we currently can't see. Worth checking manually next week whether these are persistent misconfigurations - if so, we should switch those competitors to a homepage-diff or crawl-based method rather than sitemap parsing, otherwise we're flying blind on a third of the set indefinitely.

3. **Scale asymmetry unchanged.** Learnmate (6.4k), Cluey (1.7k), Apex (1.7k), North Shore (991) still anchor the top of the URL-volume table. Tutor Doctor and Kip McGrath continue to run lean (<450 URLs) - they're competing on brand and franchise footprint, not content surface area.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because there's nothing new to react to this week, the useful move is offensive rather than defensive:

- **Ship into the quiet.** Any new /learn, /worksheets or suburb-level pages published this week will land into an empty news cycle for the segment. Good week for the ATAR planner or scholarship-test-prep expansions to get a push.

- **Comparison-page coverage gap.** We have /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative live. The next logical additions given competitor visibility are Learnmate and Apex - both are marketplace-model competitors (closer to our positioning than Kip/Tutor Doctor) and both maintain large content footprints. A /learnmate-alternative page in particular is a defensible move given their URL scale suggests strong organic pull.

- **Centres angle underexploited by peers.** None of the tracked competitors moved on centre-facing content this week, and structurally only Kip McGrath and North Shore really speak to a centre/franchise audience at all. Our /centres path remains a differentiated surface - worth pushing more into it while nobody else is.

- **Worksheets remains a moat.** No competitor in this set has a visible worksheets equivalent surfacing in sitemap activity. Continuing to grow /worksheets is uncontested territory.

## What to watch next week

- Whether the four broken sitemaps recover, and whether Begin Bright fetches again.
- Whether the publishing freeze breaks - if Cluey or Learnmate ship a batch of pages after two quiet weeks, that's a signal of a coordinated Term 3 push.
- Any first movement on VCE/HSC-timed content as we approach exam season - none of it appeared this week, which itself is mildly surprising.
