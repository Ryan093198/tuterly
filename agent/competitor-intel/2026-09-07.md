# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-09-07

**Headline:** EzyMaths is aggressively building out a national past-papers and formula-sheet library that now spans VCE, HSC, QCE, WACE and IB - not just maths. This is the only meaningful competitor movement this week, and it's a direct threat to the traffic potential of Tuterly's `/worksheets` and `/learn` sections.

## EzyMaths Tutoring (24 new pages)

All 24 new URLs sit under `/past-papers/` and cluster into three clear plays:

1. **Formula and reference sheets across every state's senior maths curriculum** - HSC Maths reference sheet, HSC Physics and Chemistry formula/data sheets, VCE General/Methods/Specialist formula sheets, QCE General/Methods/Specialist formula books, WACE Methods/Specialist/Applications formula sheets. This is a coordinated national rollout, not a one-off.
2. **Non-maths subject expansion.** Despite the brand name, they've launched pages for HSC CAFS, HSC Investigating Science, VCE Health and Human Development, VCE Food Studies, VCE GAT, and WACE Psychology. Signals a deliberate move beyond maths tutoring positioning.
3. **IB and minimum-standards content.** New IB Maths hub with AA sample papers, AI sample papers, and an "AA vs AI" comparison page. Plus OLNA (WA) and HSC minimum standard practice tests - covering the compulsory literacy/numeracy gate exams that parents anxiously google.

**Opportunities for Tuterly:**
- We have `/vce-tutoring` and `/worksheets` but no equivalent VCE formula-sheet or GAT landing page. These are extremely high-intent, evergreen search targets for Melbourne parents/students.
- VCE Health and Human Development, Food Studies - popular VCE subjects where our `/directory` and subject coverage could add depth beyond generic tutoring pages.
- No one appears to own an "AA vs AI" explainer angled at Australian IB students - EzyMaths is now filling that gap.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Past papers and formula sheets are becoming a battleground.** EzyMaths is the only mover, but they're moving fast and covering all four state senior curricula plus IB in a single sprint. This is a proven top-of-funnel play (high search volume, parent- and student-relevant, evergreen). Tuterly's `/worksheets` needs to decide whether to compete on this or intentionally cede it.
- **Subject breadth vs brand name.** EzyMaths is quietly decoupling from the "maths only" brand. Watch whether the site nav or homepage repositions in coming weeks - if so, that's a clearer strategic signal.
- **Everyone else is quiet.** Nine of eleven trackable competitors published nothing new. September is typically a build-up to end-of-year exam season, so either most competitors are relying on paid/social for the exam push or content pipelines are thin. Either way, there's a window for Tuterly to ship exam-season content with limited direct collision.
