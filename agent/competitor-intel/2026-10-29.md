# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-29

## Headline

Success Tutoring has rebuilt its entire site this week - 8,145 URLs removed and 7,508 new ones added. The old sprawl of individual suburb and tutor pages is gone, replaced by a tighter structure built around state-level centre finders (`/find-a-centre/nsw`, `/vic`, `/qld`, `/sa`, `/act`, `/nt`) and clean service pages for HSC, VCE, QCE and NAPLAN tutoring. This is a serious positioning move - away from "we tutor in [suburb]" SEO toward a national centre-brand with clear senior-years products. Worth watching whether rankings for Melbourne suburb terms open up as a result.

## Per-competitor findings

### Success Tutoring - full site rebuild
New structural pillars:
- **Service pages by curriculum**: `/services/vce`, `/services/hsc-tutoring`, `/services/qce-tutoring`, `/services/naplan-tutoring`. VCE page explicitly covers "your own subjects, SACs and VCE exam preparation" - direct overlap with Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring`.
- **State-level centre hubs** replacing hundreds of suburb pages. Victoria hub is live.
- **About section** expanded: `/about-us/our-story`, `/our-values`, `/our-tutors-and-centre-directors`, `/our-success-team`.
- **Learning hub** repopulated with articles including a direct comparison piece: "Success Tutoring vs Kumon - comparing tutoring programs", plus parent-facing content like "4 smart ways to motivate your child to love school", "6 mind foods to skyrocket student performance", "10 benefits of hiring a tutor".
- Notable removals: dozens of suburb pages (Altona, Baulkham Hills, Baldivis, Auburn, etc.) and promo pages ("65-for-14-days", "6-critical-naplan-secrets-revealed", "4-week-naplan-challenge").

The NAPLAN service page covers Years 3, 5, 7 and 9 - Years 7 and 9 are in Tuterly's core audience.

### North Shore Coaching College - 234 new pages
Mostly a `/news/` archive being surfaced (announcements, term calendars, holiday program listings going back to 2021). Not fresh content, but newly indexed. A few things worth noting:
- Holiday program pages (WA, SA) with scholarship, selective and NAPLAN prep bundles for Years 5-7.
- "Developing a Love of Literature" and "Tuition or a Life?" are evergreen parent-facing pieces.
- No new maths-specific topic guides.

### Learnmate - small cleanup
Three removals only: `/test-3/`, and two online VCE tutor landing pages (`/online-vce-accounting-tutors/`, `/online-vce-economics-tutors/`). Possibly thin-page pruning. No new content.

### Quiet this week
Cluey Learning, Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath, Apex Tuition, Alchemy Tuition, Tutoring for Excellence, Math Minds - no changes.

### Errored
EzyMaths (403 on sitemap) and Begin Bright (fetch failed). Nothing to report.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Centre-brand consolidation**: Success Tutoring's rebuild follows a model North Shore already uses - state hubs and campus pages rather than suburb SEO. If this is where the category is heading, Tuterly's suburb-level pages (`/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne`, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne`) may face less competition from Success in the short term.
2. **Senior-years framing dominates**: Every new service page from Success is Year 11-12 (VCE/HSC/QCE) or NAPLAN. No competitor is publishing dedicated Years 7-10 maths pages or topic guides. This gap continues to be wide open for Tuterly's app positioning.
3. **No new homework/practice tools or apps** from any competitor this week. Success and North Shore remain centre-based; Learnmate remains a marketplace. Tuterly's "snap a wrong answer, practise fresh versions" angle has no direct competitor product to counter it.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Years 7-10 maths topic guides**: still no competitor investing here. Worth pushing out guides on common Year 7-10 topics (linear equations, Pythagoras, algebraic fractions, surds, trig ratios) framed around "common mistakes" - directly matching how the app works.
- **NAPLAN Years 7 and 9 numeracy**: Success Tutoring's new NAPLAN page explicitly targets Years 7 and 9. A Tuterly `/naplan-numeracy` or Year 9 NAPLAN practice page positioning the app as the natural prep tool would compete well and plug a current gap in Tuterly's top-level paths.
- **Kumon comparison angle**: Success is now publicly comparing itself to Kumon. Tuterly already has `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative` - a `/kumon-alternative` page (app-based, no worksheets, no centres, cheaper) would hit the same intent and fill a competitor-named gap.
- **VCE page refresh**: Success's new VCE page leads with "your own subjects, SACs and VCE exam preparation" - worth checking Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring` covers SAC prep as explicitly.
- **Parent-facing content**: Success has repopulated articles aimed at parents (motivation, study habits). Tuterly's `/parents` section could carry equivalent pieces tied to the app's "practise questions you got wrong" workflow - e.g. how to help a Year 8 who keeps making the same algebra mistake.
