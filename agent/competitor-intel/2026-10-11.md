# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-11

## Headline

Cluey Learning has pruned roughly 1,290 URLs from its site this week - about three quarters of its previous indexed footprint - with zero new pages added. This is the kind of cleanup that usually precedes (or accompanies) a major repositioning, platform migration, or strategic retreat. Worth watching closely over the next 2-4 weeks. Given we run a dedicated `/cluey-alternative` page, any shift in how they describe themselves or what they offer directly affects our comparison angle.

## Per competitor

### Cluey Learning
Massive deletion event. The removed URLs include multiple years of "options guide" landing pages (2023, 2024), ATAR quiz pages for parents, feedback/review landing pages (including variants pointed at ProductReview and Trustpilot), English literary techniques content, and a lot of obvious staging duplicates. Current sitemap sits at 412 URLs, down from ~1,700.

Reading between the lines:
- They appear to have cleaned out staging and ToBeDeleted artefacts that had been indexed - basic hygiene.
- But they've also killed the "ATAR quiz for parents" lead-gen funnel and dated enrolment campaign pages. Either they're rebuilding these or stepping back from parent-direct lead capture.
- Removal of the review-platform landing variants (ProductReview, Trustpilot) suggests either consolidation to a single reviews page or a change in review strategy.

**Action for us:** audit our `/cluey-alternative` page against what Cluey currently claims on-site - some of the comparison points may now reference pages that no longer exist. Also worth revisiting whether we want our own ATAR quiz for parents, since they've vacated that ground (our `/atar-planner` is adjacent but not the same thing).

### Learnmate
Two new tutor landing pages: **Online VCE Economics** and **Online VCE Accounting**. Both follow the same template as their broader subject directory. These are commerce subjects where tutor supply is thinner and parent willingness-to-pay is high (ATAR scaling, business-career framing).

**Gap for us:** our `/vce-tutoring` page is a single hub. Learnmate is clearly building out subject-level landing pages one by one. VCE Economics and VCE Accounting are two subjects where we should check whether we have dedicated pages or tutor coverage. If not, these are quick wins.

### Alchemy Tuition
Renamed `/parentfeedback/` to `/reviews/`. Same content concept, cleaner URL, and the new page copy explicitly leans on "thousands of VERIFIED reviews from local families." They're sharpening their social-proof positioning.

**Takeaway for us:** worth checking how our `/sample-report` and parent-facing pages present social proof. "Verified reviews from local families" is a strong trust angle for a marketplace competitor.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Review and trust pages are being consolidated.** Both Cluey (removing ProductReview/Trustpilot variants) and Alchemy (parentfeedback → reviews) tidied up their social-proof surface area this week. Independent moves, same direction: one canonical reviews page with strong trust language. We should make sure we have one obvious destination for parent reviews.

2. **VCE subject-level landing pages are still a live frontier.** Learnmate is the only one shipping new pages this week, and they're all VCE subject pages. We have `/vce-tutoring` as a hub but no visible subject-level depth in the paths you shared. This is the clearest content gap.

3. **Nobody shipped pricing or positioning changes.** No new pricing pages, no new "why us" pages, no new location pages across the set. Our `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` and `/best-tutoring-melbourne` pages remain relatively uncontested on direct equivalents from these competitors this week.

## Suggested next moves

- Monitor Cluey daily for the next fortnight - a deletion wave this large usually means something is coming.
- Draft VCE subject landing pages starting with Economics, Accounting, and whichever other VCE subjects we have strong tutor coverage in. Learnmate is building the shelf; we should be on it.
- Review our `/cluey-alternative` copy against Cluey's current (smaller) site so our comparison claims remain accurate.
