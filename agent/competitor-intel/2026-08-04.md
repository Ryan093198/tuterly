# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-08-04

## Top priority this week

EzyMaths has quietly launched a **research section** with a flagship "State of Tutoring Australia 2026" report. This is the most strategically significant move in the dataset: a competitor is positioning itself as the industry data authority, which is a credibility play that earns backlinks, media mentions and trust with parents making a high-stakes decision. ## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Term 3 seasonality is a live battleground.** Learnmate has three separate landing pages leaning on the "trial exams starting August, finals approaching" narrative. Nobody else is doing this in the dataset. Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring` and `/atar-planner` pages could carry a seasonal overlay (dated banner, term-specific CTA) without needing new URLs.

2. **Credentialing beyond just "find a tutor."** EzyMaths is going the research/authority route; North Shore is running competition-prep events (AMC). Both are signals-of-quality plays. Tuterly's `/sample-report` is directionally similar but sits alone - there's room to build a small authority layer (data report, competition prep, scholarship stats).

3. **Curriculum-specific micro-pages.** Learnmate's BSSS French page shows they're systematically covering non-VCE/HSC senior systems (ACT BSSS). Tuterly's directory and VCE focus is Melbourne-strong, but if we ever expand nationally, BSSS, SACE, QCE and WACE landing pages are cheap wins competitors are already claiming.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Publish a data-backed report before EzyMaths owns the "state of tutoring" narrative.** Tuterly's marketplace data (tutor supply, pricing spread, subject demand in Melbourne) is more defensible than a small tutoring provider's dataset.
- **Add a term-timed landing page or seasonal module** on `/vce-tutoring` and `/atar-planner` targeting the "Term 3 / trial exams / SAC" moment - Learnmate is capturing this intent unopposed right now.
- **Consider an AMC / competition prep angle.** North Shore is treating it as an event. Tuterly's `/scholarship-test-prep` page is the natural home to expand into AMC, ICAS and similar - competitors are validating the demand.
- **Watch Learnmate's `/home-3-0/`** - if this goes live as their new homepage, the positioning and hero copy will be the clearest read on how they're pitching against marketplaces like us.
