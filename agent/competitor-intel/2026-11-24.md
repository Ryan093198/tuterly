## Patterns across competitors

1. **Very low publishing volume.** Only one competitor shipped substantive new content this week. Late November is likely a seasonal lull before Term 1 / back-to-school pushes in January.
2. **Worksheets are re-emerging as a content format.** Apex is the second competitor in recent memory to invest in a free worksheets hub. This is a format Tuterly already owns a URL for (/worksheets) but, strategically, static worksheets are the exact problem the Tuterly app solves - one-shot practice with no feedback loop. There's a positioning wedge here: "worksheets are dead - snap the one you got wrong and get infinite fresh versions."
3. **Success stories keep outperforming topic content** in what competitors choose to publish (two of Apex's three new pages are ATAR/HSC results stories). We have /sample-report but no visible student outcome narratives.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Audit Apex's new worksheets page this week.** If it ranks for generic "Year 7 maths worksheets" / "Year 8 algebra worksheets" terms, we need our /worksheets hub broken out by year and topic to compete, with the app as the "next step up from static PDFs" CTA.
- **Years 7-10 maths blog gap remains wide open.** None of the 12 competitors shipped a Years 7-10 maths topic guide, common-mistakes post, or test-prep piece this week. The content lane is uncontested right now - a good window to publish foundational topic guides (linear equations, Pythagoras, surds, index laws, etc.) each tied to a "snap a question you got wrong" app CTA.
- **No competitor shipped an app or homework/practice tool this week.** Tuterly's product differentiation (iPhone app, snap-to-practise) is still unmatched in this set. Lean into it in homepage and category-page messaging while the field is static.
- **Student outcome stories.** Apex is building a library of these. We could do the equivalent for app users - "how [student] went from 55% to 80% on their Year 9 maths test by re-practising every wrong question" - which doubles as app marketing.

## What to watch next week
- Whether Apex expands the worksheets hub into a series (year-level or topic landing pages).
- First signs of back-to-school / Term 1 content from Cluey, Kip McGrath and Success Tutoring - historically the heaviest publishers.
- Whether EzyMaths' sitemap block is permanent.
