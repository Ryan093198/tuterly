## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Total publishing freeze.** Zero new or removed URLs across seven readable competitors in one week is anomalous. Most likely explanations: (a) early July school holidays slowing content ops, (b) sitemaps cached at the CDN. Flag for next week - if the freeze continues, it's a real signal; if next week shows a burst, this week was just a lag.

2. **Two very different scale tiers.** Learnmate (6.4k) and then Cluey/Apex (~1.7k each) sit far above the centre-based and specialist players (300-450). Tuterly's directory and worksheet paths are the mechanisms that would move us into the top tier over time.

3. **Sitemap hygiene is patchy across the sector.** Nearly half our target list can't be read reliably. Not directly useful competitively, but it means our own sitemap discipline is probably already better than most - a small SEO edge worth preserving.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because there's nothing new to react to this week, these are structural observations rather than fresh gaps:

- **Learnmate's scale gap.** They have ~6.4k URLs largely on the strength of programmatic tutor and subject-suburb pages. Our `/directory`, `/tutors`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne` and `/best-tutoring-melbourne` paths are the equivalent surface. Worth auditing whether we're generating suburb- and subject-level combinations at anywhere near their density, and if not, whether we should.

- **Comparison pages.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. We do not appear to have equivalents for Learnmate (our closest marketplace competitor) or Apex Tuition (comparable scale to Cluey). Given Learnmate's footprint, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is probably the most valuable single page we could add.

- **Centre-based positioning.** Kip McGrath and (when we can read it) Begin Bright compete on the centre model. Our `/centres` path addresses this but we have no dedicated Kip McGrath comparison page. Lower priority than Learnmate but worth queuing.

## Recommended action this week

Do nothing reactive - there's nothing to react to. Use the quiet week to (a) scope a `/learnmate-alternative` page, and (b) decide whether to build a fallback crawler for the five competitors whose sitemaps are unreadable.
