# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-14

## Headline

**Apex Tuition has publicly launched a dedicated IB tutor offering, publishing 132 new pages this week - the bulk of which are individual IB tutor profiles across Sydney, Brisbane and online.** This is a clear positioning move into the International Baccalaureate market, a segment Tuterly does not currently address on its site. Also notable: Alchemy Tuition quietly launched a Melbourne Maths landing page, putting them in direct competition with Tuterly's core geography and subject focus.

## Per competitor

### Apex Tuition Australia
Massive expansion into IB. The 132 new URLs are overwhelmingly `/ib-tutors/[name]` profile pages covering a wide subject range: IB Chinese, Spanish, French, Economics, Business Management, History, Music, Geography, Sports and Exercise Science, plus generic "Academic IB Tutor" profiles. Locations include Carlton, Strathfield, Burwood, Vaucluse, Parramatta, St Lucia, Carindale - so this is a national push, not just Sydney. One new blog post ("How I Scored 92 in HSC English Advanced") continues their student-voice/testimonial content angle.

Implication: Apex is building out an IB directory structure very similar in shape to what Tuterly does for VCE. If IB enrolments are a growth lane, Tuterly has no equivalent `/ib-tutoring` entry point.

### Alchemy Tuition
One new page but a strategically important one: `/maths-tutoring/melbourne/`, positioned as "#1 Maths Tutoring" with in-home and online. This is a direct subject + city combination that overlaps with `/online-tutoring-melbourne` and `/best-tutoring-melbourne` but attacks the maths-specific intent that Tuterly doesn't own with a dedicated page.

### Tutor Doctor Australia
Nine new blog posts, three removed. Content is parent/student wellbeing and study-skills focused rather than product-led:
- NAIDOC Week explainer
- Mindful habits for school stress
- Student procrastination
- Proofreading checklist
- Roald Dahl Day / reading engagement
- Supporting HSC students without adding pressure
- Winter holiday focus-building activities
- Reading confidence and academic performance
- Smart ways to use AI for revision

They removed older posts on part-time jobs and NAPLAN anxiety. The AI-for-study post and the HSC parent-support post are the two with real strategic weight - both are angles parents are actively searching for.

### North Shore Coaching College
Three new pages, two of which are a "Starter Pack Victoria" promotion plus its T&Cs. Worth watching: this looks like a Victoria-market acquisition offer (likely a discounted trial bundle). North Shore are better known for NSW; a Victorian starter pack suggests they're pushing into Tuterly's home market.

### Cluey Learning
No changes this week (1,702 URLs stable). Worth noting given Tuterly has a `/cluey-alternative` page - no new Cluey positioning to react to.

### Kip McGrath, Learnmate, Success Tutoring, Tutoring for Excellence, Math Minds
No changes.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **IB is becoming a contested category.** Apex's 132-page launch is the clearest signal yet that IB tutoring has enough demand to justify dedicated infrastructure. No other competitor moved on this, which means first-mover advantage is still available for Tuterly if the market fit is there.

2. **Victoria-specific landing pages from non-Victorian players.** Both Alchemy (Melbourne maths) and North Shore (Victoria starter pack) made Victoria-targeted moves this week. Tuterly's home-market moat is being actively tested.

3. **Content is drifting toward wellbeing and AI.** Tutor Doctor's publishing pattern - stress, procrastination, mindful habits, AI-for-study - reflects where parent anxiety is heading. Nobody else is publishing in this space right now.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **IB tutoring page.** Apex has validated the demand with volume. A `/ib-tutoring` hub plus individual tutor profiles would be a natural extension of the existing VCE + directory model.
- **Dedicated subject-in-city pages.** Alchemy's `/maths-tutoring/melbourne/` fills a gap Tuterly's current structure doesn't - subject + city combinations beneath the general Melbourne pages. Maths, English, and Chemistry Melbourne pages would be a defensible build.
- **AI-for-study content.** Parents are searching for guidance on how students should and shouldn't use AI. Tuterly has `/learn` and `/parents` as natural homes for a sharper, more opinionated take than Tutor Doctor's piece.
- **HSC/VCE parent-support angle.** Tutor Doctor's "support students without adding pressure" is HSC-focused. Tuterly could own the VCE equivalent, tied to the `/atar-planner` tool.
- **Monitor North Shore's Victorian starter pack** - if it's a discounted trial, it may show up in paid channels and reshape price expectations in Melbourne.
