## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Total publishing freeze.** Nine competitors returned clean data and not one shipped or removed a page this week. Mid-July in Australia is school holidays, which likely explains it - families aren't shopping, so marketing teams aren't launching. Expect a surge late July / early August as Term 3 begins.

2. **Two sitemaps now blocking us (415).** Success Tutoring and Tutoring for Excellence both return HTTP 415 on `/sitemap.xml`. That's unusual and consistent across two sites - possibly a shared host or WAF rule. Worth engineering time to fix the crawler (try different Accept headers or fall back to HTML crawl) so we don't lose visibility on two direct competitors indefinitely.

3. **Size disparity is the real story.** Learnmate (6,418), Alchemy (2,691), Cluey (1,703), and Apex (1,698) dwarf the specialists. The two biggest are marketplace/directory models - the same category as Tuterly. Our `/directory` needs to be the priority surface if we're competing on that axis.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's no new competitor content to react to, these are gaps I've noticed comparing their known structures to our top-level paths:

- **Centres angle is under-exploited by competitors.** We have `/centres` as a top-level path; none of the marketplace competitors (Learnmate, Alchemy, Apex) appear to lean on physical/centre partnerships as a distinct product. This is a real wedge - worth investing in more centre-facing content and case studies while the field is empty.
- **ATAR planner is genuinely differentiated.** No competitor in this set has a tool-style page comparable to our `/atar-planner`. It's a lead-gen asset none of them are matching. Consider whether we're promoting it hard enough on parent-facing pages.
- **Worksheets as a free asset.** Our `/worksheets` path has no obvious parallel at Learnmate or Alchemy. Kip McGrath and Begin Bright historically lean on printable/practice material - if we're not surfacing worksheets prominently to organic search, we're leaving a moat unbuilt.
- **Scholarship test prep.** We have `/scholarship-test-prep`; North Shore is the obvious competitor here given their selective/scholarship positioning. Worth a manual review of their scholarship pages to make sure ours is stronger on price transparency and outcomes.
- **Melbourne-specific pages.** We have four Melbourne-focused pages (best-tutoring, prices, VCE, online). None of the national players in this set match that local depth. Keep pressing this advantage - a Melbourne suburb-level expansion under `/tutoring` would be low-risk given the quiet field.
