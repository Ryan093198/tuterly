A genuinely quiet week. Across nine competitors we could successfully crawl, zero new pages were published and zero were removed. No pricing pages changed, no new service lines appeared, no new location or subject pages went live. ## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Total publishing freeze.** Nine crawlable competitors, zero net changes. Either we are mid-school-term lull (end of May, pre-exam crunch where marketing teams go quiet) or our diff window caught a flat week. Either way, anything Tuterly ships in the next fortnight lands into a quiet feed.

2. **Three competitors have broken or misconfigured sitemaps** (Alchemy, Math Minds, Success Tutoring effectively). This is a small but real SEO hygiene gap on their end - their newer pages may not be getting indexed efficiently. Not directly exploitable, but worth remembering when assessing their organic reach.

3. **Scale gap remains structural.** Learnmate at 6.3k URLs and Cluey at 1.7k continue to dwarf the rest. Tuterly's competitive lane against them is depth of Melbourne-specific and VCE-specific content, not URL count.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because there is no new competitor content to react to, the useful exercise this week is gap analysis against Tuterly's existing top-level paths. Reviewing those, a few persistent gaps relative to what these competitors typically rank for:

- **Year-level landing pages** (e.g. Year 7-12 tutoring, primary tutoring). Tuterly has subject and location pages but no clear year-level entry points visible in the top-level paths. Cluey and North Shore both lean on these heavily.
- **Subject-specific Melbourne pages beyond VCE.** Tuterly has /vce-tutoring and /online-tutoring-melbourne but not, for instance, maths-tutoring-melbourne or english-tutoring-melbourne as distinct landing pages. These are bread-and-butter for EzyMaths and Apex.
- **NAPLAN and selective-schools content.** /scholarship-test-prep covers part of this, but a dedicated NAPLAN page is a standard competitor asset (North Shore, Begin Bright historically).
- **Comparison pages.** Tuterly already does this well with /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative. Worth extending to a Learnmate-alternative page given Learnmate is the closest marketplace model and the largest by URL count.
- **Parent-facing guides.** /parents and /learn exist, but none of the competitors are actively publishing here right now - a good moment to push thought-leadership content (cost guides, how-to-choose-a-tutor) while the feed is quiet.

## Recommended action

Treat this as a build week, not a react week. The Learnmate-alternative page and a maths-tutoring-melbourne page are the two highest-leverage additions given the current competitive set.
