# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-06-15

## Headline

Learnmate is the only competitor that moved this week, and it moved hard: 99 new pages added in a single week, all part of a programmatic "city × subject × level" landing page build-out. Every other tracked competitor was static. If Learnmate keeps this pace, they'll own the long tail of "[subject] tutor [Australian city/suburb]" search intent across Sydney, Brisbane, Adelaide and Perth within a quarter - territory Tuterly currently has almost no equivalent pages for outside Melbourne.

## Per-competitor findings

### Learnmate - 99 new pages
All new URLs follow a `/cm-[subject/level]-tutor-[location]/` pattern with near-identical copy ("Looking for X? Learnmate connects you with 700+ tutors..."). Three clear axes of expansion:

- **Geographic push outside Melbourne.** Heavy Sydney coverage including suburb-level granularity: Eastern Suburbs Sydney (maths, English), Inner West Sydney (maths, English), Sydney South (maths). Brisbane, Adelaide and Perth also picked up dedicated "Find A Tutor [city]" hubs plus subject pages.
- **Curriculum-specific pages.** New IB English Tutor Sydney, IB Maths Tutor Sydney, ATAR English Tutor Perth, Maths Methods Tutor Brisbane, Year 12 English Tutor Brisbane. They're staking ground on every state's senior curriculum, not just VCE.
- **Niche learner segments.** Dyslexia Tutoring (national, no city), Homeschool Tutor Brisbane, Reading Tutor Brisbane, Primary School Maths Tutor Adelaide, IELTS Private Tutor Sydney, Piano Tutor Adelaide, Spanish Tutor Sydney.

The "700+ tutors" claim is now baked into every new page's meta description - a deliberate marketplace-size signal.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **One mover, ten sitters.** The static-versus-Learnmate contrast is stark. Most established tutoring brands (Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath, North Shore) are not iterating on their public site at all right now. Learnmate is the only player actively building inventory.
- **Marketplace vs. franchise positioning is widening.** Learnmate is leaning into "700+ tutors, top students and qualified teachers" as a marketplace pitch on every page. Franchise/centre brands aren't responding with equivalent volume or specificity.
- **Suburb-level targeting is now a thing.** "Eastern Suburbs Sydney", "Inner West Sydney", "Sydney South" - Learnmate is going one level deeper than city. No one else is doing this in the tracked set.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Suburb-level Melbourne pages.** Learnmate is publishing Eastern Suburbs Sydney and Inner West Sydney pages but doesn't appear to have done the Melbourne equivalents yet. Tuterly already owns `/best-tutoring-melbourne` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` - the obvious next step is suburb cuts (Inner East, Bayside, North, etc.) before Learnmate gets there. This is a home-turf defence move.
2. **Special-needs and learning-difference content.** Learnmate's new "Dyslexia Tutoring" page is a national, single-topic hub. Tuterly has nothing on dyslexia, ADHD, or learning differences in its top-level paths. This is a high-intent, underserved parent segment and fits naturally under `/parents`.
3. **Homeschooling.** "Homeschool Tutor Brisbane" suggests Learnmate sees demand here. Tuterly has no homeschool angle. A Melbourne-focused homeschool tutor page would be quick to ship.
4. **Curriculum breadth beyond VCE.** Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring` is strong, but as families relocate or look online, IB and ATAR (non-VCE state) intent exists. Worth at least one IB hub given Learnmate is now building IB English and IB Maths pages.
5. **Language and music.** Spanish (Sydney) and Piano (Adelaide) pages show Learnmate is willing to chase non-academic verticals. Probably not a near-term priority for Tuterly, but useful context if/when expanding the `/directory` taxonomy.
6. **Improve on Learnmate's angle.** Their pages are thin and templated - identical body copy with a swapped subject/city. Tuterly's existing alternative pages (`/cluey-alternative`, `/tutor-doctor-alternative`) and `/sample-report` show capacity for differentiated content. Any suburb or niche page Tuterly ships should include real tutor counts, sample pricing, and a Melbourne-specific angle Learnmate can't match from a generic template.
