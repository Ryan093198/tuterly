## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because there's no competitor movement to counter, the opportunity is offensive rather than defensive:

- **Own the quiet.** Mid-August is the run-up to VCE trial exams and Term 4 planning. None of the tracked competitors published anything this week aimed at that window. Tuterly's `/atar-planner` and `/vce-tutoring` pages are natural anchors - a push on trial-exam prep content, or a timely update to the ATAR planner, would land into an empty field.

- **Scholarship test season.** We have `/scholarship-test-prep` but no competitor in the set appears to have refreshed anything around selective/scholarship testing this week. If our page hasn't been updated in a while, now is a low-noise moment to rework it.

- **Comparison pages.** We already run `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate is the closest marketplace analogue and has the largest content footprint, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is an obvious gap. Same logic, weaker case, for Apex and Alchemy given their size.

- **Centres angle.** Kip McGrath, North Shore, and Begin Bright are the centre-based operators in the set. Tuterly's `/centres` path suggests we address this audience - worth checking whether we have direct comparison content against the centre model (flexibility, cost, one-to-one vs group), since none of them are publishing on this axis right now.

- **Worksheets.** Our `/worksheets` path is a content asset none of the tracked competitors appear to match at scale. A week with no competitive noise is a good week to expand it.

## Recommended actions

1. Fix or replace the crawl for Begin Bright, Success Tutoring, and Tutoring for Excellence before next week's report.
