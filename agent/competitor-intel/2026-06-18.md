# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-06-18

## Headline observation

A genuinely quiet week. Across the seven competitors whose sitemaps fetched cleanly, **zero new URLs and zero removed URLs** were detected. No new landing pages, no new programs, no pricing pages, no blog posts. The other five competitors errored out and tell us nothing this cycle.

This is unusual enough to be worth flagging on its own: either the major players are in a content freeze (end-of-financial-year, mid-Term 2 lull), or our sitemap snapshots are stable against last week's baseline. Worth watching whether the same flatline holds next week before drawing conclusions.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Content freeze across the board.** Seven working competitors, zero net page changes. Either coincidence or a genuine mid-year slowdown. Track again next week.
- **Footprint disparity remains the structural story.** Learnmate (6,430) and Cluey (1,703) and Apex (1,697) dominate URL count. The franchise/centre players - Kip McGrath (324), Tutor Doctor (389), EzyMaths (439) - run far leaner sites. Tuterly's marketplace ambitions put us in the Learnmate/Apex bracket structurally, so that's the volume benchmark.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because nothing new shipped this week, there are no fresh content angles to react to. But the sitemap totals still surface some standing gaps worth revisiting:

- **Learnmate's 6,430-URL footprint** almost certainly includes deep subject × suburb and tutor profile pages we don't match at scale. Our /directory and /tutoring paths are the equivalent surface - worth a manual audit of how their directory pages are structured versus ours.
- **No competitor shipped an ATAR-planning tool this week** (or any week we've tracked). Our /atar-planner remains a differentiated asset. Worth more inbound links and promotion rather than assuming it's discoverable.
- **Comparison pages** (/cluey-alternative, /tutor-doctor-alternative) - neither Cluey nor Tutor Doctor moved this week, so our positioning claims on those pages don't need refreshing yet. Schedule a quarterly accuracy check rather than weekly.
- **Worksheets** (/worksheets) - none of the working competitors appear to be expanding free resource libraries this week. If we want to own the "free practice material" angle in Melbourne, the lane is open.

## Recommended actions

1. Fix the sitemap fetcher for the 415 errors (Success Tutoring, Tutoring for Excellence) - likely a header fix.
2. Investigate why Alchemy and Math Minds return zero URLs - they may have moved to a non-standard sitemap location.
3. If next week is also flat across the board, treat that as a signal to push our own content cadence harder - the window is open.
