# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-26

## Headline

Cluey Learning has pulled roughly 1,290 URLs from its sitemap this week with zero additions. That's not a theme tweak - it's a structural purge. Among the removed pages are long-standing assets like the ATAR quiz for parents, the annual "options guide" series (2023 and 2024 editions plus staging variants), English literary techniques content, and multiple feedback/review landing pages. Either Cluey is mid-migration to a new site structure, or they're deliberately shrinking their content footprint. Worth watching closely over the next 2-3 weeks to see what (if anything) comes back.

## Per-competitor findings

### Cluey Learning
- 412 URLs remaining, down from ~1,700. 1,290 removals, zero new pages.
- Notable removed topics: the "ATAR quiz for parents" tool, "2023/2024 options guide" (looks like a Year 10 subject selection asset), English literary techniques resource, and all variants of their feedback/review pages (productreview, trustpilot).
- The staging-URL cleanup suggests at least part of this is sitemap hygiene rather than genuine deprecation. But the live `/atar-quiz-for-parents` and `/english-literary-techniques` disappearing is substantive.
- Opportunity: Tuterly's `/atar-planner` is directly adjacent to what Cluey just removed. If their ATAR quiz is genuinely gone, there's a vacuum in parent-facing ATAR decision tools we could own. Also worth refreshing our `/cluey-alternative` page to reflect any positioning shifts once their rebuild lands.

### North Shore Coaching College
- Three new pages, all WA-focused and all oriented around 2027: a January holiday program (listed both as news and as a tutoring/exam-prep page) and a 2027 important exam dates page covering GATE, NAPLAN, UCAT, and ATAR.
- Removed: the WA 2026 October holiday program (natural end-of-cycle churn).
- Pattern: they publish next-year holiday programs in late October, roughly 2+ months ahead. Their exam-dates page is a useful parent anchor - it bundles four big exams on one page.
- Opportunity: Tuterly has `/scholarship-test-prep` but no equivalent "key exam dates" hub for VIC families (VCE, NAPLAN, scholarship, selective entry). This is a low-effort, high-utility page type that pulls organic traffic every January.

### Apex Tuition Australia
- One new blog post: "How I Scored 92 in WACE Physics." Student-voice case study format. Apex continues to publish WA-specific state-exam content.
- Opportunity: Tuterly has very little first-person student-score-story content. For VCE specifically, a "How I scored 45 in [subject]" series would map naturally onto `/vce-tutoring` and feed `/learn`.

### Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath, Learnmate, Alchemy, Success Tutoring, Tutoring for Excellence, Math Minds
- No changes this week.
- Learnmate (6,430 URLs) and Success Tutoring (8,150 URLs) continue to dwarf everyone else on raw page count - mostly tutor profile and location pages. Nothing new to react to, but their directory scale remains the structural moat to watch.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **State-exam vertical content is where everyone active is investing.** Apex is doing WACE Physics; North Shore is doing WA 2027 exam dates and GATE/UCAT/NAPLAN/ATAR. Nobody meaningful published national or VIC-specific this week. Tuterly's VCE focus is a defensible wedge, but we're not pushing it hard enough in new content.

2. **Holiday program pages are seasonal and time-sensitive.** North Shore publishes next-cycle holiday programs ~2 months out and retires the previous one. If Tuterly ever runs holiday-period campaigns (even partner-tutor-driven), the mid-October to early-November window is when competitors are staking their claim.

3. **The ATAR-decision parent audience is being reshuffled.** Cluey has quietly deleted their ATAR quiz for parents. North Shore is bundling ATAR into an exam-dates reference page. There's no clear owner of "parent-facing ATAR planning tool" right now - and Tuterly already has `/atar-planner`. Worth considering a push: parent-targeted landing page, maybe a quiz or year-10 subject-selection angle, promoted during the Nov-Jan planning season.

## Suggested actions

- **This month:** Build a VIC-equivalent "2027 key exam dates" hub (VCE, NAPLAN, selective, scholarship). Low effort, evergreen, fills a gap in our site structure.
- **Watch Cluey weekly** for the next 3-4 cycles. If 1,290 URLs stay gone, their SEO footprint collapses and we should refresh `/cluey-alternative` and bid harder on their branded alternative queries.
- **Consider a "VCE student score story" content series** for `/learn` - mirrors what Apex is doing in WA, but in our home market.
