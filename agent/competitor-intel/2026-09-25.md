# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-09-25

## Headline

North Shore Coaching College has undergone a major site restructure this week - 472 new URLs, with a heavy build-out of state-specific exam prep (VCE, QCE, WACE/ATAR) and holiday programs across year levels and states. This is the most strategically significant move of the week and directly overlaps with Tuterly's VCE and ATAR positioning.

## North Shore Coaching College

Large-scale expansion, though the crawl only returned titles for a handful of the 472 new pages. The visible structure reveals the intent:

- **State-based exam prep hubs**: `/tutoring/exam-preparation/vic-vce/`, `/qld-qce/`, `/wa-wce-exam-prep/`. They are systematically covering every state's senior credential, not just NSW HSC.
- **Holiday program pages by year level and state**: e.g. Year 6/7/8/9 holiday VIC, QLD Jan/Apr variants, kindergarten prep holiday. This is a granular seasonal funnel Tuterly does not run.
- **Year-level "future writer" streams** (Year 7 and general term tutoring), suggesting a productised writing offering.
- Homepage messaging now foregrounds OC, Selective, HSC, VCE, ATAR, Gifted & Talented, and UCAT together - positioning as a full academic-selection funnel.
- E-commerce infrastructure appears active: `/shop/`, `/registration-checkout/`, `/transactions/`, `/online-payment/` all newly indexed.
- Removed: the eight `/news/state/*` archive pages (housekeeping, not strategic).

**Opportunity for Tuterly**: North Shore's holiday program pages by year level are a topic Tuterly does not address. School holiday intensives are a proven parent search driver in Melbourne. Also worth noting: Tuterly's VCE presence is a single page (`/vce-tutoring`), whereas competitors are increasingly building state × year × format matrices.

## Alchemy Tuition

127 new pages and 127 removed - this is a URL structure migration, not new content. They've moved from flat `/tutoring-suburb/` slugs to a nested `/tutoring/sydney/suburb/` pattern. Same suburb pages (Dulwich Hill, Dundas, Dural, Eastwood, Emu Plains, etc.), same "#1 Tutoring [Suburb]" template, same K-12 pitch.

**Implication**: Alchemy is doubling down on Sydney suburb SEO and cleaning up their URL taxonomy to allow city-level segmentation (implying Melbourne or Brisbane sub-trees may follow). Tuterly's `/directory` and `/best-tutoring-melbourne` pages compete in this space for Melbourne - the nested pattern is worth watching if they extend it.

## Learnmate

Two new pages, both online VCE subject tutor pages:
- `/tutors/online-vce-economics-tutors/`
- `/tutors/online-vce-accounting-tutors/`

They are steadily filling out their VCE-subject × online matrix. Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne` pages are single generic hubs; Learnmate is winning on long-tail subject-specific intent (VCE Economics, VCE Accounting).

**Opportunity for Tuterly**: A per-VCE-subject page set (Methods, Specialist, Chemistry, Physics, Biology, English, Economics, Accounting, Legal) would close a clear gap. Learnmate now has this footprint at scale (6,432 total URLs) and is still adding.

## Apex Tuition Australia

One new page: `/resources/year-10-maths-worksheets-free-worksheets-answers`. A worksheet resource for Year 10 maths with answers included.

**Relevance to Tuterly**: This overlaps directly with `/worksheets`. Apex is bundling worksheets + answer keys as a lead magnet at year-level granularity. Worth checking whether Tuterly's worksheets library covers Year 10 maths with answers and how discoverable those are.

## No changes this week

Cluey Learning, Tutor Doctor Australia, EzyMaths, Kip McGrath, Success Tutoring, Tutoring for Excellence, and Math Minds published nothing new and removed nothing. Begin Bright's site failed to fetch - retry next week.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **State-by-state senior exam prep is the growth area**. North Shore is building VCE/QCE/WACE hubs; Learnmate is building VCE-subject pages. Tuterly currently has one VCE page and no equivalent for interstate credentials - defensible if the strategy is Melbourne-only, but the competitive weight on VCE-subject long-tail is increasing.

2. **Suburb-page arms race continues**. Alchemy's migration to `/tutoring/sydney/suburb/` signals ongoing investment in local SEO. Tuterly's Melbourne-suburb coverage via `/directory` and `/best-tutoring-melbourne` should be audited against how Alchemy structures theirs.

3. **Holiday programs are a gap**. North Shore is the only competitor actively building holiday-program landing pages, and they're doing so by year × state. Tuterly has no holiday-tutoring content - this is a low-competition, seasonal opportunity, especially heading into summer.

4. **Free worksheet resources with answer keys** (Apex's move) reinforce that year-level maths worksheet pages remain a viable acquisition channel. Tuterly already owns `/worksheets` - worth stress-testing the depth of that library against year-level searches.
