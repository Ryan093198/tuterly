## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Total publishing standstill.** Ten sites, zero deltas. Either our diffing is missing something subtle (worth a sanity check on the crawler) or the sector is genuinely idle in late August. Given this is mid-Term 3 in Victoria, that tracks - most operators are in delivery mode, not marketing mode.
2. **Size disparity is the real story.** Learnmate (6,424), Alchemy (2,693), Cluey (1,703) and Apex (1,698) sit in a different league to Kip McGrath (324), Tutoring for Excellence (203) and Math Minds (66). The big four are winning on programmatic surface area - tutor profiles, subject × location pages, or blog depth. Tuterly's own top-level structure is lean (16 paths shown). The gap isn't strategy, it's page volume.
3. **Two "alternative" competitors, no defensive moves.** Cluey and Tutor Doctor - the two brands Tuterly has explicit /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative pages targeting - have shipped nothing. No counter-positioning, no pricing updates. Our comparison pages continue to run unchallenged.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Push while they're quiet.** With no competitor launches this week, any new landing page or worksheet drop from us has an uncontested window. Prioritise content already in the pipeline.
- **Programmatic subject × location gap.** Tuterly has /vce-tutoring, /online-tutoring-melbourne and /best-tutoring-melbourne but no visible per-subject Melbourne pages (e.g. VCE Maths Methods tutoring Melbourne, VCE English tutoring Melbourne). Learnmate and Alchemy's URL counts strongly suggest this is where they get discovery volume. Even 20-30 well-built subject pages would meaningfully close the surface-area gap.
- **Year-level pages missing.** No /year-11, /year-12, /year-7-tutoring style paths in Tuterly's top level. Cluey and Kip McGrath structure heavily around year levels - it's how parents search.
- **Scholarship prep is a solo lane.** Tuterly's /scholarship-test-prep doesn't appear to have a direct equivalent in the sample of competitors we track. Worth investing in - selective/scholarship parents are high-intent and price-insensitive.
- **/atar-planner and /sample-report are genuinely differentiated.** Nothing in competitor sitemaps suggests interactive planners or sample reports. These are worth featuring more prominently in acquisition, not just as utility pages.
- **Begin Bright and North Shore are blind spots.** Two consecutive failures would justify a manual review to make sure we're not missing a repositioning.

Ship one new subject × Melbourne landing page and one year-level page. Uncontested week, low risk, compounding benefit.
