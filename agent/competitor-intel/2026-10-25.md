# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-25

## Headline

North Shore Coaching College has launched a 205-page location rollout in a single week - a nationwide suburb-level land grab spanning Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Canberra and regional NSW. This is the only material competitor move this week, and it directly targets local-intent searches ("tutoring in [suburb]") that Tuterly currently addresses only at a city level (Melbourne) and via the generic directory.

## North Shore Coaching College

North Shore added at least 205 new `/location/[suburb]/` pages. The sample alone covers:

- **Melbourne**: Brighton, Burwood (listed twice as `/burwood/` and `/vicburwood/`, suggesting a disambiguation with NSW Burwood), Bundoora, Craigieburn, South Morang, Springvale
- **Sydney**: Ashfield, Chatswood, Surry Hills, Wentworthville, Campbelltown, Burwood
- **Brisbane/QLD**: Indooroopilly, MacGregor, Robertson, Kenmore, Mango Hill, Wavell Heights, Forest Lake, Kelvin Grove
- **Perth**: Carine, Atwell, Rossmoyne, Shenton
- **Canberra**: Belconnen

Page titles are bare ("Suburb | North Shore Coaching College") with no meta descriptions in the sample, which suggests this is a fast, templated rollout rather than individually crafted suburb content. That's both a signal of intent (they're betting on local SEO at scale) and a weakness (thin pages that a well-written suburb page could outrank).

North Shore operates on a franchise/centre model, so these pages likely map to physical or affiliated centres. The Melbourne footprint (Brighton, Burwood, Bundoora, Craigieburn, South Morang, Springvale) is the directly competitive slice.

## Patterns

Only one competitor moved this week, and the move was a suburb-page land grab. Nobody shipped new pricing, new service lines, new positioning, or new content categories. The incumbents (Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Kip, Learnmate) are quiet - no price changes, no new subject or year-level pages, no fresh competitor-comparison pages targeting us.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Suburb pages are the obvious gap.** Tuterly has `/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne` and `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` at the city level, but no suburb layer. North Shore has just planted flags in Brighton, Burwood, Bundoora, Craigieburn, South Morang and Springvale. Given their pages are thin templates, Tuterly can win these by shipping genuinely useful suburb pages - local tutor counts from the directory, suburb-specific school lists (e.g. Brighton → Brighton Grammar, Firbank; Balwyn → Balwyn High catchment), typical hourly rates in that suburb, and transit/online options. The `/directory` already contains the raw data to make these non-thin.

2. **Prioritise the Melbourne suburbs North Shore just claimed** before their pages age into search rankings. Brighton and Burwood in particular are high-intent, high-income catchments.

3. **Competitor-alternative pages**: Tuterly has `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Neither Cluey nor Tutor Doctor shipped anything this week, so there's no new positioning to counter - but consider adding `/kip-mcgrath-alternative` and `/north-shore-alternative`, especially the latter given North Shore's sudden visibility push.

4. **Learnmate silence is notable.** As the most direct marketplace competitor at 6,430 URLs, their lack of movement is an opening to publish anything comparative (tutor quality, pricing transparency, Melbourne focus) without them responding in-kind.

5. **EzyMaths 403**: if they've locked down their sitemap deliberately, assume they're planning something. Flag for a manual check next week.

## What to watch next week

- Whether North Shore extends the suburb rollout further or starts fleshing out the thin pages with content.
- Whether EzyMaths' block is permanent (indicating a redesign/relaunch) or transient.
- Any Learnmate movement - their silence at this scale is the most informative non-event in the dataset.
