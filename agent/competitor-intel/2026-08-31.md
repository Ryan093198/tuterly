# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-08-31

## Headline

EzyMaths has quietly built out a full NAPLAN content silo covering Years 3, 5, 7 and 9 across all four test strands (numeracy, reading, writing, conventions of language). This is a 21-page structured launch - not a blog post - which signals a serious play for NAPLAN search traffic ahead of the 2027 test cycle. Tuterly has no dedicated NAPLAN content at all, and this is the clearest opening on the board right now.

## EzyMaths Tutoring

- Launched a `/naplan/` hub with year-level landing pages for Years 3, 5, 7, 9.
- Under each year, five sub-pages: an overview plus one page per NAPLAN strand (numeracy, reading, writing, conventions of language).
- Notable that a maths-branded tutor is expanding into reading/writing/language content. Either they're broadening positioning beyond maths, or they're using NAPLAN as a top-of-funnel lure and referring non-maths enquiries out.
- No pricing or homepage changes detected.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **NAPLAN is the live battleground.** EzyMaths is the only mover this week and they went heavy on NAPLAN. Given the size of the drop (21 structured pages in one hit), expect other competitors to react over the next 1-2 months.
- **Marketplace-side pruning at Learnmate** while agency-model competitors are static. The direct-employ tutor businesses (Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Kip, Apex, Alchemy) are quiet; the marketplace is actively editing its category pages.
- **Content silos beat blog posts.** EzyMaths didn't publish a "How to prep for NAPLAN" article - they built a matrix (year × strand). This is the structural pattern Tuterly's own worksheets and ATAR planner already lean toward.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Build a NAPLAN hub before EzyMaths ranks.** Tuterly has `/worksheets` and `/learn` infrastructure that could support a NAPLAN section faster than a static tutor site can. A year × strand matrix (matching EzyMaths' shape) plus downloadable sample questions from the worksheets library would beat their offering on utility. Priority: Years 3 and 5 (highest parent-search intent).
2. **Angle to differentiate:** EzyMaths' pages are almost certainly generic marketing copy. Tuterly can pair each strand page with actual past-question style worksheets and a tutor-match CTA - something EzyMaths (employed tutors, no marketplace) cannot easily do.
3. **VCE commerce subjects.** If Learnmate is pulling accounting and economics category pages, there may be under-served demand or under-supplied tutors in those subjects. Worth checking Tuterly's own tutor supply in VCE Accounting and Economics - if we have coverage, dedicated landing pages (mirroring the `/vce-tutoring` page structure) could pick up traffic Learnmate is walking away from.
4. **Kip's centre network** should be monitored monthly, not weekly. One closure isn't a trend, but if 3+ centres drop in a quarter it's a story worth telling on the `/centres` page ("why the franchise model is retreating").

## What I'd track next week

- Whether any other competitor responds to EzyMaths' NAPLAN launch.
- Whether Learnmate's VCE category deletions continue.
- Begin Bright once the fetch issue resolves - they've been dark for a cycle.
