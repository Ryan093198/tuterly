# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-08-26

## Headline

Cluey has done a large-scale content and structural refresh this week - 237 new URLs, no removals. It's the only competitor with meaningful movement, and the shape of the new pages tells us where they're placing their bets: capital-city landing pages, a free maths concept library, and Year 10 curriculum-specific pages split by state. This is a direct SEO play at the same parent-searching-for-help audience Tuterly serves, and it exposes two clear gaps in Tuterly's footprint (free worked-example content and non-Melbourne city pages).

## Cluey Learning

The 237 new URLs cluster into three distinct plays:

**1. City-level landing pages (non-Sydney/Melbourne):** New dedicated pages for Brisbane, Adelaide, Canberra and Perth, all following the same "Private Primary & High School Tutors" template. Cluey is clearly trying to own the "tutoring [city]" query nationally, not just in their historical strongholds.

**2. Free maths concept "resources" library:** A large batch of concept pages under `/resources/` - degrees-to-radians, radians-to-degrees, volume of a cylinder, volume of a cone, list of geometric shapes, quotient rule, quadratic formula, parallelogram, area of a triangle, distance formula, isosceles triangle. Each is positioned as "free theory, examples and practice questions" with a tutor CTA. This is a textbook top-of-funnel content play - capture students searching for homework help, convert parents to paid tutoring.

**3. Year 10 maths curriculum splits:** New pages for Year 10 Maths (Australian curriculum), Year 10 Maths NSW, Year 10 Maths Advanced (Australian), and Year 10 Maths Advanced NSW. They're segmenting by both year level and curriculum jurisdiction. Expect Year 11/12 and other state variants to follow.

**4. Other:** New homepage, refreshed subject hubs (online maths, online english, online chemistry), a homeschool tutoring page, and a `/payment-details/` page (worth watching if we can see pricing there next scrape).

Positioning language is unchanged: "personalised", "1-to-1", "build confidence". No new pricing claims visible in the sample.

## Cross-competitor patterns

Only one competitor moved this week, so "pattern" is really "Cluey vs everyone else." That itself is the pattern: the incumbents (Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath, EzyMaths, Apex, Alchemy, Learnmate) are sitting still on content, while Cluey is aggressively expanding its SEO surface area on three fronts simultaneously - geographic, curriculum-granular, and free-concept-library.

Cluey's free maths concept pages mirror the model Third Space Learning and Khan-adjacent players use overseas: capture the student doing homework, sell the parent the tutor. No Australian competitor in this set has a comparable library.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **City pages beyond Melbourne.** Tuterly currently has `/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne`. Cluey is now going after Brisbane, Adelaide, Canberra and Perth with dedicated pages. If Tuterly wants to defend Melbourne and eventually expand, at minimum a Sydney page is overdue; a decision is needed on whether to follow nationally or double down on Melbourne depth.

2. **Concept/worked-example content.** Tuterly has `/worksheets` and `/learn` - the raw material to compete with Cluey's `/resources/` library. Publishing focused, VCE-aligned concept pages (quadratic formula, quotient rule, radians, etc.) with worked examples and a link to book a Melbourne tutor would directly contest the same search intent, with a stronger local conversion angle.

3. **Curriculum-and-year-level pages.** Cluey is splitting Year 10 Maths by state (Australian curriculum vs NSW). Tuterly's `/vce-tutoring` page is the analogue, but there's room for year-by-year VCE Maths pages (Methods, Specialist, Further/General by year level) - more granular than what Cluey has and specifically Victorian.

4. **Watch Cluey's `/payment-details/` page.** New this week - if pricing is visible there next scrape, it directly informs Tuterly's `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` and `/cluey-alternative` pages.

5. **Cluey-alternative page refresh.** With Cluey's homepage and subject hubs refreshed, the `/cluey-alternative` comparison should be re-checked against their current live positioning claims.
