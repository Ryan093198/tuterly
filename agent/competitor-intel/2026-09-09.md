## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Publishing has effectively stalled** across the mid-to-large players (Cluey, Tutor Doctor, Kip McGrath, EzyMaths, Learnmate, Tutoring for Excellence all flat). This is the second signal in recent weeks that no one is running an aggressive content cadence - the field is open for Tuterly to publish and rank without competitive noise.
- **Apex is the only competitor treating worksheets as an ongoing content asset.** They are the direct analogue to Tuterly's `/worksheets` section and the one to benchmark against.
- **Several competitors have persistently broken or blocked sitemaps** (North Shore, Alchemy, Math Minds, Begin Bright). Even if they are publishing, they're invisible to standard discovery - a small SEO advantage for anyone (including Tuterly) with clean sitemap hygiene.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Worksheet depth vs Apex.** Apex is publishing at the subskill level (e.g. "substituting into formulas" rather than just "algebra"). Worth auditing whether Tuterly's `/worksheets` library matches that granularity for Year 7-10 maths, and specifically for early-algebra topics where Apex is clearly investing. If we're broader/shallower, we lose the long-tail queries.
2. **Metadata quality as a cheap edge.** Apex's new page shipped without a description or OG title. If Tuterly's worksheet pages have proper titles, descriptions and OG tags, we'll out-preview them in search and social shares even where the content is comparable.
3. **Content vacuum in VCE/ATAR.** No competitor this week published anything in the senior-secondary space, which is Tuterly's strongest positioning territory (`/vce-tutoring`, `/atar-planner`, `/scholarship-test-prep`). A steady weekly output here would compound with almost zero competitive pushback.
4. **Comparison/alternative pages remain uncontested.** Tuterly has `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Neither Cluey nor Tutor Doctor is publishing anything defensive or comparative back. Worth extending the pattern to Kip McGrath and Learnmate, both of which are large but static.

## What to ignore

- Success Tutoring's 2-URL sitemap - not a real signal, likely misconfigured.
- The recurring sitemap errors on North Shore, Alchemy, Math Minds and Begin Bright - unless a fetch method changes, we won't get diffs from these and shouldn't over-index on their silence.
