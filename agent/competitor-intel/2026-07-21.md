The only pattern this week is stillness. Nine sites, ~12,000 URLs crawled, one deletion. A few possible reads:

1. **Mid-winter school-holiday lull.** Late July in Australia sits between term 2 and term 3; marketing teams often pause launches until the term-3 back-to-school push in early August. Expect a wave of new content in the next 2-4 weeks, particularly around Year 12 trials, VCE mock exams, and semester 2 catch-up messaging.
