# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-11-05

## Headline

Apex Tuition is making a serious play on two fronts at once: a full suite of state-based ATAR calculators (NSW, QLD, WA, SA, IB) and a large Year 4 maths worksheet library. The ATAR calculator play directly overlaps with Tuterly's `/atar-planner`, and the worksheets suggest they're building out a full Year-by-Year maths content library that will eventually hit our Years 7-10 core. Worth paying attention to this week.

## Apex Tuition Australia

55 new pages, dominated by two initiatives:

**ATAR calculators (5 new pages)** - one per curriculum: HSC, QCE, WACE, IB-to-ATAR, and SACE, all branded "2026". This is a coordinated launch covering every non-VCE state plus IB. Apex is positioning itself as the national ATAR utility site. Tuterly has `/atar-planner` but it's VCE-focused; Apex is going wide.

This looks like a templated roll-out - if Year 4 is this comprehensive, Years 5-10 are likely queued. Tuterly has `/worksheets` but needs to check whether our Years 7-10 maths topic coverage is as granular.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Calculators and utilities as acquisition tools.** Apex's ATAR calculator roll-out mirrors a broader trend of competitors using tools (not blog posts) as the top-of-funnel. Tuterly's snap-a-question app is already a tool-first product, but on web we're thin outside `/atar-planner`. A Years 7-10 practice-focused utility (e.g. a "common mistakes diagnostic" or a topic-level skill checker) could sit naturally beside the app.

2. **Worksheet libraries are being built out systematically.** Apex is doing per-topic, per-year-level landing pages. For Tuterly, the parallel question is whether `/worksheets` has Years 7-10 coverage at the same topic granularity (symmetry, fractions, perimeter/area, algebra basics, etc.). If not, that's the fastest-to-ship gap to close, and it feeds the app's practice narrative.

3. **Quiet week elsewhere.** 8 of 11 trackable competitors shipped nothing. Apex is the only one investing in content velocity right now.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Years 7-10 maths topic guides.** No competitor shipped Year 7-10 maths topic content this week, but Apex's Year 4 template will reach Year 7 eventually. Get ahead with topic guides on the Year 7-10 "high-mistake" areas: fractions-to-decimals conversion, linear equations, Pythagoras, surds, index laws, algebraic fractions, probability trees. These double as blog content and as feeders into the "snap a question" app flow.

- **"Common mistakes" angle.** None of the competitors are publishing common-mistake content for Years 7-10 maths. This is the single most natural content angle for Tuterly given the app's premise (students snap questions they got wrong). Owning "Year 8 common algebra mistakes" and similar queries is uncontested right now.

- **Test prep for Years 7-10.** No new NAPLAN, selective, or scholarship prep content from competitors this week despite us having `/scholarship-test-prep`. Worth expanding into Year 7-9 NAPLAN maths prep as app-adjacent content.

- **National ATAR calculators.** Apex just claimed NSW/QLD/WA/SA/IB calculator territory. If Tuterly wants to defend `/atar-planner` as more than a VCE tool, this is the moment to decide whether to compete nationally or double down on being the best VCE planner.

- **No competitor apps or homework/practice tools launched this week.** Tuterly's app positioning remains uncontested in this data set.
