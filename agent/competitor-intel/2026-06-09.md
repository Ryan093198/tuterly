A genuinely quiet week. Across the 12 competitors monitored, only one page-level change was detected: Learnmate refreshed a single IB Chemistry Paper 2 guide from a 2025 to a 2026 edition. No new product pages, no pricing moves, no positioning shifts anywhere in the set.

The more useful signal this week is what the silence implies — most competitors are content-static heading into the mid-year exam period, which is a window Tuterly could exploit with timely VCE/HSC/IB exam-prep content while rivals coast.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Pre-exam dormancy.** With mid-year exams imminent across VCE, HSC and IB, you'd expect a surge of exam-prep content. Instead, nine of the nine reachable competitors published nothing new this week. Learnmate's lone update was a date-stamp refresh on existing content. This is an open field.

2. **IB curriculum coverage gap on Tuterly.** Learnmate's ongoing IB investment (Chemistry Paper 2 here, presumably more in their back catalogue) highlights that Tuterly's top-level paths cover VCE, ATAR planning, and Melbourne tutoring, but have no visible IB landing page or subject hub. If IB families are a viable segment in Melbourne, this is a clean whitespace.

3. **Persistent sitemap fragility on smaller players.** Begin Bright, Alchemy, Success Tutoring, Tutoring for Excellence and Math Minds all returned errors or empty sitemaps. Some of these have been intermittent before. Worth treating this group as lower-signal until they stabilise — or as a sign that their web operations are thin, which is itself competitive context.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Own the mid-year exam window.** Competitors are silent. A focused push on VCE Unit 1/2 mid-year exam prep — subject-by-subject guides under `/vce-tutoring` or `/learn` — would land in an uncontested cycle.
- **IB landing page.** Even a single `/ib-tutoring` page (or IB Chemistry / IB Maths subject pages) would directly counter Learnmate's strongest content vertical and address a curriculum Tuterly currently ignores publicly.
- **Annual-refresh play.** Learnmate's habit of redating exam guides each year is worth copying for any evergreen content Tuterly already owns — particularly the ATAR planner and scholarship test prep pages, which benefit from a visible "2026" stamp.
- **Comparison pages.** Tuterly already has `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate is the most actively-publishing competitor in this set, a `/learnmate-alternative` page would round out the comparison suite against the three most visible rivals.

Five of twelve competitors are not returning usable sitemap data. If this persists another week or two, it may be worth either (a) switching to HTML crawling for those domains, or (b) dropping the consistently-broken ones from the watch list to reduce noise.
