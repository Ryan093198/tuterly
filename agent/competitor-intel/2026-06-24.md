## Cross-competitor patterns

The only pattern this week is the absence of one: nobody shipped anything new that our crawler picked up. A few interpretations worth holding lightly until next week confirms or contradicts:

- End of financial year is typically a slow content period for education businesses - resources go into sales and renewals rather than publishing.
- The two HTTP 415 failures (Success Tutoring, Tutoring for Excellence) suggest a shared hosting or CMS pattern that is blocking our sitemap request. Fixing the crawler here is higher value than any single competitor finding this week.
- The footprint hierarchy is unchanged and worth keeping front of mind for strategy: Learnmate (6,430) > Alchemy (2,691) > Cluey (1,703) ≈ Apex (1,697) > North Shore (1,022) - these are the sites with the deepest content moats to chip away at.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

With no new competitor moves to react to, this is a good week to push on gaps that have been sitting in the backlog rather than chase. Two specific angles stand out when comparing our top-level paths to what these competitors are known to invest in:

- **Scholarship test prep depth.** We have `/scholarship-test-prep` as a single page. North Shore and Cluey both build out wider scholarship and selective-school content trees. A quiet week is a good week to expand ours into specific school targets (Melbourne High, MacRob, Scotch, etc.) before they publish more.
- **ATAR planner as a wedge.** None of the competitors crawled have a tool comparable to `/atar-planner`. Worth a dedicated marketing push or a linked content series ("how to plan Year 12 subject loads", "ATAR scaling explained") that drives traffic to the tool. This is a structural advantage that is not being pressured this week.
- **Centres positioning.** `/centres` is unusual for a marketplace - most listed competitors are either pure agencies (Tutor Doctor, Alchemy, Apex) or pure centre chains (Kip McGrath, North Shore, Begin Bright, Math Minds). Tuterly straddling both is a distinctive angle that could be sharpened in messaging while nobody is actively countering it.
