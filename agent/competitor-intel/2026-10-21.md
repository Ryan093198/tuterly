## Cross-competitor patterns

The dominant pattern is stasis - a full week where no competitor published anything new publicly. This is unusual enough to flag. Possible explanations: school holiday lull (late October is between Term 3 holidays and the VCE exam period), teams focused on end-of-year ops rather than content, or we're simply looking at a quiet week before Term 4 wind-down.

The one directional signal - Learnmate removing VCE Accounting and Economics tutor pages - is interesting because both are commerce-stream subjects that usually carry decent search demand around exam/enrolment season. If they're struggling to staff those subjects, there's a short-term supply gap in the Learnmate marketplace heading into 2027 planning.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **VCE Accounting and Economics tutor pages.** Learnmate has just vacated two SEO-relevant URLs. We cover `/vce-tutoring` as a hub but don't appear to have subject-level landing pages. Spinning up VCE Accounting and VCE Economics pages now - while Learnmate's equivalents 404 and get deindexed - is low-effort and well-timed.

2. **Exam-period content void.** Nobody published this week despite VCE written exams starting imminently. If we have anything useful on the `/learn` or `/atar-planner` side about final-week prep, exam-day logistics, or 2027 subject selection, publishing into silence is cheaper than publishing into noise.

3. **The quiet period itself is the opportunity.** Competitors re-crawl and re-rank based on freshness signals. A week where none of them are moving is a week where consistent output from us compounds disproportionately.
