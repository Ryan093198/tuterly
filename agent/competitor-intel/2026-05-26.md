# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-05-26

## Headline observation

Learnmate has quietly pruned 42 pages from its site, all carrying a `cm-` prefix and all targeting generic, location-light search terms ("algebra tutor online", "chemistry home tuition near me", "best math coaching near me", "English home tuition near me", etc.). This is the only meaningful movement across the entire competitor set this week, and it suggests Learnmate is either retreating from a programmatic landing-page experiment that wasn't converting, or consolidating that traffic into a smaller number of stronger pages. Worth watching closely - if those keywords now have weaker coverage on Learnmate's side, there's a short window to pick up ground on subject-plus-mode searches (online chemistry, online English, online maths) that Tuterly doesn't currently have dedicated pages for.

## Cross-competitor patterns

It's an exceptionally quiet week. Eleven competitors held completely steady, one (Learnmate) only deleted, none added new pages. Three points worth noting:

1. **No-one is launching new content right now.** Late May, mid-term, is historically a slow publishing period. If Tuterly ships anything substantial in the next fortnight - new guide, new suburb page, new subject vertical - it will land into an empty news cycle.

2. **Learnmate's retreat from "near me" subject pages is the second time in recent memory we've seen a marketplace competitor shrink rather than grow.** The pattern across the industry seems to be that pure programmatic location/subject combinations no longer earn their keep. Tuterly should avoid that trap if it's tempted to scale `/directory` the same way.

3. **The big three by URL count (Learnmate 6,331, Alchemy 2,691, Cluey 1,703) are not moving.** Stability at the top tier means market share isn't shifting via content this week.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Online subject pages.** Learnmate just removed online algebra, online physics, online chemistry and online English pages. Tuterly has `/online-tutoring-melbourne` and `/vce-tutoring` but no subject-specific online pages. A small set of well-written online subject pages (online VCE chemistry, online VCE methods, online VCE English) would fill a gap Learnmate has just vacated and align with Tuterly's existing Melbourne/VCE positioning.

- **Chinese and language tutoring.** Learnmate dropped a "Chinese home tuition" page. Tuterly has no language-tutoring page at all. Language tutoring (Mandarin, especially for VCE Chinese SL/Second Language) is a defensible niche in Melbourne with strong demand and weak marketplace coverage.

- **Chatswood and other suburb pages.** Learnmate dropped at least one suburb page (Chatswood, Sydney - not relevant to Tuterly directly, but the broader signal is that suburb pages need to be genuinely useful, not thin). Tuterly's `/best-tutoring-melbourne` could be expanded into a small set of high-quality Melbourne suburb pages (Glen Waverley, Balwyn, Brighton, Doncaster) where competitors are thin.

- **Comparison pages.** Tuterly already has `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate is the biggest marketplace by URL count and a direct structural competitor, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is the obvious next addition.
