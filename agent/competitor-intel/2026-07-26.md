# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-07-26

## Headline

A genuinely quiet week. Across eleven successfully-crawled competitors, zero new URLs and zero removed URLs. No pricing pages moved, no new landing pages, no new comparison or location pages. This is unusual enough to be worth noting on its own - it suggests either a mid-year content lull (end of July, school holidays winding down) or that our diff baseline needs a sanity check next week.

## Per-competitor findings

**Cluey Learning** - No changes across 1,703 URLs. Our `/cluey-alternative` page continues to face a static target.

**Tutor Doctor Australia** - No changes across 398 URLs. Same story for our `/tutor-doctor-alternative` page.

**EzyMaths Tutoring** - No changes (439 URLs).

**Kip McGrath Education** - No changes (322 URLs).

**Begin Bright** - Fetch failed this week. Worth a manual spot check to confirm the site is still up and that it's not a persistent block; if it stays broken next week we should switch scraping approach.

**Learnmate** - No changes across 6,419 URLs. As the closest structural competitor (Melbourne marketplace model), the flat line here is the most important single data point in this report. Their tutor directory and subject pages are stable - no new subject verticals, no new tutor onboarding pages surfaced.

**Apex Tuition Australia** - No changes (1,698 URLs).

**Alchemy Tuition** - No changes (2,691 URLs). Their content-heavy footprint remains their moat but they didn't extend it this week.

**Success Tutoring** - Still only 2 URLs indexed. Either heavy JS rendering we're not resolving, or their public site is deliberately thin and funnels to booking. Flag: if we can't crawl them properly we're flying blind on a real competitor.

**North Shore Coaching College** - No changes (984 URLs).

**Tutoring for Excellence** - No changes (203 URLs).

**Math Minds** - No changes (66 URLs).

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Complete stasis.** Zero net URL changes across the entire set is not normal. Two possibilities: (a) genuine winter/holiday content freeze - plausible given timing, or (b) our differ is comparing against a stale or identical snapshot. Worth verifying the pipeline before drawing strategic conclusions next week.

2. **No competitor moved on pricing, comparison pages, or new subject/location landing pages** - meaning our current positioning pages (`/cluey-alternative`, `/tutor-doctor-alternative`, `/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne`) are not being actively countered.

3. **Crawl quality issues to resolve:** Begin Bright failed entirely and Success Tutoring returns only 2 URLs. Two of twelve competitors are effectively invisible to us right now.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because there's nothing new to react to, this is a good week to press offensive advantages rather than play defence:

- **Scholarship test prep** - none of the competitors appear to have moved on this topic this week, and we already own `/scholarship-test-prep`. A push on selective schools / scholarship content while competitors are quiet would compound.
- **ATAR planner** - still a distinctive tool. No competitor has launched anything comparable this week (or ever, based on our accumulated view). Worth pushing harder in acquisition.
- **Worksheets** - Cluey, Kip McGrath and Math Minds all sit adjacent to this territory but none extended it this week. Good moment to expand `/worksheets` coverage.
- **Learnmate comparison page** - we have Cluey and Tutor Doctor comparison pages but not Learnmate, our closest structural competitor. Given how static their site is, a "Tuterly vs Learnmate" page would be easy to build and hard for them to counter quickly.
- **Centres angle** - `/centres` is a positioning we have that Kip McGrath, North Shore and Begin Bright (the actual centre operators) don't seem to be defending with new content. Room to sharpen the marketplace-vs-centre narrative.

## Recommended actions

1. Sanity-check the diffing pipeline before next week's report - zero changes across eleven sites is suspicious.
2. Investigate the Begin Bright fetch failure and the Success Tutoring 2-URL result.
3. Treat this quiet week as a build week: ship the Learnmate comparison page and expand ATAR planner / scholarship content while competitors are dormant.
