# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-09-29

## Headline

Alchemy Tuition has restructured its entire Sydney suburb landing-page footprint this week - moving 211 pages from flat URLs (e.g. `/english-tutoring-abbotsford/`) into a nested hierarchy (`/english-tutoring/sydney/abbotsford/` and `/tutoring/sydney/abbotsford/`). This is a deliberate, large-scale local-SEO play targeting Sydney suburbs at both a general "tutoring" and subject-specific "English tutoring" level. It's the only meaningful competitor movement this week, and it's aimed squarely at owning suburb-level search intent - a lane Tuterly does not currently contest in Melbourne beyond a few high-level pages.

## Per-competitor findings

### Alchemy Tuition (211 new / 211 removed)
- Migrated a huge block of Sydney suburb pages from `/english-tutoring-{suburb}/` to `/english-tutoring/sydney/{suburb}/`. Same for general `/tutoring/sydney/{suburb}/`.
- Sample suburbs covered include Abbotsford, Annandale, Ashfield, Avalon, Balmain, Balgowlah, Artarmon, Asquith, Austral, Cambridge Park, Canley Heights, Drummoyne, East Ryde, Ebenezer, Lower Portland, Middle Dural - a mix of inner-west, north shore, northern beaches, and outer/rural NW Sydney.
- Titles and descriptions are templated ("#1 English Tutoring {Suburb}! More than 15 years of experience... Award-winning private tuition for K-12 students").
- Structure implies they're building a `/tutoring/{city}/{suburb}/` and `/{subject}-tutoring/{city}/{suburb}/` scaffold that could easily extend to Melbourne, Brisbane, and other subjects (maths, science). Worth watching over coming weeks to see if Melbourne suburbs appear.
- One legacy URL removed was a bizarre full-sentence slug (`/1-english-tutoring-acacia-gardens-more-than-15-years...`), suggesting they're also cleaning up historic SEO experiments.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **The market is unusually quiet.** Nine of eleven live competitors made zero changes. Only Alchemy is actively publishing, and their move is structural rather than new content.
- **Suburb-level pages remain the dominant SEO tactic** for the bricks-and-mortar and franchise operators. Alchemy's URL reshuffle reinforces that they see nested subject-by-suburb pages as the winning template.
- **No pricing, positioning, or product changes** detected anywhere this week. No new parent guides, no ATAR/exam-season content despite the timing (VCE exams imminent).

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Melbourne suburb + subject pages.** Alchemy is telegraphing the template that works for them in Sydney: `/{subject}-tutoring/melbourne/{suburb}/`. Tuterly has `/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne`, and `/vce-tutoring` at the top level but nothing at suburb granularity. Even a focused set of 20-30 high-intent Melbourne suburbs (Balwyn, Glen Waverley, Kew, Brighton, Camberwell, Doncaster, etc.) crossed with VCE subjects would create defensible local coverage before Alchemy expands south.
2. **VCE exam-season content vacuum.** Late September / early October is exactly when Year 12 families are searching for last-minute help. Nobody in this set has published anything topical. Tuterly's `/learn` and `/atar-planner` could be paired with a timely push (exam-week study plans, subject-specific final revision) with essentially no competing fresh content.
3. **Alchemy migration = temporary vulnerability.** Their 211 URLs just changed paths. Even with redirects, there's typically a ranking dip for a few weeks. If Tuterly has (or wants) any pages targeting the same Sydney intent, this is the window - though Melbourne focus probably makes this lower priority.
4. **Marketplace differentiation.** Learnmate (the closest marketplace analog at 6.4k URLs) hasn't moved. Tuterly's alternative pages (`/cluey-alternative`, `/tutor-doctor-alternative`) have no `/learnmate-alternative` equivalent - worth considering given they're the biggest direct-competitor marketplace and appear complacent.

## What to watch next week
- Whether Alchemy's new suburb structure extends to Melbourne or to additional subjects (maths, science).
- Any exam-season content drops from Cluey or Learnmate as VCE/HSC hit peak.
- Whether Begin Bright and North Shore come back online with any accumulated changes.
