## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Publishing has stalled.** Eleven of twelve trackable competitors shipped zero new pages. Either everyone is in end-of-year wind-down mode, or the Australian tutoring category is genuinely under-investing in content right now. Either way, the window to land evergreen Years 7-10 maths content with little competitive noise is open.
2. **Score-story content remains the default blog format** (Apex's Extension 2 piece being the week's only example). Nobody in the set is publishing topic explainers, common-mistake breakdowns, or test-prep walkthroughs for Years 7-10 maths.
3. **No competitor app or practice-tool movement detected** this week. No new homework helpers, no new student-facing tools surfaced in any sitemap. Tuterly's "snap a wrong question, practise a fresh version" loop remains uncontested in what we can see.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Years 7-10 maths topic guides.** Still a white-space category. Nobody tracked this week added anything targeting Year 7 fractions, Year 8 linear equations, Year 9 Pythagoras/trig, Year 10 quadratics, surds, or indices. These are directly aligned with the app's practice content and we have no `/learn` pages serving these queries by the look of our top-level paths.
- **Common-mistake content.** Apex's score-story is a format we could flip: instead of "how I scored 96", "the 5 mistakes Year 9 students make on linear equations" - which maps directly to the app's wrong-question loop and gives us a natural CTA.
- **Year 10 selective/scholarship and end-of-year exam prep.** North Shore owns scholarship exam prep positioning but hasn't published anything new. We have `/scholarship-test-prep` - worth refreshing with Year 9-10 maths-specific angles now, before competitors restart publishing in January.
- **Student score-story format, Melbourne/VCE-flavoured.** Apex is building NSW/HSC authority via these. We should run the same play for Melbourne Year 10 → VCE transitions and tie it to app usage.
- **EzyMaths blocking us** is itself a signal they're the most direct Years 7-10 maths competitor in content terms. Worth a manual browse next week to see what they're publishing that we can't crawl.
