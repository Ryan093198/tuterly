## Cross-competitor patterns

Nothing was published, changed, or removed by any of the nine crawlable competitors this week. That's unusual - normally one or two publish a blog post. Possible explanations: pre-exam-season lull (Year 12 exams wrapping, Years 7-10 heading into end-of-year reports), or a reporting artefact if sitemaps are cached. Worth watching whether next week shows a catch-up spike.

The two maths-specialist competitors (EzyMaths, Math Minds) remain the closest conceptual neighbours to the Tuterly app. Neither has been seen shipping an app or a "snap your wrong answer" style tool in any data we have. No competitor crawled this week advertises a student-facing practice app at all - the category is still tutor-marketplace or centre-based coaching. That gap is the core defensible position for the Tuterly app.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's nothing reactive to do, these are standing gaps worth using the quiet week to attack:

1. **Years 7-10 maths topic guides.** We have `/worksheets` and `/learn` but no visible competitor-grade topic hub for Year 7-10 maths (algebra, linear equations, Pythagoras, surds, indices, probability, trig ratios). None of the competitors with large footprints (Success, Learnmate, Alchemy, Apex) have specialised Year 7-10 maths topic libraries we're being outranked by - this is an open field rather than a catch-up play.

2. **"Common mistakes" content.** This angle is a natural fit for the app's core loop (students snap questions they got wrong). No competitor publishes a "common Year 8 algebra mistakes" or "why students lose marks on linear equations" series. This is both on-brand and genuinely useful ammunition for the app's marketing.

3. **End-of-year test and NAPLAN-adjacent prep for Years 7-9.** `/scholarship-test-prep` exists but we don't have a Year 7-9 maths exam-prep cluster. Competitors aren't publishing here either.

4. **Maths-specialist competitor comparison pages.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. An `/ezymaths-alternative` or `/mathminds-alternative` would catch searches from parents already narrowed to maths-only options - which is exactly our segment. EzyMaths' blocked sitemap this week is a reminder we don't know what they're publishing; a comparison page de-risks that.

5. **App-vs-tutor positioning page.** No competitor has one because no competitor has an app. A page framing "when a practice app beats weekly tutoring for Years 7-10 maths" would own a category of its own.
