## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Quiet week overall.** Eight of the ten scrapeable competitors made zero changes. This is a natural moment to move faster than the field.
2. **State-specific landing pages are the emerging pattern.** North Shore's `/book-your-free-trial-class-nsw/` mirrors what most competitors are doing structurally - state-scoped conversion pages. Tuterly's Melbourne-specific pages (`/best-tutoring-melbourne`, `/tutoring-prices-melbourne`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne`) already do this well for Victoria, but there's no equivalent NSW/QLD footprint.
3. **Holiday program and NAPLAN workshop pages are recurring seasonal assets** for the coaching-college segment. Tuterly has no equivalent seasonal or event-based landing pages.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Free trial page as a distinct URL.** North Shore is treating the free trial as a standalone conversion asset with its own T&Cs page. Tuterly doesn't appear to have a dedicated trial/first-session landing page in the top-level paths. Worth testing.
- **NAPLAN writing content.** North Shore is running a workshop specifically on NAPLAN writing - a narrow, high-intent parent search. Tuterly has `/worksheets` and `/learn` but nothing NAPLAN-specific visible at the top level. A NAPLAN prep hub (writing, numeracy, reading) is an obvious gap given NAPLAN happens nationally.
- **School holiday programs.** None of Tuterly's top-level paths address holiday programs or intensives. This is a recurring seasonal search that competitors are targeting.
- **VCE subject pages Learnmate is retiring.** If Learnmate is pulling VCE Accounting and Economics tutor pages, either those subjects aren't converting for them or their supply is thin. Both are opportunities for Tuterly to own if we have tutors in those subjects - low competition, existing search demand.
- **Cluey and Tutor Doctor comparison pages already exist** (`/cluey-alternative`, `/tutor-doctor-alternative`). Given how static both competitors' sites are, these pages should be low-maintenance evergreen assets - worth an audit to ensure they're ranking and converting.
