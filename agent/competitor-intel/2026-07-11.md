# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-07-11

## Headline

Cluey has torn out roughly 1,290 URLs from its site this week - about three-quarters of what it was previously publishing - while adding nothing new. This looks like a major consolidation or platform migration, not routine cleanup. The removed pages include their ATAR quiz for parents, subject content like "english-literary-techniques", multiple feedback landing pages (Trustpilot, ProductReview), and a large tail of "2023-options" and "2024-options" guides plus staging duplicates. Whatever Cluey relaunches next is worth watching closely - and in the interim there is a real window where their organic footprint on ATAR-adjacent and subject-technique queries has collapsed.

## Per competitor

### Cluey Learning
- Down to 413 URLs, with 1,290 removed and zero added.
- Removed content clusters:
  - **Options guides** ("2023-options", "2024-options" and their staging variants) - suggests they've retired their subject-selection funnel, at least in its current form.
  - **ATAR quiz for parents** - a parent-facing lead capture tool is gone (including several staging/"ToBeDeleted" versions, so this appears deliberate).
  - **Subject technique content** e.g. english-literary-techniques.
  - **Review/feedback landing pages** across Trustpilot and ProductReview - they may be centralising social proof.
- The sheer volume of staging URLs in the removal list points to a housekeeping pass alongside real content pruning.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Quiet week almost everywhere except Cluey.** Six of the seven competitors we can read made zero changes. The only publishing activity was Learnmate's two VCE subject pages.
- **VCE subject-specific pages remain the standard growth tactic** on the marketplace side (Learnmate). Cluey's retreat from options/ATAR quiz content is the opposite move - stepping back from top-of-funnel guidance content.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **Fill the Cluey vacuum on ATAR/subject-selection content.** Their ATAR quiz for parents and options guides are gone. Tuterly already has `/atar-planner` and `/parents` - now is a good moment to promote them harder and consider a parent-facing subject-selection quiz or interactive tool while Cluey is silent on the topic. Also worth checking whether the removed Cluey URLs still rank; if so, they'll drop soon.
2. **Cluey-alternative page refresh.** With Cluey visibly in transition, `/cluey-alternative` should be updated to reflect current-state differences. If they've dropped parent tools and options guides, that's a concrete comparison point.
3. **VCE subject coverage gap vs Learnmate.** We have `/vce-tutoring` as a hub but Learnmate is systematically building per-subject landing pages (now including Accounting and Economics). Consider whether subject-level VCE pages under `/vce-tutoring/…` are worth the investment - Learnmate clearly believes so and keeps compounding.
4. **English literary techniques content.** Cluey has pulled its english-literary-techniques resource. This is a high-intent VCE English topic; a stronger version under `/learn` or `/worksheets` could pick up displaced traffic.
5. **Parent-review consolidation.** Cluey removed multiple Trustpilot/ProductReview landing pages. If we don't already have a consolidated social-proof page for parents, this is a signal that competitors treat it as valuable enough to iterate on.

## Watchlist for next week

- Whatever Cluey publishes to replace the 1,290 removed pages - the new IA and messaging will reveal their repositioning.
- Whether Learnmate keeps adding VCE subject pages (rate and which subjects).
- Restoring visibility into Success Tutoring, Alchemy, Begin Bright, Math Minds, and Tutoring for Excellence.
