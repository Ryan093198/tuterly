## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **One noisy player, ten silent ones.** Success Tutoring is the only competitor doing anything visible; the rest of the market is static. If Success is genuinely accelerating (new centres, new content) they'll pull ahead on share of voice unless someone answers.
2. **Centres-as-content**: Success turns every new franchise into a blog post that doubles as a local landing page (suburb + "tutoring" in the slug). Given we have a `/centres` path and location pages like `/best-tutoring-melbourne` and `/online-tutoring-melbourne`, this is the same playbook - they're just executing it at higher volume.
3. **Exam-technique content is the sharp end** of what Success publishes. Cheat sheets, verb glossaries, band/score guides, worked essay examples. This is closer to our `/worksheets` and `/learn` remit than to generic study-tips fluff.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Defend Brighton/Bayside VCE**. Success is announcing a physical Brighton centre with VCE positioning. Our `/vce-tutoring`, `/best-tutoring-melbourne` and `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` pages should explicitly out-position them on price transparency, tutor choice, and online flexibility before they establish local search authority. A dedicated Bayside/Brighton page under `/directory` or as a suburb landing could pre-empt them.
- **Topic gaps we don't obviously cover**:
  - VCE and HSC "action verbs" / command-word glossaries - natural fit for `/learn` or `/worksheets`.
  - Subject-specific "how to get an A / Band 6 / study score X" guides for VCE subjects (Business Management, Chemistry, English).
  - Worked essay exemplars for VCE English texts - Success is doing T.S. Eliot; VCE text list equivalents (e.g. current VCAA texts) would be high-intent parent/student content for us.
  - "Online vs in-person tutoring" comparison - useful top-of-funnel piece that also feeds our `/online-tutoring-melbourne` positioning.
  - School-readiness / pre-prep content - nobody else in our tracked set touches this; probably off-strategy for Tuterly, but worth a conscious yes/no.
- **Angle we can beat them on**: Success's content is centre-and-franchise flavoured. Tuterly's marketplace model lets us publish exam content authored by named high-ATAR tutors, which is a credibility angle they structurally can't match. Bylined `/learn` content would differentiate.
