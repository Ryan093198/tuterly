# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-10-22

## Headline

Cluey Learning has pulled down roughly 1,290 URLs this week with zero new pages added. This looks like a major site cleanup or restructure in progress - old campaign pages (2023 and 2024 "options guides"), staging drafts, duplicate feedback pages, and even an ATAR quiz for parents are gone. For us this is worth watching closely: Cluey is the competitor our `/cluey-alternative` page targets directly, and if they're mid-rebuild there may be a short window where their messaging, pricing or product framing shifts. We should re-audit `/cluey-alternative` in 2-3 weeks against whatever replaces this content.

## Patterns across competitors

1. **Quiet week on new content.** Only Apex published anything new, and only two posts. Most direct competitors made zero visible changes. Cluey is the sole mover, and only in subtraction.
2. **Command-terms / exam-mechanics content is emerging as a format.** Apex's "Decoding VCE Biology Command Terms" fits a pattern we've seen before where tutoring brands build subject-specific exam-literacy guides. These rank well for anxious Year 11-12 searches and double as credibility content.
3. **Cluey's cleanup may precede a repositioning.** The removal of a parent-facing quiz plus multiple years of campaign guides suggests they're consolidating around a tighter proposition. Worth a manual visit next week to see what their nav and homepage look like now.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **VCE command terms / exam language guides.** Apex is building this. We have `/vce-tutoring` and `/worksheets` but no visible content explaining how VCE students should read command terms (explain, evaluate, justify, etc.) across subjects. A single hub page covering command terms for VCE Biology, Chemistry, English, Methods and Psychology would be a defensible resource and sits naturally under `/learn`.
- **Refresh the Cluey comparison.** With ~1,290 Cluey pages gone, parts of our `/cluey-alternative` page may now reference content or features that no longer exist on their site. Diff it against their live site in the next 1-2 weeks.
- **SACE / interstate framing.** Apex is writing for SACE students. We're Melbourne-focused (and that's a strength), but if we ever expand `/learn` content, SACE and HSC command-term equivalents would broaden reach without diluting the Melbourne marketplace positioning.
- **Parent-facing ATAR tools.** Cluey has killed their ATAR quiz for parents. Our `/atar-planner` is well-placed to be *the* parent-facing ATAR tool in the market - worth a small push (homepage prominence, a parent-angle landing variant under `/parents`) while a direct competitor is retreating from this format.
