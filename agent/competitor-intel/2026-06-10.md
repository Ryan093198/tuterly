## Patterns across competitors

1. **Mid-year publishing lull.** Zero new pages from 10 of 11 successfully crawled sites in a single week is unusual. June sits between Term 2 exams and the July school holidays - competitors may be holding content launches for the holiday-decision window when parents shop for tutors. Expect a publishing spike in late June / early July. We should have our own holiday-period landing content ready before they do.

2. **VCE subject pages remain the contested ground.** The only movement this week was a VCE subject page. Learnmate, Cluey and Apex all maintain deep subject-level content; Tuterly currently has `/vce-tutoring` as a single hub plus the `/atar-planner` tool. We do not appear to have individual VCE subject landing pages (Methods, Specialist, English, Chemistry, Biology, Psychology, Economics, Legal Studies, etc.).

3. **No pricing or positioning changes detected** at any competitor. Our `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` and comparison pages (`/cluey-alternative`, `/tutor-doctor-alternative`) remain uncontested by direct response.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Build out VCE subject-specific pages.** Learnmate's quiet move on `/subject/vce-english/` is a reminder that the per-subject pages convert and rank. Tuterly has the `/atar-planner` and `/sample-report` assets that no competitor can match - bolting those into individual subject pages (e.g. `/vce-methods-tutoring`, `/vce-english-tutoring`) would give us pages that are both better targeted and more useful than what Learnmate or Cluey publish.

- **Pre-empt the July holiday content wave.** If competitors are saving launches for late June, we have roughly two weeks to publish holiday-intensive content (holiday programs, mid-year catch-up, Term 3 prep) before the field gets noisy. Nothing on our current top-level paths speaks directly to this seasonal moment.

- **Cluey and Tutor Doctor comparison pages are unchallenged.** Neither competitor has published anything that would force us to update `/cluey-alternative` or `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Good time to refresh those with current pricing and feature claims while the underlying facts are stable.

- **Scholarship test prep remains uncontested in the visible data.** No competitor moved on scholarship content this week, and few have dedicated pages. `/scholarship-test-prep` is a defensible position worth reinforcing with deeper sub-pages (per-school, per-test format) before someone else notices the gap.
