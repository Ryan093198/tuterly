Our /directory and /tutoring paths could be expanded along the same axes. A quiet competitor week is a low-risk time to ship a batch of these without triggering anyone's attention.

Given Learnmate is our closest model-match and Kip McGrath is the dominant bricks-and-mortar player parents actually compare us to, /learnmate-alternative and /kip-mcgrath-alternative would round out the set. North Shore and Cluey both traditionally play in this space with deeper content - worth a manual audit next week to confirm whether we're genuinely competitive on depth, since the sitemap-diff view won't catch quality gaps on unchanged pages.
