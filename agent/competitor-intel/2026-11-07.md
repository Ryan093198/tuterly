# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-11-07

**Headline: a completely quiet week across the tracked set.** Every competitor we could crawl returned zero new and zero removed URLs. No pricing pages, no new subject or year-level landing pages, no app announcements, no blog activity. This is unusual enough to be worth flagging on its own - either publishing calendars have paused heading into end-of-school-year, or our diffing is only catching sitemap-indexed changes and some competitors have moved to publishing outside their sitemaps. Worth a manual spot-check next week on Cluey and Success Tutoring specifically.

## Cross-competitor patterns

The only pattern this week is the absence of one. Eleven of twelve tracked sites published nothing new to their sitemaps in the same week. Possible explanations:

- End-of-year content freeze ahead of summer holidays and the gap between school years.
- Teams redirecting effort to paid acquisition rather than organic content as enrolment season for 2027 begins.
- Sitemap staleness - some CMSes don't regenerate sitemaps on every publish, so changes may land next week in a lump.

No new competitor apps, no new practice/homework tools, no pricing changes detected.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Because there's nothing new to react to, this is a good week to push on the structural gaps we already know exist:

1. **Years 7-10 maths topic library.** None of the direct competitors own this territory with depth. Cluey and Math Minds are the natural threats. While they're quiet, we should be shipping topic guides (e.g. linear equations, Pythagoras, surds, quadratics, probability) tied directly to "snap a question like this" app CTAs. Tuterly currently has `/worksheets` and `/learn` but no visible per-topic Year 7-10 maths hub in the top-level paths.

2. **Common-mistakes content.** This is uniquely on-brand for us (the whole app is built around questions students got wrong) and no competitor appears to own it. A "most common Year 8 algebra mistakes" style series would be defensible and differentiated.

3. **NAPLAN and end-of-year test prep.** No competitor shipped test-prep content this week despite the calendar. We have `/scholarship-test-prep` but nothing visibly aimed at Year 7/9 NAPLAN maths or Year 10 end-of-year exams - an obvious adjacency for the app.

4. **Comparison pages.** We already have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Kip McGrath's assessment-led positioning overlaps most with our app loop, a `/kip-mcgrath-alternative` page is the next logical one. EzyMaths and Math Minds alternative pages would also capture pure-maths intent.

5. **Visibility fix.** Get EzyMaths and Begin Bright onto a manual-review cadence so we're not flying blind on two maths-relevant competitors.
