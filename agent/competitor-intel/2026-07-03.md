There is no publishing pattern this week - it's a dead week across the board. Two things are still worth naming:

1. **Content velocity across the tracked set is very low.** Even ignoring the errored competitors, five active sites with a combined ~4,500 URLs published nothing. Tuterly's own content cadence, if steady, compounds against a slow field.
2. **Learnmate is the only site making editorial decisions in public.** They're the most active competitor by URL count and the one to watch most closely - deletions from a marketplace of their size usually precede a restructure.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **HSC Extension gap (if we care about NSW):** Learnmate stepping back from Extension 1/2 Sydney pages is a small opening. Low priority unless NSW expansion is on the roadmap - our current positioning is Melbourne/VCE.
- **Comparison pages remain underexploited by competitors.** We already have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. None of the tracked competitors run comparison content against each other or against us. Adding `/learnmate-alternative` would be a cheap next step given Learnmate is the largest and most active player.
- **ATAR planner and worksheets remain unmatched.** No competitor this week (or in the current URL sets we can see) has an equivalent to `/atar-planner` or `/worksheets`. These are still our clearest product-led differentiators - worth continuing to invest in and link to prominently.
- **Scholarship test prep is quiet across competitors.** Our `/scholarship-test-prep` page has no visible competitor equivalent in the tracked set. Consider whether it deserves deeper sub-pages (by school, by test type) before someone else moves in.
