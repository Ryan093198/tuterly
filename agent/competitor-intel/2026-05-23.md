# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-05-23

**Headline: a genuinely quiet week.** Across nine successfully crawled competitors covering roughly 12,250 URLs, there were zero new pages and zero removals. No competitor shipped new content, landing pages, location pages, or pricing changes in the past week. This is unusual enough to be worth noting on its own - it's a clear window to publish without being drowned out.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Total publishing freeze.** Nine working competitors, zero new pages between them. Either this is end-of-term-and-pre-exam quiet period behaviour, or the crawl ran against cached sitemaps. Worth watching whether next week's diff also comes back empty - if it does, we should sanity-check the crawler.

2. **Three competitors have broken or empty sitemaps** (Begin Bright, Alchemy, Math Minds, and arguably Success Tutoring). These are smaller players. It means we have a blind spot on roughly a third of the watchlist - mostly the boutique/independent end.

3. **Marketplace supply is static.** Learnmate, our closest model-match, added no new tutor profile pages. If their tutor acquisition has genuinely flatlined (vs. a sitemap quirk), that's a recruitment opportunity for Tuterly.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's no new competitor content to react to, the useful move this week is offensive rather than defensive. Three angles worth considering:

- **Publish into the silence.** A quiet week is a good week to ship the kind of content competitors are slow on - mid-year VCE check-in guides, Unit 3/4 SAC prep, or scholarship test timing pieces tied to /scholarship-test-prep and /vce-tutoring. Nothing on the watchlist will be competing for attention.

- **Learnmate tutor poaching.** If their supply growth has stalled, it's worth reviewing their tutor pool for recruitment outreach to Tuterly's marketplace side (/tutors).

- **Standing gaps still unaddressed.** From prior weeks, competitors continue not to publish anything resembling Tuterly's /atar-planner, /worksheets, or /sample-report - these remain genuine differentiators rather than table stakes. No competitor moved on them this week.

## What to watch next week

- Whether the zero-change pattern repeats (and if so, validate the crawler against one or two competitor sites manually).
- Begin Bright fetch recovery.
- Whether Learnmate resumes adding tutor profile URLs.
- Success Tutoring's sitemap - 2 URLs is almost certainly wrong.
