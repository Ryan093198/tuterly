# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-09-04

## Headline

Cluey Learning has removed roughly 1,290 URLs from its public site in a single week - close to three-quarters of what it was previously exposing - while adding nothing new. The removed URLs are dominated by staging pages, duplicate feedback variants, old "2023 options" guides and ATAR quiz drafts. On the surface this looks like a large sitemap hygiene / CMS cleanup rather than a strategic content pivot, but the sheer scale is worth watching. If Cluey is consolidating around a smaller, more focused set of pages, we may see a repositioning or a relaunch shortly. Worth checking their homepage and nav manually over the next fortnight.

## Cross-competitor patterns

- **Very quiet publishing week.** Across eleven reachable competitors, only Learnmate published a single new page. This is a genuinely slow period - probably a good moment for Tuterly to ship content while competitors are silent.
- **Tutor-profile content as an SEO moat.** Learnmate is the only competitor consistently adding indexable pages, and they're doing it through tutor profiles. Our `/directory` should be structured so each tutor has a crawlable, richly described profile page - if it isn't already, this is a cheap parity move with compounding SEO benefit.
- **Site consolidation at Cluey.** The scale of Cluey's URL removals suggests either a CMS migration or a deliberate pruning. Either way, they may be temporarily weaker in long-tail search while things settle. Good moment to press on `/cluey-alternative` visibility.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

1. **English literary techniques content.** Cluey has dropped their page on this. It's a high-intent VCE/HSC topic and fits neatly under `/learn` or as a worksheet pack. Low effort, decent evergreen value.
2. **Primary-school literacy and numeracy positioning.** Learnmate's new tutor profile leans into primary school literacy/numeracy/general support. Our top-level paths are heavily VCE/ATAR/Melbourne-focused - we don't have a clear primary-school landing page. Worth considering a `/primary-tutoring` or similar to capture parent search intent that Learnmate is quietly building around.
3. **Tutor profile pages as SEO surface area.** If `/directory` doesn't already generate an indexable page per tutor with a proper title, description and subject metadata, that's the single highest-leverage change we could make to match Learnmate's footprint.
4. **Press the Cluey-alternative angle.** With Cluey visibly restructuring, revisit `/cluey-alternative` messaging and make sure it's current. Consider adding a short section on stability/continuity if any of their removed features (ATAR quiz, feedback pages) don't return.
