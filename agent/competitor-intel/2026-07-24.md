## Cross-competitor patterns

Nothing moved this week. That's the pattern. Late July is mid-Term 3 in Victoria and typically a low-publishing window for tutoring sites — most content pushes come in the pre-Term 4 / pre-exam runup (August–September) and again in January. Expect the next real activity from Cluey and Learnmate in about four to six weeks as VCE exam season approaches.

Two crawl hygiene notes:
- Begin Bright errored — needs investigation.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since there's no fire to fight, here's where our own path structure looks thin relative to what competitors typically cover:

1. **Year-level landing pages.** We have subject and location angles (`/vce-tutoring`, `/online-tutoring-melbourne`) but no year-specific pages. Cluey, Kip McGrath and North Shore all segment by year level (Year 7, Year 10, etc.) as a primary navigation axis. This is a durable SEO and parent-decision surface we're missing.

2. **Primary-school coverage.** Our current pages skew heavily to VCE / ATAR / senior secondary. Kip McGrath, Begin Bright and Math Minds all target primary parents directly. `/parents` exists but doesn't segment by stage. A dedicated primary tutoring page is a clean gap.

3. **NAPLAN.** No path on our site addresses it. It's a recurring content pillar for North Shore, Kip McGrath and Cluey. `/scholarship-test-prep` shows we're willing to play in test prep — NAPLAN prep is the obvious sibling.

4. **Subject-specific landers beyond maths/VCE.** English, science and specifically HSC/QCE variants would let us expand the `/…-tutoring` template we've already built for VCE. Apex and Learnmate both run wide subject taxonomies.

5. **More "alternative to" pages.** Our two comparison pages (`/cluey-alternative`, `/tutor-doctor-alternative`) target the biggest brands. Learnmate is the most structurally similar competitor to us and has no comparison page pointed at it. A `/learnmate-alternative` page focused on our curation vs their open marketplace would be worth testing.

6. **Worksheets angle.** `/worksheets` is a real differentiator — none of the competitors we track have a comparable free-resource library at scale. Under-promoted. Consider linking it more aggressively from `/parents` and subject pages.

Recommend picking one of items 1–3 as a Term 3 content build while competitors are quiet.
