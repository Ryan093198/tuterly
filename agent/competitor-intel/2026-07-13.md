## Standing opportunities for Tuterly

Since there are no new competitor moves to react to, here are structural gaps still visible from footprint size and known positioning (no new signal this week, just what the totals imply):

- **Marketplace scale is Learnmate's moat.** Their 6.4k URLs vs our directory suggests they're winning on tutor-profile SEO. Worth auditing whether /directory and /tutors are generating individual indexed pages per tutor and per subject/suburb combination at comparable depth.
- **Comparison pages remain a Tuterly-specific asset.** We have /cluey-alternative and /tutor-doctor-alternative live. There are no equivalents on any competitor site (Alchemy, Learnmate, Apex Tuition are all plausible additional targets). Consider whether /alchemy-alternative or /learnmate-alternative would be worth adding given their size.
- **Pricing transparency.** We have /tutoring-prices-melbourne. None of the tracked competitors appear to have equivalent public pricing pages of their own that surface in the crawls - this remains a differentiator worth keeping prominent.
- **ATAR planning tool.** /atar-planner has no visible analogue at any competitor. This is a defensible product-led SEO asset; worth continuing to invest in.
