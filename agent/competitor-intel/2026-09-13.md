## Opportunities for Tuterly

Since no one is shipping, this is a week to press on gaps we already know exist:

- **Holiday programs / Term 4 prep.** None of our tracked competitors published anything on this yet. Tuterly doesn't appear to have a dedicated holiday-program or exam-intensive landing page in the top-level paths listed. First mover on a "VCE exam intensive" or "Sept/Oct holiday tutoring Melbourne" page could own that search intent before the competitor wave.
- **Comparison pages.** We have `/cluey-alternative` and `/tutor-doctor-alternative`. Given Learnmate is the closest structural competitor (marketplace model, huge URL footprint) and they've been static, a `/learnmate-alternative` page is an obvious next add. Kip McGrath and Success Tutoring are also candidates given their franchise/centre positioning contrasts with ours.
- **Primary-school and early-years content.** Begin Bright and Kip McGrath both anchor there; our top-level paths skew secondary/VCE. A `/primary-tutoring-melbourne` or year-level pages (Year 3-6) would extend reach without competing head-on with our VCE strength.
- **Selective / scholarship depth.** We have `/scholarship-test-prep` - worth checking whether North Shore and Apex (both strong in this segment) have deeper sub-pages by school (Melbourne High, MacRob, Nossal, Suzanne Cory). If they do and we don't, that's a build target.
- **Worksheets as a moat.** `/worksheets` is a differentiator - none of the competitors surfaced here appear to have equivalent free resource libraries at scale. Worth investing while they're quiet.
