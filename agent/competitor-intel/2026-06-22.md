# Tuterly competitor intel - 2026-06-22

## Headline observation

**EzyMaths Tutoring has removed 198 pages this week** - a near-total site collapse, going from ~241 indexed URLs down to ~43. The removed pages include their homepage, contact page, FAQ, About, Mission, Our Tutors, For Schools (entire section), For Tutors (jobs/recruitment), Getting Started, and Past Papers. This is either a full site rebuild in progress, a migration, or the business is winding down its public web presence. Worth watching closely over the next 2-3 weeks - if EzyMaths is genuinely contracting, there's a maths-tutoring demand pocket (especially their schools and past-papers traffic) potentially up for grabs.

## Cross-competitor patterns

1. **Unusually quiet week.** Of the 8 competitors that returned data, 7 show zero net changes. Either the sector is in a mid-year holding pattern (consistent with school holidays late June) or publishing cadence is genuinely low across the board. Tuterly can publish into a quiet field this week with little competitive noise.

2. **The two scale players (Learnmate at 6.4k URLs, Alchemy at 2.7k URLs) keep dwarfing everyone else.** Both rely on programmatic page generation. Tuterly's directory is the right structural answer - the question is breadth of coverage, not strategy.

3. **No new pricing pages, no new alternative/comparison pages, no new VCE or ATAR content** appeared anywhere this week. Our `/atar-planner`, `/vce-tutoring`, and `/tutoring-prices-melbourne` pages are uncontested in terms of fresh competing content right now.

## Opportunities for Tuterly

- **Past Papers gap.** EzyMaths just removed `/past-papers/`. We have `/worksheets` but no dedicated past-papers hub. A VCE/HSC past-papers landing page would slot neatly next to `/worksheets` and `/atar-planner`, and pick up displaced EzyMaths traffic.
- **"For Schools" angle.** EzyMaths removed their entire For Schools section. Tuterly currently has `/centres` but nothing explicitly aimed at schools partnering with tutors. If EzyMaths is exiting that channel, there's a B2B lane worth a landing page.
- **Tutor recruitment content.** EzyMaths killed `/for-tutors/jobs/`. Our `/tutors` page handles tutor signup but a dedicated "tutor jobs Melbourne" page could capture the recruitment-intent searches EzyMaths is vacating.
- **Math-specific landing page.** With EzyMaths in apparent retreat and Math Minds tiny (66 URLs), a Tuterly `/maths-tutoring-melbourne` page (mirroring the structure of `/vce-tutoring`) would face minimal direct competition.
- **EzyMaths alternative page.** If their retreat continues, an `/ezymaths-alternative` page would fit the pattern we already run for Cluey and Tutor Doctor - and would catch any parents searching for them post-shutdown.

## What to watch next week

1. Does EzyMaths add new pages (rebuild) or stay stripped (wind-down)?
2. Do the four errored competitors come back online, and have they repositioned?
3. Any movement on Learnmate or Alchemy's programmatic page counts - those are the leading indicators of marketplace expansion.
