## The range, at a glance

Online tutoring in Victoria generally falls into three price bands:

- **National online agencies:** roughly $90 to $150 per hour. You pay a premium for a fully managed service where the company assigns and manages the tutor.
- **Platforms and marketplaces:** typically more affordable, because you choose your own tutor and pay them more directly. Tuterly sessions, for example, start from $75 per hour, all-inclusive.
- **Independent tutors:** often $30 to $60 per hour, especially high-achieving uni students. The lowest cost, but with no vetting, reviews or tooling included.

## What actually drives the price

**How the tutor is paid.** In an agency model, a large share of your hourly rate goes to the company, not the tutor. On a platform or with an independent, more of what you pay reaches the person actually teaching, which is why you often get a comparable or stronger tutor for less.

**What is bundled in.** Some rates are just the session. Others include session reports, practice materials, and progress tracking. "All-inclusive" matters, an $80 rate with reports and worksheets built in can be better value than a $70 rate where everything else is extra.

**Tutor experience and results.** A 99-ATAR VCE Specialist tutor commands more than a generalist, and reasonably so. Match the level of tutor to the job, primary maths does not need the same specialist as Year 12 Methods.

## Why online is often better value

Online removes travel for the tutor, which widens the pool and keeps rates competitive, and it removes travel for you, which is a real saving in time and fuel, especially outside metro Melbourne. You are also not limited to local supply, so you are not forced to pay a premium for the one specialist in town.

## Working out what you should pay

Start from the job, not the rate. For confidence-building and homework support at primary or junior-secondary level, the middle band is usually plenty. For VCE, selective-entry or scholarship prep, paying for a proven subject specialist is worth it, the stakes and the measurable payoff are higher.

Then compare like with like: ask each option for its hourly rate and exactly what is included.
