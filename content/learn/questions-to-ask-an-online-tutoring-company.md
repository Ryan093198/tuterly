## Why the questions matter

Most online tutoring companies look similar from the homepage. The differences show up fast once you ask a few direct questions, and the willingness to answer them is itself a signal. Here are eight worth asking before you commit to weekly sessions.

## The eight questions

**1. What did the tutor score in this exact subject?** Not their overall ATAR, the study score or result in the subject your child is taking. Specialisation is what moves marks.

**2. Do I choose the tutor, or are they assigned?** Neither is wrong, but you should know. Agencies typically assign; platforms let you choose and switch.

**3. Do I get a report after every session?** Ask to see an example. A clear write-up of what was covered and what to practise is the difference between tutoring you can see and a black box.

**4. How do you track progress over time?** For VCE and selective-entry prep, progress against a fixed topic list is measurable. A company that can show you the trend is worth more.

**5. What is the hourly rate, and what is included?** If you cannot get a rate without an enquiry form, that is a flag. Ask what the rate includes, reports, practice materials, and dashboards should not all be extras.

**6. Are your tutors vetted, and do they hold a current Working With Children Check?** This is the floor. A straight answer should come easily.

**7. Is there a lock-in contract or minimum term?** Good tutoring keeps you because it works, not because you are contracted. Know the cancellation terms before you start.

**8. What happens if it is not the right fit?** Can you change tutors easily? A confident company makes switching simple rather than painful.

## Reading the answers

You are listening for two things: substance, and how readily it comes. A company that answers all eight clearly and quickly is telling you it has nothing to hide. Hesitation, vague answers, or "fill in the form and we will discuss it" usually means the honest answer is not the one they want to lead with.
